## In short

**Gadādhara dāsa**, of the village of **Gaṇisiṁha** near Indrāṇī on the Bhāgīrathī, by Agradvīpa, brother of **Kāśīrāma dāsa** (the Bengali translator of the Mahābhārata), wrote about **1778**, at Mākhanpur in the Purī district, the **Puruṣottama-māhātmya** (later called *Jagat-maṅgala*), which opens with the praise of **Lord Gaurāṅga** and gathers Purāṇic evidence of His divinity.

## Life and book

He lived with his father, **Kamalākānta dāsa**, at Purī; his elder brother Kāśīrāma translated the Mahābhārata into Bengali, and Kṛṣṇadāsa was his other brother. In the *Puruṣottama-māhātmya* he sings the praises of Lord Gaurāṅga at the very beginning — which suggests he was His devotee — presents Purāṇic evidence of His divinity, and ends with the merits of devotion to Him.
