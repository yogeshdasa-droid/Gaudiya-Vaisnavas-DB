## In short

**Puruṣottama Paṇḍita**, a brāhmaṇa poet of **Orissa**, wrote the famous historical kāvya **Kāñcī-kāverī**, on the conquest of Kāñcī by King **Puruṣottamadeva Gajapati** (see #1131). Oṛiyā Vaiṣṇava texts count him among Lord Caitanya's associates: **Madanalatā gopī** in the *Guru-bhakti-gītā*, **Madanālasā sakhī** in the *Svarūpa-varṇana* and in an Oṛiyā *Caitanya-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, which also calls him **Mahābala**.

## The sources

S. Rath's *Caitanya-bandanā* names him as the author of the *Kāñcī-kāverī*. In the *Guru-bhakti-gītā* (Utkal University, 1975, vol. III, ch. 49, p. 176) he is Madanalatā gopī. Dāśarathi dāsa's *Braja-bihāra* (Oṛiyā manuscript, Utkal University Library, cat. 49) calls him a paṇḍita by title and a brāhmaṇa by caste. The *Svarūpa-varṇana* ascribed to Rūpa Gosvāmī (Utkal University Library, cat. 382) and a *Śrī Caitanya-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* ascribed to Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja (Orissa State Museum, cat. L470[B]) describe him as Madanālasā sakhī in his previous life, and the latter also as Mahābala.
