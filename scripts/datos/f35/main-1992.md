## In short

**Pāgala Haranātha** (born 1865), son of **Jayarāma Bandyopādhyāya** and **Bhagavatī Sundarī** of **Sonāmukhī** (Bankura), was a holy man known for supernatural powers. His whole life is told in the book **Pāgala Haranātha**, and Bhāgavatacandra Mitra, Akiñcana Nandī and Kṣīrodacandra Bhaṭṭācārya each wrote detailed accounts of it.

## Life

He lost his father at two. He passed the Entrance examination from the Kuciākola school in 1884-85 and, after the F.A., entered the B.A. class of the Metropolitan College in 1887; he fell ill with a severe cough, sat the B.A. three times without passing, looked for work for two years, and in **1893** took a post under the **King of Kashmir**. There **Lālā Aṭalabihārī Nandī** came in contact with him and, impressed by his supernatural powers, became his disciple, keeping in touch with him afterwards by letter.
