## In short

The Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna has three entries «**Govinda Ācārya**», perhaps one person, perhaps more: (1) of the sub-branch of **Gadādhara Paṇḍita**, a resident of Malladeśa — «I bow to Govinda Ācārya, full of the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa, who relishes the joy of Govinda» (Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta 50); (2) a learned **musician** named in the Vaiṣṇava-vandanā and the Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā, «who made the wonderful dhāmālī songs of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa», whose padas are mixed with those of the two poets named Govinda dāsa; (3) the author of the **Govinda-bhāgavata**.
