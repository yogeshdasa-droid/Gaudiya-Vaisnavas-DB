## In short

**Mukunda Sarasvatī**, a sannyāsī of another sect living in Vṛndāvana, once gave **Sanātana Gosvāmī** a red cloth, which Sanātana tied on his head when he went to see **Jagadānanda Paṇḍita**. Jagadānanda, taking it for Jagannātha's prasāda, was delighted — until he learned it was the gift of a sannyāsī of another sect, and in fury nearly struck Sanātana with an earthen pot (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 13.50).
