## In short

**Jayagopāla dāsa** was the guru of **Ghanaśyāma dāsa**, author of a *Kṛṣṇa-vilāsa*. He wrote the **Bhakti-bhāva-pradīpa**, based on Sanskrit Vaiṣṇava nibandhas, and in 1629 another Sanskrit work called **Bhakti-ratnākara** (not Narahari's).
