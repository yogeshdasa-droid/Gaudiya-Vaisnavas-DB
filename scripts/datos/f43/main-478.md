## In short

**Harinārāyaṇa**, ruler of the **Pañcakoṭa** estate, which stretched from the Pareśnātha hills to Burdwan, received dīkṣā from the son of **Trimalla Bhaṭṭa** of Raṅga-kṣetra, who later put him in the care of **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya**. A devotee of **Śrī Rāmacandra** (Bhakti-ratnākara 9.303, 454-455), he inspired **Govinda Kavirāja** to write the **Rāma-caritra-gīta**.
