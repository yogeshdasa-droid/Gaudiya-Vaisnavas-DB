## In short

**Hussain Shah** (Hussain Khān Saiyyad) first served **Subuddhi Rāya**, and later became king of Gauḍa; on his wife's advice he spoiled Subuddhi Rāya's standing as a Hindu (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 25.180-182). **Rūpa** and **Sanātana** were his ministers. He regarded Lord Caitanya as the Supreme Lord, and it was he who kept Sanātana in confinement (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 25.180, 222; 19.18-30).
