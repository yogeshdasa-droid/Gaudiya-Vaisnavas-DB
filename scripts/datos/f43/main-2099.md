## In short

**Jagannātha dāsa**, a padakartā and renowned musician (Vaiṣṇava-vandanā), has nine padas in the **Padakalpataru**, and eleven more in the unpublished **Pada-ratnāvalī**, among them songs of the Subala-milana and Naukā-vilāsa.
