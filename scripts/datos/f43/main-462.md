## In short

**Haricandana**, a man of Orissa in **Mahārāja Pratāparudra**'s service and a sevāit of Lord Jagannātha, quarrelled with **Śrīvāsa Ṭhākura** in front of Lord Jagannātha's chariot (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 13.91-93, 97). With Maṅgarāja Mahāpātra (#903) he was told by the king to bring a new boat for the Lord at Cuttack.

## Sources

The record's text; the record of Maṅgarāja Mahāpātra (#903). Three Haricandanas, disciples of Rasikānanda, have their own records.
