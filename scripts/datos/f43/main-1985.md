## In short

**Jagadānanda Ṭhākura** of **Maṅgaladihi** (Birbhum), fourth in descent from **Pānuā Gopāla**, wrote the **Śrī Śyāmacandrodaya** in Bengali tripadī verse, with kīrtana padas, and made the Ṭhākura family of Maṅgaladihi shine. The book tells in verse how **Dhruva Gosvāmī** (#215) brought Śrī Śyāmacāṅd to Maṅgaladihi.

## Sources
