## In short

**Nābhā-devī**, mother of **Śrī Advaita Ācārya** (Bhakti-ratnākara 12.1756-1758), was the wife of **Kuvera Paṇḍita** (#819) and the daughter of **Mahānanda Vipra** of Navagrāma, of the family of **Narasiṁha Nāḍiyāla** (Prema-vilāsa 24). Before Advaita she had six sons and a daughter; four of the sons died on pilgrimage, and in their grief the parents moved from Lāuḍ to Śāntipura, where Advaita was born.
