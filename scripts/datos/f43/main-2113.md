## In short

**Kṛṣṇadāsa**, the eldest son of **Hita Harivaṁśa** of Vṛndāvana (#492), served the Deity **Śrī Rādhāvallabhajī** in Vṛndāvana; his brother was Sūryadāsa. (Harivaṁśa had been a disciple of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī, who disowned him for defying his order.)
