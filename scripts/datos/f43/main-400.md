## In short

**Govardhana Bhaṭṭa**, a Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava in the line of **Gadādhara Bhaṭṭa**, wrote in the eighteenth century the **Madhu-keli-vallī** (223 ślokas, mostly on the Holī pastimes), the **Śrī Rūpa-Sanātana-stotra** (49 ślokas in śārdūla-vikrīḍita, a poem of high merit on the lives of Rūpa and Sanātana) and the **Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa-stava** (104 ślokas).
