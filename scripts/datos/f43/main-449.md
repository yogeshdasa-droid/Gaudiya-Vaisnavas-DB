## In short

**Hare Kṛṣṇa dāsa**, a Vaiṣṇava poet, was one of the translators of the **Rāsa-pañcādhyāya** into Bengali payāra verse; padas 60 and 1372 of the **Padakalpataru** are his. Amūlya Mukhopādhyāya, in the Ānanda Bazar Patrikā, mentions a collection of 63 of his padas; since they name Bhūgarbha Gosvāmī, Ṭhākura Gadādhara, Pūjārī Gosvāmī and others, he probably lived some three hundred years ago.
