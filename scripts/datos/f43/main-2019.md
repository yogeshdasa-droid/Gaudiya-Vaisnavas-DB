## In short

**Gopāla dāsa** was the guru of **Kṛṣṇadāsa** (#2110), the elder brother of Kāśīrāma dāsa, who gave him the name **Śrī Kṛṣṇa-kiṅkara**. His own name appears in the bhaṇitā of the **Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vilāsa** as its author, though it is said the book was really compiled by his disciple Kṛṣṇadāsa.
