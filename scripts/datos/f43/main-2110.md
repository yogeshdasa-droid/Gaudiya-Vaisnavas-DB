## In short

**Kṛṣṇadāsa**, elder brother of **Kāśīrāma dāsa** (the Bengali translator of the Mahābhārata) and of **Gadādhara dāsa** (#273), composed the **Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vilāsa** with the bhaṇitā «**Kṛṣṇa-kiṅkara**», the name given him by his guru, the Vaiṣṇava **Gopāla dāsa** (#2019), whose name appears in the book as its author.

## Sources

The record's text; the records of Gopāla dāsa (#2019) and Gadādhara dāsa (#273). It was in the record «Kṛṣṇa-dāsa Others» #1888 until 07-11-2026.
