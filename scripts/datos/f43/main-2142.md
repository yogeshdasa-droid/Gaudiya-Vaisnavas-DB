## In short

**Balarāma**, a devotee of Lord Caitanya in Orissa, was the second son of **Kānāi Khuṇṭiyā** (#663) (Vaiṣṇava-vandanā). Some say that Kānāi Khuṇṭiyā worshipped Śrī Baladeva and Lord Jagannātha as his sons (*Bhakti Movement in Orissa*, p. 79).
