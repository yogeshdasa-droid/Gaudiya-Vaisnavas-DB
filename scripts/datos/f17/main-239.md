## In short

**Durlabha dāsa Bābājī** did bhajana on the north bank of **Govinda-kuṇḍa** when **Manohara dāsa Paṇḍita Bābājī** came to live in a house by the kuṇḍa. During a plague in Vṛndāvana the messenger of death came for him, but could not take him while he chanted — and by the grace of the holy name his life was lengthened.

## The messenger of death

Vṛndāvana was in the grip of a plague. One hot midday, as he chanted japa under a margosa tree, a huge, terrifying grey figure appeared before him. Amazed, Durlabha dāsa asked why he had come. "I am the messenger of death," it said, "sent to take you." He answered calmly: "I am ready; you are free to take me." But the being replied that it could not, unless he stopped chanting — and when he refused, it vanished.

Later he told **Manohara dāsa** what had happened, to understand it. Manohara dāsa explained that he had in fact been destined to die at the very moment the figure appeared, but that by the grace of the holy name he had been given a longer life.
