## In short

**Yaśovanta dāsa**, one of the Oṛiyā **Pañcasakhās**, was born in **1487** at **Ārabaṅga** near Jagatsinghpur (Cuttack), son of **Jagu Mallik** and **Rekhā Devī** (Acyutānanda's *Udaya-kāhānī*). Initiated by **Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu** at Purī — who called him **Svarūpa Tīrtha** — he sang in the Lord's saṅkīrtana and wrote books on the bhakti He taught, above all the **Prema-bhakti-brahma-gītā**.

## Meeting the Lord

Some scholars think he was a kṣatriya or a Mahānāyaka. As a youth, guarding a cornfield from deer and wild boars, he saw the Lord appear before him: "Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu is My incarnation. Take initiation from Him and serve His feet; then preach His message and save the fallen." The Lord vanished, and Yaśovanta set out to find his master. At last he saw Lord Gaurāṅga, the son of Śacī, and fell at His feet; Mahāprabhu embraced him, called him **Svarūpa Tīrtha**, initiated him into the name of Hari and named him **Suvāhera** (Īśvara dāsa's *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, ed. A. B. Mohanty, ch. 46).

## With the saṅkīrtana

Īśvara dāsa says he was a constant singer in the saṅkīrtana party; at Cuttack he joined the saṅkīrtana at a mahotsava held by **King Pratāparudra**. Sudarśana dāsa's *Caurāsī Ājñā* says he was present, and took a major part, at the demonstration of the Pañcasakhās' powers before Śrī Caitanya, Pratāparudra and Sārvabhauma (Utkal University ms., ch. 43).

## His works

After his initiation he went home and lived in meditation, writing books that reveal Śrī Caitanya's teaching. Formerly a follower of jñāna-miśrā bhakti, in the **Prema-bhakti-brahma-gītā** he explains the path of prema-bhakti in great detail.
