## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Śrīrūpa Siddhāntī Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Siddhasvarūpa Brahmacārī** — was one of the foremost disciples of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, a fearless preacher and writer, co-founder with **Bhakti Viveka Bhāratī Mahārāja** of the **Śrī Sārasvata Gauḍīya Āśrama and Mission**.

## Life

- As Siddhasvarūpa Brahmacārī he once exposed the followers of Vivekānanda and Rāmakṛṣṇa in a public assembly and was pelted with stones; his guru was delighted, came to that town, called another assembly and had him refute Vivekānanda point by point — this time the people cheered him.
- He went out collecting with **[[Śrīla Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1798|Vinodabihārī Brahmacārī]]** (Keśava Mahārāja), whom he called his elder brother and śikṣā-guru; Vinoda would not let him carry the heavier bag of vegetables from Howrah station. In 1968 he led the funeral rites of Keśava Mahārāja.
- He honoured his godbrother **Bhakti Viveka Bhāratī Mahārāja** as śikṣā-guru; after their guru's departure they founded the Śrī Sārasvata Gauḍīya Āśrama and Mission (three branches). He gave sannyāsa to seven disciples.
- He translated Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa's *Govinda-bhāṣya* into Bengali (a wish of his guru), wrote commentaries on the twelve principal Upaniṣads, translated the Gītā commentaries of Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa and Viśvanātha Cakravartī, and published *Guru-preṣṭha* and other books.
- He built three temples full of sculpted theistic exhibitions; the best known is the **Gauḍīya Sārasvata Āśrama** on Satasan Road, north of Haridāsa Ṭhākura's samādhi, **Purī**.
- Appearance: **Kārtika Śukla Pañcamī**; departure: **Āśvina Kṛṣṇa Ṣaṣṭhī** (Gauḍīya calendar).
