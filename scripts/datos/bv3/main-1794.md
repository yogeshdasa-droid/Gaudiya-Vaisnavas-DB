## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Dayita Mādhava Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Hayagrīva dāsa Brahmacārī** — was the disciple whom **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]** praised for his «volcanic energy», and the founder of the **Śrī Caitanya Gauḍīya Maṭha**, with more than twenty maṭhas in India.

## Family

- Born on **Friday 18 November 1904**, Utthāna Ekādaśī, at 8 am, at **Kanchanpara** (Madaripur, Faridpur district, East Bengal), on the Padmā river near Premtali, where Narottama Ṭhākura received prema.
- Father: **Niśikānta Deva Śarmā Bandyopādhyāya** (died when he was four); mother: **Śaivalinī Devī**; grandfather: Caṇḍī Prasāda Bandyopādhyāya of Vikrampur. Birth name **Heramba Kumāra** (pet name Gaṇeśa); he grew up in the house of his maternal uncle Yaśodānandana.

## Life

- At eleven he knew the whole Bhagavad-gītā by heart, from hearing it from his mother. In Calcutta he wept for Kṛṣṇa at night; Nārada appeared in a dream and gave him a mantra he could not remember. He went to the Himalayas beyond Haridvāra, fasted three days and heard a voice: «Go back; your guru has appeared there.» A childless rich couple in Haridvāra wanted to adopt him; he refused.
- **1925**: he met Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī at Māyāpura; **1927**: harināma and dīkṣā at the Ultadanga Gauḍīya Maṭha, Calcutta, as Hayagrīva dāsa Brahmacārī, and joined the maṭha. His guru sent him ahead on every difficult task and had his passport ready to send him to the West, but a householder thought him too young and handsome, and Bhakti Pradīpa Tīrtha went instead. Many took him for his guru's son, so much did he resemble him.
- With **Nandasuno Brahmacārī** (later [[Śrīla Bhakti Hṛdaya Bon Mahārāja#ficha-1795|Bon Mahārāja]]) he silenced a Sanskrit scholar in Kāśī who mocked their guru for giving the thread to non-brāhmaṇas. He preached in Assam, Bengal and East Bengal; at Hauli in Assam he answered a maulavi with the example of the Urdu book: one must learn the script to read it, and special vision to see the soul.
- **Sannyāsa**: Gaura Pūrṇimā **1944**, at the Ṭoṭā-Gopīnātha temple, Purī, from his godbrother **Bhakti Gaurava Vaikhānasa Mahārāja**.
- He founded maṭhas at Tezpur, Guwahati and Goalpara (Assam), Hyderabad, Dehradun, Māyāpura, Calcutta, Chandigarh, New Delhi and others, managed the Sorbhog Gauḍīya Maṭha, registered the **Śrī Caitanya Gauḍīya Maṭha** (1953), and gave sannyāsa to 21 disciples (1965–77).
- He left this world on **27 February 1979**, and named his disciple **[[Bhakti Vallabha Tīrtha#ficha-1808|Bhakti Vallabha Tīrtha Mahārāja]]** his successor.
- Bhakti Pramoda Purī Mahārāja noted that his appearance falls on the departure day of Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī (Utthāna Ekādaśī) and his departure on that of Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī (Śukla Pratipat).
- Appearance: **Kārtika Śukla Ekādaśī** (Utthāna Ekādaśī); departure: **Phālguna Śukla Pratipat** (Gauḍīya calendar).

## Other versions

- Dīkṣā: «4 September 1927, on Rādhāṣṭamī» (Mahāvīra Mahārāja and the Gokul text) or «1 November 1927» (Bhakti Vallabha Tīrtha).
- One text says he started his own mission «on 4 October 1936», another that the Śrī Caitanya Gauḍīya Maṭha was founded in 1953; the first branch, at Tezpur, dates from 23 January 1950.
