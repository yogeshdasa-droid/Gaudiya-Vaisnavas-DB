## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Kumuda Santa Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Rādhāramaṇa dāsa**, titled *Rāga-kumuda* by his guru — joined **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]** as a boy of eleven, preached in Burma, India and Europe, and founded the **Śrī Gaurāṅga Maṭha** (Keshiary), the **Śrī Caitanya Āśramas** (Kharagpur, Behala-Calcutta, Purī) and the **Śrī Gaura Sārasvata Santa Gauḍīya Maṭha** (Chhekati).

## Family

- Born on the 2nd of Vaiśākha, **1921**, at **Narma** (Medinīpura, West Bengal), near the places of Śyāmānanda and Rasikānanda, in the house of **Śrī Vaikuṇṭhanātha Rāya**, astrologer, doctor and landlord; mother: **Śrīmatī Ratnamayī Devī**. The youngest of three sons, named **Rādhāramaṇa**.

## Life

- **[[Śrīla Bhakti Hṛdaya Bon Mahārāja#ficha-1795|Bon Mahārāja]]**, **[[Bhakti Pramoda Purī Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1799|Bhakti Pramoda Purī]]** and **[[Śrīla Bhakti Dayita Mādhava Gosvāmī#ficha-1794|Bhakti Dayita Mādhava]]** noticed the boy; with Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī's consent he came to the maṭha at **eleven**, studied at the New Indian School in Calcutta, received harināma, and later learned Sanskrit from Gauradāsa Paṇḍita at Māyāpura.
- His guru gave him saffron cloth on **20 March 1930** after the Māyāpura exhibition, sent him to preach in East Bengal with Bon Mahārāja after the Dacca exhibition (1933), put him in charge of the Prapanna Āśrama (Brahmanpara, Howrah), and sent him to preach in Hindi (Rae Bareli, Shikarpur, Karachi, Pune) and to **Burma** (8 March 1936), seeing him off himself. In Mandalay he wrote «Guru-dāsatva hoy mor paricaya», which his guru had printed in the *Nadia Prakash*; he gave him the title **«Rāga-kumuda»** on Gaura Pūrṇimā.
- After his guru's departure he went on pilgrimage with Bhakti Bhūdeva Śrautī Mahārāja and did bhajana in his village; his guru appeared in a dream and told him to take sannyāsa. **Sannyāsa**: **12 February 1942** at the Kṣīra-corā Gopīnātha temple, Remuṇā, from **[[Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1667|Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara Mahārāja]]**.
- For 32 years he preached all over India and led the Navadvīpa-dhāma parikramā with **[[Bhakti Vilāsa Tīrtha Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1811|Bhakti Vilāsa Tīrtha Mahārāja]]**; from **1975** he preached in Britain, Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Holland and Belgium. He installed Śrī Rādhā-Gopīnātha (Keshiary), Rādhā-Govinda (Kharagpur), Rādhā-Madana-mohana (Behala), Rādhā-Ramaṇa (Purī) and Rādhā-Mādhava (Chhekati). He honoured Bon Mahārāja as a śikṣā-guru.
- Books: *Saṁlāpe Śikṣā*, *Patre Upadeśa*, *Gītā-siddhānta-sāra*, *Santa-vāṇī*, *Śrī Gaurāṅga Mahāprabhur Jīvanī*, *Sādhana-kaṇa*, *Dāmodara-vrata-māhātmya*, *Bhakti-gīti*, *Dīkṣā*; Bengali translations of *Moha-mudgara* and *Moha-kuṭhāra*.
- The text was written for his 87th appearance day, while he was still living.
- Appearance: **Vaiśākha Kṛṣṇa Ṣaṣṭhī**; departure: **Pauṣa Śukla Trayodaśī** (Gauḍīya calendar).
