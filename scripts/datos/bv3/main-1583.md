## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Vaibhava Purī Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Nṛsiṁha Kavirāja**, «Nṛsiṁhānanda Prabhu» — was an Oriyā disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, an Āyurvedic doctor and Bhāgavata scholar, founder-ācārya of the **Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mission** (18 maṭhas, mostly in Andhra and Orissa), founding member of the WVA, and a close friend of **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda#ficha-125|A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī]]**.

## Family

- Born in **1913**, on the fifth day of the dark fortnight of Māgha, in his grandfather's house at **Fulta**, 5 km from Berhampur (Ganjam, Orissa); the family came from Tulasī near Ichhapur and lived at Kutharsingi.
- Father: **Śrī Dāmodara**; mother: **Śrīmatī Devī**; the second of four sons (no sisters); named **Nṛsiṁha**.

## Life

- Studied at Surangi and at **Khallikote College**, Berhampur (B.A.); a follower of Gandhi, he was president of the district freedom fighters' association.
- He learned Āyurveda from **Kavirāja Madhusūdana Śarmā**, a disciple of Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī, and by his encouragement received **initiation on 3 August 1936**, Balarāma's appearance day, at Māyāpura. He opened a charitable Āyurvedic hospital at Bhanjanagar and was known as Nṛsiṁha Kavirāja.
- After his guru's departure (1 January 1937) he left home for Māyāpura on **25 June 1937**; on the advice of the senior sannyāsīs he took dīkṣā from **[[Bhakti Prasāda Purī Gosvāmī#ficha-123|Ananta Vāsudeva]]**, but later left him.
- **1937–42**: head of the **Rāya Rāmānanda Gauḍīya Maṭha at Kovvur** (Andhra) and manager of the Madras maṭha; he rebuilt the Kovvur temple. **1942–48** at Māyāpura: he studied up to Bhāgavata-ācārya, led Bhakti Pradīpa Tīrtha Mahārāja's Bhāgavata class, met the siddha **Vaṁśīdāsa Bābājī**, served **[[Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī#ficha-1802|Śrīdhara Mahārāja]]** and installed Mahāprabhu's footprints at the Rādhā-Madana-mohana temple. **1947**: with Vaikhānasa Mahārāja he organised the seven-day celebration of his guru's tenth anniversary in the Berhampur Town Hall. **1948–66**: again head of Kovvur and Madras, until he left Kovvur over indiscipline.
- **Sannyāsa**: **7 February 1966** at Māyāpura from **Bhakti Svarūpa Giri Mahārāja** (whose only disciple he was), after consulting Vaikhānasa Mahārāja; Keśava Mahārāja protested: «He is ours.»
- **June 1966**: he registered the **Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Āśrama** at Kakinada; in **1983** the **Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mission** at Iṭoṭā, Purī, with the Śrī Caitanya Candra Āśrama as its centre. He also had a temple near Sevā-kuñja in Vṛndāvana and a maṭha in Māyāpura, hospitals and libraries, and from 28 July 1988 the **Śrī Bhakti Vijñāna Nityānanda Book Trust**, publishing in Telugu, Oriyā, Hindi and English, and the monthly *The Siddhānta*.
- With Bhaktivedānta Svāmī: in **1971** he invited him to open a temple at Rajahmundry; in **1973** Svāmī Mahārāja stayed some twenty days at his Visakhapatnam āśrama with fifteen disciples; he helped lay the foundation of ISKCON's Māyāpura temple.
- He preached in Oriyā, Telugu, Hindi, Bengali and English; toured Europe from 5 May 1997 and America (New York, August 2001).
- He left this world in Purī, where he was given samādhi.
- Appearance: **Māgha Kṛṣṇa Ṣaṣṭhī**; departure: **Phālguna Śukla Saptamī** (Gauḍīya calendar).

## Other versions

- One text gives the year of his sannyāsa and of his first registered board as «1996» (a misprint for 1966).
- His birth is given as Māgha Kṛṣṇa **Pañcamī** («the 5th day of the waning moon of Māgha»); the Gauḍīya calendar celebrates it on Māgha Kṛṣṇa Ṣaṣṭhī.
