## In short

Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Trivikrama Gosvāmī Mahārāja was a disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1798|Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja]]** and one of the three «pillars» of the **Śrī Gauḍīya Vedānta Samiti**, with **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Vāmana Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1810|Vāmana]]** and **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Nārāyaṇa Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1809|Nārāyaṇa]]** Mahārāja, who honoured him as a śikṣā-guru.

## Family

- Born in **Burdwan district** (West Bengal), near Ambikā-Kālnā, close to the birthplace of Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita.

## Life

- Keśava Mahārāja used to preach in his village with **Narottamānanda Brahmacārī** (later Bhakti Kamala Madhusūdana Mahārāja); by Narottamānanda's preaching he joined the maṭha — as Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja did later.
- His guru put him in charge of organising all the Samiti's preaching and sending preachers to the districts of Bengal; he preached and collected especially in Burdwan. Once his relatives came to take him home by force from a programme in his village, and his host drove them away with a stick and a knife.
- He taught the young Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja kīrtana, preaching and collecting, rebuked him lovingly and protected him from his guru's displeasure; the two argued philosophy and slept in the same room. When Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja preached in the West, Trivikrama Mahārāja answered every letter at once.
- Externally «harder than a thunderbolt», internally «softer than a flower»: he told everyone their faults face to face, argued even with his own guru, and in old age still sewed his own shirts and cooked for guests. At over 75 he did the month-long Govardhana parikramā barefoot.
- By 1964 he was managing the Samiti's parikramās and preaching; he lived at Chinsurah and Navadvīpa.
- He fell ill in Calcutta before joining the 2002 Vraja-maṇḍala parikramā and left this world on **7 November 2002**, at 12.25 pm, on tṛtīyā in the month of Kārtika, after Annakūṭa, Dīpāvalī and Bhrātṛ-dvitīyā, in Navadvīpa-dhāma (Māyāpura); he was placed in samādhi the same day.
- Appearance: **Māgha Kṛṣṇa Dvādaśī**; departure: **Kārtika Śukla Tṛtīyā** (Gauḍīya calendar).
