## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Vinodabihārī Brahmacārī**, called «Vinoda» by his guru — was an intimate disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, the founder of the **Śrī Gauḍīya Vedānta Samiti** (1940), and the sannyāsa guru of **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda#ficha-125|A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī]]** and of the three «pillars» of his Samiti: **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Vāmana Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1810|Vāmana]]**, **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Trivikrama Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1552|Trivikrama]]** and **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Nārāyaṇa Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1809|Nārāyaṇa]]** Mahārāja.

## Family

- Born on **24 January 1898**, Māgha Kṛṣṇa Tṛtīyā, in a wealthy Vaiṣṇava landholding family of **Banaripara** (Barisal district, East Bengal).
- Father: **Śaratcandra Guhaṭhākurtā**, a devotee who read the Gītā, Bhāgavatam and Caitanya-caritāmṛta daily; mother: **Bhuvanamohinī Devī**, learned and strict, who managed the land.
- He was the youngest of four brothers; one of them, a headmaster, became **[[Bhakti Kevala Auḍulomi Mahārāja#ficha-127|Bhakti Kevala Auḍulomi Mahārāja]]**, ācārya of the Gauḍīya Mission.
- Names: the astrologers called him Śailendranātha; his father, Janārdana; the ladies, «Jonā» (moonbeam) for his golden face. Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja gives his name as **Bhīma**.

## Life

- As a child he did not speak for months, until his mother fed him rice begged from a family of the cremation grounds, as a Muslim mendicant had advised. He grew up hearing the Gītā, Bhāgavatam and Vedānta, started a magazine at school and was a great speaker and footballer; at **16** he managed his father's tenants.
- At 16 he first came to the Caitanya Maṭha in Māyāpura and received harināma from Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī; he studied at **Daulatpur College**, where he refuted his teacher's impersonal explanation of the Caitanya-caritāmṛta, and left. For a time he joined the independence movement and Subhas Candra Bose's revolutionaries; years later his guru persuaded the British to withdraw the warrant against him: «He has become a sādhu.»
- At 18 he came back to the maṭha with his aunt and never returned home. When she called his guru's plan to preach all over the world a dream, Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī said: **«Vinoda will see to it.»**
- **Dīkṣā**: Gaura Pūrṇimā **1919**, at the Yogapīṭha, Māyāpura; he asked for and received the guru-mantra, the first to get it. His guru spoke to him with the intimate «tui», as to very few, and called him a **«Vedāntic paṇḍita»**; he collected one paisa from each person at stations all day, slept with the money box as a pillow, and recovered the rents of the maṭha's land.
- He would not go home even when his mother was dying, for fear of being caught again in family duties.
- With the brahmacārī Gauragovinda (later Bhakti Gabhastinemi Mahārāja) he visited **[[Srila Gaura Kiśora Dāsa Bābājī#ficha-1819|Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī]]**, who had locked himself in; Bābājī Mahārāja opened the door for «the dear ones of Sarasvatī Ṭhākura» and blessed him: «I have taken all the dangers and obstacles of your life.»
- After 1937, during the troubles in the Gauḍīya Maṭha, he lived for months in Allahabad in the house of **Abhaya Caraṇa** (Bhaktivedānta Svāmī), and with him and Nṛsiṁhānanda Brahmacārī founded the **Śrī Gauḍīya Vedānta Samiti** in Calcutta on Akṣaya Tṛtīyā, **April 1940**; that year he also founded the Śyāmānanda Gauḍīya Maṭha in Medinīpura. Then known as **Kṛtiratna Prabhu**.
- **Sannyāsa**: in a dream his guru gave him sannyāsa and the name *Keśava*; he then took it at **Katwa** on **Bhādra Pūrṇimā** (Viśvarūpa-mahotsava) from **[[Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī#ficha-1802|Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Mahārāja]]**, with Bhakti Bhūdeva Śrautī Mahārāja as priest.
- He founded the **Devānanda Gauḍīya Maṭha** (Navadvīpa), the **Keśavajī Gauḍīya Maṭha** (Mathurā), and maṭhas at Chinsurah and elsewhere, and edited the *Gauḍīya Patrikā*, dictating his articles without notes. On **17 September 1959**, at the Keśavajī Maṭha in Mathurā, he gave sannyāsa to A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī.
- He left this world on **6 October 1968** (19 Āśvina 1375 Bengali), Śāradīya Rāsa Pūrṇimā, at sandhyā-ārati at the Devānanda Gauḍīya Maṭha, holding his guru's picture; Śrīmatī Rādhikā's garland broke and fell at that moment. Samādhi at the Devānanda Gauḍīya Maṭha, rites led by Bhakti Śrīrūpa Siddhāntī Mahārāja.
- Appearance: **Māgha Kṛṣṇa Tṛtīyā** (= Phālguna Kṛṣṇa Tṛtīyā in the Gauḍīya calendar); departure: **Āśvina Pūrṇimā**; sannyāsa day: **Bhādra Pūrṇimā** (Gauḍīya calendar).
