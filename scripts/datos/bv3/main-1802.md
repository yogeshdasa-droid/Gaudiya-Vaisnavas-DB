## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī Mahārāja, disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, was the founder of the **Śrī Caitanya Sārasvata Maṭha** in Navadvīpa, a poet and philosopher in Sanskrit and Bengali, and the godbrother whom many of the others honoured as the senior guardian of the line after their guru's departure.

## Family and early life

- Born in **1895** in a respected brāhmaṇa family of the Bhaṭṭācārya order at **Hapaniya**, Burdwan district (West Bengal); studied at Baharampur.
- From childhood he felt drawn to the mission of Śrī Caitanya's saṅkīrtana.

## Life

- **1926**: joined the Gauḍīya Maṭha and took initiation from Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī; **1930**: sannyāsa. His guru named him *Bhakti Rakṣaka* («guardian of devotion») and *Śrīdhara*, echoing Jīva Gosvāmī, who called Śrīdhara Svāmī *bhakti-eka-rakṣaka*.
- He preached all over India in Bengali, Hindi, Sanskrit and English. His guru praised his Sanskrit poem *Śrī Bhaktivinoda-viraha-daśakam*, and just before his departure (1937) asked him to sing *Śrī Rūpa-mañjarī-pada* in his presence.
- After 1937 his godbrothers revered him as the senior representative of the line. **A.C. Bhaktivedānta** (then a householder) gave him a large part of his house in Calcutta for his preaching; they remained intimate friends all their lives.
- He gave sannyāsa to **[[Śrīla Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1798|Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava]]** at Katwa on Bhādra Pūrṇimā, and was the first to give sannyāsa to **[[Bhakti Sāraṅga Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1377|Bhakti Sāraṅga Gosvāmī]]**.
- Inclined to stay unknown, he founded the **Śrī Caitanya Sārasvata Maṭha** at Navadvīpa. In his eighties people from all over the world came to hear him; his taped talks became the English books *Search for Śrī Kṛṣṇa – Reality the Beautiful*, *Śrī Guru and His Grace*, *The Golden Volcano of Divine Love*, *Loving Search for the Lost Servant* and *Bhagavad-gītā – The Hidden Treasure of the Sweet Absolute*. In Sanskrit he wrote the *Prapanna-jīvanāmṛta*.
- He named **[[Sundar Govinda Dev Goswami Mahārāj#ficha-1494|Bhakti Sundara Govinda]]** his successor as ācārya of the maṭha.
- He left this world on **12 August 1988**; his samādhi temple is at the Śrī Caitanya Sārasvata Maṭha, Navadvīpa.
- Appearance: **Kārtika Kṛṣṇa Navamī**; departure: **Śrāvaṇa Amāvasyā** (Gauḍīya calendar).
