## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Prapanna Dāmodara Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Cidānanda dāsa Brahmacārī** — was given to **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]** as a child of six and lived with him from then on; one of his last living disciples, he spent his final twenty years in Purī at the **Śrī Śrī Jagannātha Caitanya Saṅgha**.

## Family

- Born in **1916** at **Maheshpur** (Jessore district, now Bangladesh), the village of Nityānanda's associate Sundarānanda Ṭhākura. His parents, both of brāhmaṇa families, had lost several children; his mother prayed for a saint who would not fall under māyā. A light filled the room at his birth, and in a vision a goddess told her to keep him pure. He was called **Ek-khodhi**, «an offering». His mother died when he was two; he was raised by his uncle, a brahmacārī who served the family Deities Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Vallabha.

## Life

- In **1921**, at six, his father gave him to Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī at the Gauḍīya Maṭha on Ultadanga Junction Road, Calcutta; the boy would not leave his side again.
- **1932**, aged sixteen: dīkṣā as **Śrīman Cidānanda dāsa Brahmacārī**, then the sacred thread. His guru taught him astrology himself and had him take a B.A.
- After his guru's departure he preached at Jhargram with his godbrother Paramahaṁsa Mahārāja.
- **Sannyāsa**: **May 1947**, in Vṛndāvana, from **[[Bhakti Sāraṅga Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1377|Bhakti Sāraṅga Gosvāmī Mahārāja]]**.
- At forty-five he served at the **Puruṣottama Maṭha**, Purī (under Bhakti Sambandha Bhāgavata Mahārāja), and preached in Calcutta with **Bhakti Vijñāna Āśrama Mahārāja**, who left him his small maṭha in **Māyāpura**, near Mahāprabhu's birthplace.
- In the 1970s–80s he helped ISKCON, which built its headquarters next to his āśrama, to acquire land; after Bhaktivedānta Svāmī's departure he gave his land to ISKCON for the Bhaktivedānta Book Trust building and received a place in Purī, where in the early 1980s he founded the **Śrī Śrī Jagannātha Caitanya Saṅgha** (Gaur Bhat Sahi Road, between Ṭoṭā-Gopīnātha and Haridāsa Ṭhākura's samādhi). At ISKCON's request he published the evidence for Mahāprabhu's true birthplace, as Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura had established it.
- After heart attacks in **1989** he lived secluded in Purī, worshipping Śrī Śrī Gaura-Gadādhara, Mahābhāva-Rasarāja and Jagannātha, Baladeva, Subhadrā and Sudarśana.
- He left this world in Purī on **Guru Pūrṇimā, Monday 14 July 2003**, at brāhma-muhūrta, a month before turning eighty-eight; as is the custom in Purī, his body was cremated by the sea and the ashes scattered in the ocean.

## Other versions

- The text gives his birth year as 1916 and his age at death as «a month short of eighty-eight» (which would mean 1915).
