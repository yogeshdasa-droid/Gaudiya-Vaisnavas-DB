## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara Gosvāmī Mahārāja was a sannyāsī disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]** whose mission has seven temples. He was the first to proclaim that **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda#ficha-125|A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī]]** was a *śaktyāveśa-avatāra*, an empowered incarnation; later **[[Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī#ficha-1802|Śrīdhara Mahārāja]]** said the same to his godbrothers.

## Life

- On **12 February 1942** he gave sannyāsa to **[[Bhakti Kumuda Santa Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-810|Bhakti Kumuda Santa Mahārāja]]** at the Kṣīra-corā Gopīnātha temple, Remuṇā.
- In **1947** he was one of the sannyāsīs at the celebration of his guru's tenth anniversary in Berhampur. He had a good voice and was a fine kīrtana singer; once, coming from Śrīdhara Mahārāja's āśrama, he met **[[Bhakti Vaibhava Purī Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1583|Bhakti Vaibhava Purī]]** at Howrah station and asked him to take charge of the maṭha (from the life of Bhakti Vaibhava Purī).
- Appearance: **Vaiśākha Śukla Dvitīyā**; departure: **Kārtika Kṛṣṇa Ṣaṣṭhī** (Gauḍīya calendar).
