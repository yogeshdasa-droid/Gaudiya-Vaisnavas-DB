## In short

**Nityānanda dāsa «Sādhu»** — born **Puliśabihārī Mallika** at Kaluṭolā, Calcutta — was won over with his whole family when **Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa Deva** and his saṅkīrtana party came to his house unannounced in 1896. By his guru's order he founded the **Śrī Rādhāramaṇa Sevāśrama** and **Mātṛ-mandira** at Navadvīpa (1911). He died in **1913**, chanting Harināma, after nursing the victims of a cholera epidemic.

## Service

Seeing his great enthusiasm for service, people called him «Sādhu». He was a friend to the downtrodden and the lonely, and his love and humility relieved many hearts. In 1913 a severe cholera epidemic broke out at Navadvīpa during the Dhūlaṭ festival; Nityānanda helped the afflicted without rest, caught the disease himself and died performing Harināma.
