## In short

An unnamed **siddha bābā** did bhajana at **Paurṇamāsī-kuṇḍa** near **Nandagrāma** in the days of Siddha **Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa Bābājī** of Kāmyavana and Siddha **Vaiṣṇava dāsa Bābā** of Kosī (#1594). He worshipped a **Govardhana-śilā** that he always kept hanging from his neck. Told by **Advaita dāsa Bābājī**.

## The śilā

He lived under a tree, though his kuṭīra was close by, and worshipped Śrī Govardhana-śilā, which he kept hanging round his neck in a piece of cloth and never took off — not even for rituals, for nature's call or to bathe. He would sit for long periods in ecstatic trance. When he came back to outward consciousness his followers cooked, offered bhoga to Śrī Govardhana and took prasāda; no one knew where the ingredients came from, but there was always enough for any number of visitors.

## Sources

The record's text (told by the singer and scholar Advaita dāsa Bābājī). It was in the record of another bābā of Nandagrāma (#965) until 11-11-2026.
