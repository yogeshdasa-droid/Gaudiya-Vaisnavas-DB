## In short

**Halāyudha**, ninth in descent from Yajurvedī **Dakṣa Maharṣi** of the Kāśyapa gotra — one of the five Gauḍa brāhmaṇas brought from Kānyakubja by Mahārāja **Ādiśūra** —, was a minister at the court of **Rājā Lakṣmaṇa Sena** and wrote many books of memoirs. He was fourth in the line of Jagannātha dāsa Vaiṣṇavācārya Gosvāmī of the śrīpāṭa of Kāṣṭhakāṭā (Dhaka).

## Atonement

It is said that in his youth he committed the sin of trying to seduce his stepmother while his father was away. To atone, he resolved to burn himself in a fire of husks (tuṣānala), but was saved by the intervention of **Śrī Dāmodara Śālagrāma**.
