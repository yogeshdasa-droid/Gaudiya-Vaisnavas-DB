## In short

**Kumudānanda Paṇḍita** («Gāndharva Kumudānanda»), called **Daśama Gopāla** in some books and **Upagopāla** in others — Gāndharva gopa in Kṛṣṇa-līlā (Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā 136) —, came from **Chittagong** and had his śrīpāṭa at **Dāiṅhāṭ** (Burdwan), where he installed the Deity **Rasikarāja**.

## Dāiṅhāṭ

The śrīpāṭa no longer exists; the pāṭabāḍī is now inside a local householder's compound, and Rasikarāja is served by Gosvāmīs descended from the family of Rāmacaraṇa Cakravartī of Dāiṅhāṭ.
