## In short

**Śrīvāsa's mother-in-law**, the mother of **Mālinī-devī**, once hid in a basket in Śrīvāsa's house to watch Mahāprabhu's kīrtana; the Lord felt something amiss, Śrīvāsa found her and pulled her out by the hair. Later, on another visit, Mahāprabhu showed her His favour (Caitanya-bhāgavata Madhya 16.5-20; Bhakti-ratnākara 12.2745-2749, 12.1934).

## The basket

One day, when Mahāprabhu was absorbed in kīrtana in Śrīvāsa's house, she hid in a basket in the room, hoping to see and hear it. Suddenly the Lord felt uneasy and asked Śrīvāsa whether some outsider was there. After a long search Śrīvāsa found his mother-in-law hiding and angrily dragged her out by the hair.
