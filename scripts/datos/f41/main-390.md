## In short

**Gopīramaṇa Cakravartī** is named in three sources: as a disciple of **Hṛdayānanda**, present at the installation of **Rādhāvinoda** in the house of Govinda (Bhāvaka) Cakravartī (Bhakti-ratnākara 14.97); as a disciple of **Govardhana dāsa Dāmodara** with his śrīpāṭa at **Budhurī** (Rasikamaṅgala); and as a disciple of **Narottama Ṭhākura** who looked after the visiting Vaiṣṇavas at the **Kheturī** festival and was at the festival of Narottama's disappearance (Narottama-vilāsa 6, 12; Prema-vilāsa 20).

## Sources

The record's texts (three entries). They may speak of two or three persons of the same name; nothing in them says whether they are one.
