## In short

**Jaganmohinī** («Tukkā»), daughter of **Mahārāja Pratāparudra**, was given in marriage to **Kṛṣṇadeva Rāya** of Vijayanagara (#775) with the lands he had seized as dowry. Ill-treated by him, she left her husband's house and lived quietly at a place called **Kambam**. She composed five Sanskrit ślokas, the **Tukkā-pañcakam**.
