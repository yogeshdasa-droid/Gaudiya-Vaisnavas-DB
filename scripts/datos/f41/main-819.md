## In short

**Kuvera Paṇḍita**, father of **Śrī Advaita Ācārya**, a brāhmaṇa of the Bharadvāja line, was minister to **King Divyasiṁha** of **Lāuḍ** (Śrīhaṭṭa) and wrote the **Dattaka-candrikā**. He married **Nābhā-devī**, daughter of Mahānanda Vipra of Navagrāma. Of their six sons, four died on pilgrimage; grieving, the parents moved from Lāuḍ to **Śāntipura**, and there Advaita Prabhu was born (Prema-vilāsa 24). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Kuvera**, king of the Guhyakas (Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā 81-85).

## His family

Nābhā-devī had six sons and one daughter. When grown up, all six sons went on pilgrimage and four of them died on the way; the two who came back married at their father's wish.
