## In short

**Vāsudeva Śiyāla**, a brāhmaṇa of Rāḍha, was at first a Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava but was driven out of the community for misconduct (Prema-vilāsa 24). He was one of the impostors who, when the worship of Lord Caitanya spread, posed as incarnations of Kṛṣṇa, Rāma and others to draw the same reverence — with Viṣṇudāsa Kapīndra, Mādhava Cūḍādhārī and others.

## The impostors

Out of contempt people gave them names like *Śiyāla* («jackal») or *Kapīndra* («monkey-king»). The Gaura-gaṇa-candrikā, the Prema-vilāsa and the Caitanya-bhāgavata tell about these hypocrites.
