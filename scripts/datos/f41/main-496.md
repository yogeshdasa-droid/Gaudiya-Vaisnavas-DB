## In short

**Hemādri**, minister in the Yādava kingdom of **Devagiri** (Mahārāṣṭra) between 1260 and 1309, took shelter of **Vopadeva**, whose *Kaivalya-dīpikā* (a ṭīkā on the *Muktāphala*) became associated with Hemādri's name. He compiled the vast **Caturvarga-cintāmaṇi** on Smṛti, much used in South India.

## His works

*Caturvarga-cintāmaṇi*; *Āyurveda-rasāyana* (a ṭīkā on Vāgbhaṭa's *Aṣṭāṅga-hṛdaya*); *Cintāmaṇi*, *Kāmadhenu* and *Kalpadruma*; his *Rāja-praśasti* has much historical information on the Yādava royal family of Devagiri.
