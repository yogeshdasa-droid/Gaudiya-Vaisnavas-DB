## In short

**Balarāma Ācārya** of **Cāndapura** was the priest of **Hiraṇya** and **Govardhana Majumdār** of Saptagrāma, the family of Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 3.165-166). **Haridāsa Ṭhākura** stayed some days in his house, where the young **Raghunātha dāsa**, studying there, visited him daily; and it was Balarāma who brought Haridāsa to the Majumdārs' court, where Gopāla Cakravartī insulted him.

## At the Majumdārs' court

Balarāma brought Haridāsa to Govardhana's house, where they sang the glories of the holy name. Govardhana's employee **Gopāla Cakravartī** argued with Haridāsa Ṭhākura, and was struck with leprosy (see #1935).
