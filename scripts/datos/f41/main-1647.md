## In short

**Basanta Rāya**, a kāyastha, son of **Guṇānanda Guhā** (#426) and uncle of Mahārāja **Pratāpāditya** of Bengal, founded the kingdom of **Jessore** with his cousin **Vikramāditya**. About 1570, at his initiative and with his money, his father Guṇānanda built in Vṛndāvana the new temple of **Madana-mohana**, south of the old one.

## Madana-mohana

During Suleiman Karrani's rule in Bengal (1563-1572) Guṇānanda settled in Vṛndāvana for the rest of his life; there, with Basanta Rāya's help, he built the temple where Madana-mohana was served before the temple built by Kṛṣṇadāsa crumbled (see #426).
