## In short

**Rāmanārāyaṇa Miśra** («Candrabhāgā») was a disciple of **Harinātha**, son of Dāmodara dāsa, the younger brother of **Gopīnātha Pūjārī**, priest of Śrī Rādhāramaṇa and follower of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī. He wrote the elaborate **Bhāvabhāva-vibhāvikā** on the Rāsa-pañcādhyāya of the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam.

## His works

The *Bhāvabhāva-vibhāvikā*; a detailed commentary called *Prabhu* on the *Śrī Gaurāṅga-candrodaya* said to be part of the Vāyu Purāṇa; and a *Sūkṣmatama-vṛtti* on the Brahma-sūtra, which in places seems out of harmony with Lord Caitanya's tradition.
