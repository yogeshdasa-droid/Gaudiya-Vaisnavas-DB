## In short

**Ānandī** wrote a commentary on **Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī**'s *Śrī Caitanya-candrāmṛta* (1645 Śaka, 1723) and, in 1640 Śaka (1718), a grammar called **Śīghrabodha**, whose examples are mostly about Lord Caitanya — a staunch devotee of Gaurāṅga.

## His works

His commentary shows that Prabodhānanda's teaching was widely studied in his time. Ṭīkā 31 of the *Caitanya-candrāmṛta* contains a compilation on the **Gaura-mantra**, and each śloka of the book is followed by an explanatory śloka of his own in harmony with it.
