## In short

**Kṛṣṇakamala Gosvāmī** (born 1811) of **Bhajanghāṭ** (Nadia), of a family that counted among its luminaries four associates of Lord Caitanya — **Kaṁsāri Sena, Sadāśiva Kavirāja, Puruṣottama** and **Kānu Ṭhākura** —, wrote eight dramas for the folk theatre (yātrā) on the pastimes of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa and Gaurāṅga, which moved thousands of listeners night after night.

## His dramas

*Svapna-vilāsa*, *Nandaharaṇa*, *Divyonmāda* (*Rāy-unmādinī*), *Vicitra-vilāsa*, *Bhārata-milana*, *Gāndharvā-milana*, *Kālīya-damana* and *Nimāi-sannyāsa*. His blend of learning, poetry and music made him famous.
