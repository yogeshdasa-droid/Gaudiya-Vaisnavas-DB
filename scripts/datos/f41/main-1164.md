## In short

**Rādhānanda Deva** (1615–1684), eldest son of **Rasikānanda Prabhu** (Rasikamaṅgala Paścima 14.27, 31-33), became at fourteen the head of **Śyāmānanda's gaddī** at Gopīvallabhapura. A learned scholar and musician, he won lasting fame with his **Śrī Rādhā-Govinda Kāvya**, written on the lines of Jayadeva's Gīta-govinda, and composed some padas.

## Life

His childhood sports are vividly described in the *Śyāmānanda-rasārṇava*. He died in 1606 Śaka (1684), leaving two sons, **Nayanānanda** (#2006) and **Rasānanda**. (Sūryānanda of Galtā, #1505, is said to have been reborn as his eldest son.)
