## In short

**Kṛṣṇadayāla Candra**, of the Suvarṇa-vaṇik caste of **Pāñcthupi** (Murshidabad), born early in the seventeenth Śaka century, was a devout Vaiṣṇava and an accomplished **Manoharaśāhī** kīrtana singer; the famous kīrtanīyā **Advaita dāsa Bābājī** of Vṛndāvana (#17) was his student. He is surely the Kṛṣṇadayāla of Pāñcthupi whose company woke renunciation in **Banwārīlāl Siṁha** (#93).

## Training

A local teacher, Kṛṣṇahari Hājrā, taught him singing. As a boy he studied Sanskrit grammar and the Bhāgavata-śāstra under **Rāmakṛṣṇa Vidyābhūṣaṇa** of Muniādihi, master of alaṅkāra and the Bhāgavatam, and he was close to the famous paṇḍita **Rāmasundara Tarkavāgīśa** of Ḍunobāhārā (Birbhum).
