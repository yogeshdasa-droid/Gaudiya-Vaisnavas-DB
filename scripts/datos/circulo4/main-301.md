## In short

Gaṅgānārāyaṇa Cakravartī, called **Ṭhākura Cakravartī**, was a vārendra brāhmaṇa scholar of **Gambhilā** (now Gāmlā, Bāluchar, Murshidabad), on the bank of the Ganges, who became a disciple of **Narottama Ṭhākura** — a brāhmaṇa taking dīkṣā from a kāyastha. "Another branch, Gaṅgānārāyaṇa Cakravartī, lives at Gambhilā on the bank of the Ganges; everyone calls him Ṭhākura Cakravartī" (*Prema-vilāsa* 20).

## Life

He was a great scholar, much respected, who fed and taught **five hundred students** every day. In his pride of learning he at first despised Narottama, but through the company of Narottama's disciple **Harirāma Ācārya** he came to understand his greatness, wept over his own pride, and received his mercy (*Narottama-vilāsa* 10). When he asked for dīkṣā, Narottama said: "You are a brāhmaṇa; what will the brāhmaṇas of the land say?" — "Lord, does one whom you favour fear brāhmaṇas without devotion?" By Narottama's grace he studied the Gosvāmīs' books and swam in the ocean of saṅkīrtana.

For taking dīkṣā from a kāyastha he was persecuted and abused by many brāhmaṇas; in time they all fell at his feet and took dīkṣā from him. With **Rāmacandra Kavirāja** he defeated the smārta paṇḍitas of Rājā Narasiṁha at the market of Kumārapura, selling pān and answering them in Sanskrit; when Narottama entered the Gaṅgā at Gambhilā at the end of his life, it was he and Rāmakṛṣṇa Ācārya who led the two kīrtanas and poured water over him. The Deities of **Gaurāṅga and Viṣṇupriyā** that came out of Vipradāsa's granary are served at Gambhilā.

## Family and disciples

His wife **Nārāyaṇī Devī** and daughter **Viṣṇupriyā** took dīkṣā from him. Having no son, he adopted **Kṛṣṇacaraṇa**, the younger son of his godbrother **Rāmakṛṣṇa Ācārya**, and initiated him; Kṛṣṇacaraṇa became very dear to the devotees of Vṛndāvana for his bhajana. The famous **Viśvanātha Cakravartī** was his student (and the śikṣā disciple of Kṛṣṇacaraṇa).

## Other persons of the same name
