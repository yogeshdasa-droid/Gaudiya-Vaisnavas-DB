## In short

Hṛdaya Caitanya (first called **Hṛdayānanda**) was the dearest disciple of **Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita** of Ambikā-Kālnā, who gave him the service of the Deities of Gaura-Nityānanda there, and the dīkṣā guru of **Śyāmānanda Prabhu**. He was the son of **Vāṇīnātha** and a nephew of **Gadādhara**; with Gadādhara's permission Gaurīdāsa took charge of him (*Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* 58).

## The name Hṛdaya Caitanya

Once, with the Lord's appearance day three days away and Gaurīdāsa still away visiting disciples, Hṛdayānanda sent out the invitations himself. Gaurīdāsa came back pleased within but feigned anger — "In my presence you act on your own!" — and sent him away. Sitting under a tree by the Ganges, Hṛdayānanda received a large donation from a passing merchant and, on his guru's order, held the festival on the river bank; Gaura and Nityānanda Themselves left the altar and danced in his kīrtana. When the pūjārī Baḍa Gaṅgādāsa found the altar empty, Gaurīdāsa went to the river with a stick; the two Lords vanished into Hṛdayānanda's heart. Weeping, Gaurīdāsa embraced him: "From today your name is **Hṛdaya Caitanya**" — he who has Caitanya in his heart — and gave him the service of the Deities (see the record of Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita).

## Śyāmānanda

The boy Duḥkhī came to him from Orissa, saying Hṛdaya Caitanya was his guru; he named him **Kṛṣṇadāsa**, initiated him, and sent him to study with Jīva Gosvāmī in Vṛndāvana. When he heard that his disciple had taken a new name and tilaka, he went to Vṛndāvana in anger and rebuked him, until Śrī Rādhā scolded him in a dream; then he embraced Śyāmānanda in tears. Later he sent him to preach in Orissa, where the saṅkīrtana had almost stopped, and when Śyāmānanda heard of his guru's passing he fainted, and was consoled by him in a dream.

He received **Jāhnavā Mātā** at Ambikā on her way to Kheturī, and Vīrabhadra on his way to Vṛndāvana, and went to the Kheturī festival with the devotees of Ambikā. Gaurīdāsa's granddaughter married his son, and the gosvāmīs of Ambikā, who worship in sakhya-rasa, descend from him (*Suvala-maṅgala*).

## Other persons of the same name

Hṛdayānanda Sena of Advaita's line (*Prema-vilāsa* 19) and the Hṛdayānanda of Gadādhara's line in Yadunātha's *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* (perhaps this same person).
