## In short

Śrī Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, the **maternal grandfather of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu** and father of Śacī Devī, came from Śrīhaṭṭa to settle at **Belpukhuriyā** in Navadvīpa. A great expert in astrology (jyotiṣa), he cast the horoscope of his grandson. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Garga Muni** and **Sumukha Gopa** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 104–105).

## Family

His gotra was **Rathītara**. He had two sons, **Yogeśvara Paṇḍita** (the elder) and **Ratnagarbha Ācārya**, and two daughters, **Śrī Śacī Mātā** and **Śrīmatī Sarvajayā Devī** (*Abhidhāna*, entry Lokanātha Paṇḍita; one text of Śacī's record, quoting the *Prema-vilāsa* 7, says one daughter and two sons). Śacī married **Jagannātha Miśra**; Sarvajayā, it seems, married **Candraśekhara Ācārya**, the Lord's uncle. Ratnagarbha's son **Lokanātha Paṇḍita** took dīkṣā from the Lord's elder brother Viśvarūpa (Śaṅkarāraṇya) and travelled with him (*Abhidhāna*).

"Śacī's father's house was at Belpukhuriyā; Yogeśvara Paṇḍita was the eldest son; then Ratnagarbha Paṇḍita, and Śacī was younger" (quoted in the *Abhidhāna*). Like many Vaiṣṇavas of the time — Advaita, Śrīvāsa, Jagannātha Miśra, Murāri Gupta — he came to Navadvīpa from Śrīhaṭṭa (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 2.35).

## The horoscope

Being an expert astrologer, he cast the horoscope of the newborn Nimāi (see *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 13).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Garga Muni** and **Sumukha Gopa** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 104–105).
