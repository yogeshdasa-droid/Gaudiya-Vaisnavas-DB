## In short

Śrī **Madhu Paṇḍita**, a disciple of Gadādhara Paṇḍita living in Vṛndāvana, to whom **Śrī Gopīnātha** of Vaṁśīvaṭa revealed Himself; he became the servant (adhikārī) of Gopīnātha and installed Śrī Rādhā beside Him (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). He is not named in the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

## Gopīnātha

"Paramānanda Bhaṭṭācārya, a great soul, and Śrī Madhu Paṇḍita, the abode of all virtues — Kṛṣṇa, the son of Vraja's king, is ruled by the love of both … Near Vaṁśīvaṭa, most charming, Gopīnātha sports in great joy; suddenly He showed Himself by His mercy, and Śrī Madhu Paṇḍita became the one to serve Him" (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 2.473–479). The *Sādhana-dīpikā* says the same. Thousands came to see Gopīnātha, and their burning of material life was cooled. He was a friend of **Paramānanda Bhaṭṭācārya**. When **Śrīnivāsa** and his companions left Vṛndāvana with the cart of books, Madhu Paṇḍita gave him Gopīnātha's garland as a blessing. The Rādhā-Gopīnātha deities are now worshipped in **Jaipur**.

## Saibonā

He came from **Saibonā**, two miles from Khaḍadaha (24 Parganas), where he set up the worship of **Rādhā-Nanda-dulāl**; some say this Nanda-dulāl, made of the same stone as Śyāmasundara of Khaḍadaha and Vallabhajī of Vallabhapur and brought from the Nawab's palace, was his. The service is now done by disciples of Śrī Oṁkāranātha. On the order of Gadādhara he went to Vṛndāvana. The *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* (34) praises him: "full of love sweet as honey, ever relishing rasa in Vṛndāvana". The *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna* counts him the thirty-first of Gadādhara's branch.

## Appearance and disappearance

**Disappearance:** Vaiśākha śukla navamī (Gauḍīya calendar).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Maṇḍalī sakhī**, according to the *Caitanya-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (Rāmāi Paṇḍita) and the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* of Balarāma dāsa.
