## In short

**Vallabha-caitanya dāsa** ("Ṭhākura Vallabha" in the family books), a brāhmaṇa of Rāḍha, was a disciple of **Gadādhara Paṇḍita** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 12.82) and preached in **Vikramapura** (East Bengal), with his śrīpāṭa at **Pañcasāra**.

## Life

It is said he worshipped Mahāśakti with great austerity in the Himālayas, until she told him that Rādhā was living in Navadvīpa as the companion of Lord Gaurāṅga; he came at once to Navadvīpa and took initiation from Gadādhara. Sent by Lord Caitanya to preach, he went to Vikramapura and settled at Pañcasāra, where there were then twenty Sanskrit schools. The Lord, crossing the Padmā to Nūrapura in Vikramapura, stayed briefly at Pañcasāra and bathed at the meeting of the rivers on the Kārtika Vāruṇī. Seeing his spiritual power, the Muslim ruler gave him land (Tāluk no. 60), where, told in a dream, he installed **Śrī Rādhāramaṇa**.

## Family

His disciple **Pūrṇacandra Bhaṭṭācārya** gave him his daughter in marriage; they had four sons, **Rāmacandra, Mādhavendra, Mathurānātha and Rāmakṛṣṇa** (the youngest lived celibate, serving a deity in Vṛndāvana), and a daughter, married to **Vaidika Viṣṇudāsa**, who installed Vāsudeva at Khātrā (Faridpur). His descendants live at Pañcasāra, Vinodapura, Cara-gaṅgārāmapura, Deobhoga, Icchāpura, Bāsāila, Siyālaḍi and other places (*Nāmāmṛta-samudra* 104; *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* 18).
