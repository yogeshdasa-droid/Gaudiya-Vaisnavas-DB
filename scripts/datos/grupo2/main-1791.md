## In short

**Raghunātha Paṇḍita** of Varāhanagara, a brāhmaṇa of Gadādhara Paṇḍita's branch ("Bhāgavatācārya, Haridāsa Brahmacārī", *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 12.79), to whom Lord Caitanya gave the title **Bhāgavatācārya**. He wrote the **Śrī Kṛṣṇa-prema-taraṅgiṇī**, a Bengali verse rendering of the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*. In Vraja he was **Śvetamañjarī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 195, 203: "Śrīmad Bhāgavatācārya, very dear to Gaurāṅga, made the book Kṛṣṇa-prema-taraṅgiṇī").

## The Lord at Varāhanagara

On His way to Nīlācala after sannyāsa, after Kumārahaṭṭa and Pāṇihāṭi, the Lord came to his house at Varāhanagara. Seeing Him, Raghunātha recited verses of the *Bhāgavatam* on bhakti, and the Lord fell into ecstasy, crying "Speak, speak!": "I have never heard the *Bhāgavatam* read like this; your name shall be Bhāgavatācārya, and this shall be your only work." He embraced him and spent the night there (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Antya 5.110–121). One text gives the day of the visit as Caitra kṛṣṇa dvādaśī.

## His guru

In the *Kṛṣṇa-prema-taraṅgiṇī* he calls himself a disciple of **Gadādhara Paṇḍita**: "the glories of Paṇḍita Gosāñi are sung in the three worlds … he is the pleasure potency of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, my worshipable Lord, my gurudeva". He had begun the book when he met the Lord, and it has many descriptions of Him.

## His śrīpāṭa

On the Gaṅgā at **Māli-pāḍā, Varāhanagara**, north of Calcutta: the **Pāṭbāḍī**, kept by the followers of Rāmadāsa Bābājī, with his own manuscripts and many others of Vaiṣṇava authors, and things used by the Pañca-tattva and their associates.

## Other Bhāgavatācāryas

The record also had short entries of other persons of the same title: a **Bhāgavata Ācārya** of Advaita's branch (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 12.58), once the scholar **Baḍa Śyāmadāsa**, who surrendered to Advaita after losing a debate (*Prema-vilāsa* 24), and a Bhāgavata Ācārya of Lord Caitanya's branch (Ādi 10.113, 119). They are not this Raghunātha Paṇḍita. **Bhāgavata dāsa**, companion of Bhūgarbha in Vṛndāvana (Ādi 12.81), and the Bhāgavata dāsa disciple of Narottama, are others again.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Śvetamañjarī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 195).
