## In short

Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita, called **Paṇḍita Prabhu** and **Gadāi**, is one of the **Pañca-tattva**: the Lord's own pleasure potency (īśa-śakti, *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 1.41, 4.227, 6.48), in Kṛṣṇa-līlā **Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī**, into whom Lalitā also entered (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 147–153). He was the Lord's companion from childhood, a lifelong brahmacārī, the disciple of **Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi**, and in Purī he served **Ṭoṭā-Gopīnātha** under a vow of kṣetra-sannyāsa and read the *Bhāgavatam* to the Lord. So the Lord is called **"Gadādhara-prāṇanātha"** and **"Gadāira Gaurāṅga"** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Antya 7.159–164).

## Birth and family

A Vārendra-śreṇī brāhmaṇa of the Kāśyapa gotra. Father: **Mādhava Miśra**; mother: **Ratnāvatī Devī**; younger brother: **Vāṇīnātha** (father of Nayanānanda). He was born on the **amāvasyā of Vaiśākha, 1408 Śaka** (1486 CE) (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). He lived in his birthplace until his twelfth year and at thirteen came to his maternal uncle's house in **Navadvīpa**, near Jagannātha Miśra's; some say Surarāja, a rich man of Kāndipura, brought him from Beliṭi. The birthplace is given as **Beliṭi** (Chittagong); Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa (*Sāhitya-pariṣat-patrikā* 4, 1377 BS) says Śrīhaṭṭa, the *Prema-vilāsa* (24) Chittagong. His grandfather was Vilāsa Ācārya, court scholar of King Citrasena at Beliṭi (*Prema-vilāsa* 24).

## With Śrī Caitanya in Navadvīpa

He studied at the same school as Nimāi, who teased him with questions of nyāya (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Ādi 12.20–27). He met **Īśvara Purī** at Gopīnātha Ācārya's house and studied with him Purī's *Kṛṣṇa-līlāmṛta* (Ādi 11.99–100). After the Lord came back from Gayā he fainted hearing His kīrtana at Śuklāmbara's house (Madhya 1.56–108); he told Advaita not to watch the Lord's worship (Ādi 12.126–142); he held back the Lord's hands when, in separation, He tore at His chest, and Śacī asked him never to leave her son (Madhya 2.202–209); he brought Him betel (Madhya 6.65, 20.27, 22.19). He danced as a gopī in the drama at Candraśekhara's house (Madhya 18.101–116), stood at the Lord's left in the dance after the Kāzī was subdued (Madhya 23.211, 491), played in the water after Jagāi and Mādhāi were delivered (Madhya 13.341), was told to do Viṣṇu-pūjā in the Lord's house (Madhya 25.91), and spent the last night with Him before His sannyāsa (Madhya 26.166–171, 28.44).

## His guru: Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi

Mukunda Datta took him to see **Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi**, newly come from Chittagong. Seeing him seated like a prince among mirrors, betel and peacock fans, Gadādhara doubted him; Mukunda sang the *Bhāgavatam* verse on Pūtanā and Puṇḍarīka fell into a storm of ecstasy. Repenting his offence, Gadādhara asked to become his disciple and, with the Lord's permission, received **dīkṣā** from him on an auspicious day (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Madhya 7.44–112). Years later in Purī, feeling he could not say his mantra properly, he **took the mantra again** from Puṇḍarīka, who had come for the Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī festival (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Antya 10.22–27; *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 16.78).

## In Purī: Ṭoṭā-Gopīnātha

He went with the Lord to Nīlācala (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Antya 2.35, 3.228–231), took **kṣetra-sannyāsa** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 1.252) and served the deity of **Ṭoṭā-Gopīnātha** near the sea at Yameśvara, where the Lord came often to hear him read the *Bhāgavatam* (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Antya 10.22–26; *Bhakti-ratnākara* 3). Nityānanda, the Lord and Gadādhara ate together at Ṭoṭā-Gopīnātha (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Antya 7.112–164), and played in the water of Narendra-sarovara (Antya 8.122). When the Lord left for Vṛndāvana he wanted to follow, breaking his vow; the Lord sent him back from Kaṭaka: "the Paṇḍita's love for Gaurāṅga cannot be understood — he gave up his vow and the service of Kṛṣṇa like straw" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 16.130–143). He refused, out of loyalty to the Lord, to initiate **Vallabha Bhaṭṭa** while the Lord seemed displeased with him, and later, with the Lord's permission, gave him the mantra (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Antya 7.86–171). Lord Caitanya wrote a verse in His own hand in Gadādhara's *Gītā*, which, with the small Gopīnātha he wore at his neck, he left at the end to his nephew **Nayanānanda** (*Prema-vilāsa*); they are still at Bharatpur.

## Disappearance

The Lord, at the end of His forty-eight years, entered the deity of Gopīnātha served by Gadādhara (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 8.356–357). Gadādhara stayed on in great separation (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 3.135–143), met the young **Śrīnivāsa** (3.147–152) and passed away in Purī on the **amāvasyā of Jyaiṣṭha, 1456 Śaka**, at forty-seven, eleven months after the Lord (*Abhidhāna*).

## His branch

The *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* (Ādi 12.79–86) counts thirty-two branches of Gadādhara; the *Abhidhāna* (entry «Paṇḍita-gosvāmi-śākhā»), adding those of Yadunātha dāsa's *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta*, gives fifty-six. Among them (the numbers of the *Abhidhāna*; a few are not legible in our copy): Dhruvānanda and Śrīdhara Brahmacārī ("the best of the branches"), Bhāgavatācārya, (4) Haridāsa Brahmacārī, (5) Ananta Ācārya, (6) Kavidatta, (7) Nayana Miśra, (8) Gaṅgāmantrī (with Māmu Ṭhākura and Kaṇṭhābharaṇa), Bhūgarbha and Bhāgavata dāsa, (14) Vāṇīnātha Brahmacārī, (15) Vallabha-caitanya, (16) Śrīnātha, (17) Uddhava dāsa, (18) Jitāmitra, (19) Jagannātha dāsa the wood-cutter, (20) Hari Ācārya, (21) Sādipuriyā Gopāla, (23) Lakṣmīnātha, (24) Baṅgavāṭī Caitanya dāsa, (25) Raghunātha, (26) Śivānanda Cakravartī, (27) Jayānanda, (28) Amogha Paṇḍita, (29) Mādhava Ācārya, (30) Gopāla dāsa, (31) Madhu Paṇḍita, (32) Candraśekhara, (33) Vakreśvara Paṇḍita, (34) Dāmodara Paṇḍita, (35) Svarūpa Dāmodara, (36) a second Ananta Ācārya, (37) Kṛṣṇadāsa, (38) Paramānanda Bhaṭṭācārya, (39) Bhavānanda Gosvāmī, (40) Yadunātha (Gāṅgulī) Cakravartī, (41) Puṣpa-gopāla, (42) Kṛṣṇadāsa Brahmacārī, (43) Lokanātha Bhaṭṭa, (44) Ananta Ācārya of the Gaṅgā bank, (45) Maṅgala Vaiṣṇava dāsa, (46) Govinda Ācārya, (47) Akrūra Ṭhākura, (48) Saṅketa Ācārya, (49) King Pratāparudra, (50) Ācārya Kamalākānta, (51) Yādavācārya, (52) Vallabha Bhaṭṭa of Āḍāila, (53) Nārāyaṇa Paḍihārī, (54) Hṛdayānanda, (55) Caitanya-vallabha, (56) Hasti-gopāla. **Acyutānanda**, Advaita's son, took shelter of him: "the chief of Gadādhara Paṇḍita's disciples" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Antya 4.206; *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*). **Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī** was also his disciple.

## Mantra, prayers and writings

His **mantra**, meditation and **gāyatrī**, and the **Gaura-Gadādhara mantra**, are in Dhyānacandra Gosvāmī's *Paddhati* (52, 60, 72); the *Caitanyārcana-candrikā* quoted there places him at the Lord's left in the Yogapīṭha (37–44). **Aṣṭakas** on him by Sanātana, Rūpa, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Lokanātha, Bhūgarbha, Paramānanda Gosvāmī and Śivānanda Cakravartī; *Gaura-Gadādharāṣṭakas* by Acyutānanda and Nayanānanda Miśra; a *Rati-janaka-dvādaśa-nāma-stotra* and a hundred and eight names by Sārvabhauma; the *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* by Yadunātha. He wrote the **Premāmṛta-stotra** (*Sādhana-dīpikā* 9). Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura: *smara gaura-gadādhara-keli-kalām…*

## Appearance and disappearance

**Appearance:** Vaiśākha amāvasyā (Gauḍīya calendar; *Abhidhāna*: 1408 Śaka).
**Disappearance:** Jyaiṣṭha amāvasyā, 1456 Śaka, in Purī (*Abhidhāna*); the Gauḍīya calendar, which counts months from full moon to full moon, puts this new moon in Āṣāḍha (Vāmana).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī**, with Lalitā entering him (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 147–153); Svarūpa Dāmodara's notes and Vāsudeva Ghoṣa's songs say the same.
