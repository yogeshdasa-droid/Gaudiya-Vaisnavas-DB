## In short

**Gadādhara Bhaṭṭa** of Vṛndāvana, a disciple of **Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī**, was the author of the **Mohinī-vāṇī**, "good, kind, gentle, and a most delightful speaker on the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*" (*Bhaktamāla* 23). "Bhaṭṭa Gadādhara, dear to Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī, revealed the charming meanings of the *Bhāgavata*" (*Nāmāmṛta-samudra* 271).

## Jīva Gosvāmī's letter

Jīva Gosvāmī was so moved on hearing one of his songs that he sent two men to his village with a letter bearing this verse: "Without worshipping the dust of Rādhā's lotus feet, without taking shelter of Vṛndāvana marked with her footprints, without speaking with those whose hearts are deep with her love, how can one dive into the rasa of the dark ocean, Śyāma?" At dawn they met a man bathing and asked him where Gadādhara Bhaṭṭa lived; he asked where they came from: "from the crown of all, Vṛndāvana-dhāma". Hearing the name of Vṛndāvana he fell unconscious in love. Knowing then who he was, they gave him the letter; he touched it to his head, read it, left at once for Vṛndāvana, met Jīva Gosvāmī and surrendered at the feet of Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa (*Bhaktamāla* 23).

## Another Gadādhara Bhaṭṭa

The *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna* has first a Gadādhara Bhaṭṭa who was **Raṅgadevī** in Kṛṣṇa-līlā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 165), whose śrīpāṭa is at **Hanumānpur** in Tailaṅga-deśa (Andhra), and then, as a second entry, this disciple of Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa. They may be two persons.

## Not to be confused

Not **Gadādhara Paṇḍita**, nor **Gadādhara dāsa**, nor the logician **Gadādhara Bhaṭṭācārya** (author of the *Śaktivāda*).
