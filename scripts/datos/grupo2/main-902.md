## In short

**Maṅgala Vaiṣṇava** (Maṅgala Ṭhākura), of Gadādhara Paṇḍita's branch ("Yadu Gāṅgulī and Maṅgala Vaiṣṇava", *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 12.86), was initiated by Gadādhara himself and served the deity of **Śrī Gaura-gopāla**, worshipped before by Gadādhara; his line lives at **Kāndaḍā** (Birbhum).

## Life

He lived at **Kirīṭakoṇā** in Murshidabad. Losing his parents as a child, he wandered from place to place and settled on the high ground of **Rāḍhīpura**, west of Kāndaḍā, with his family Śālagrāma of **Nṛsiṁha**. He was always in meditation and offered all the food he begged to his deity. Gadādhara came to him and of his own accord gave him initiation, on the day of the autumn **Kalpārambha**, and stayed until the new moon; he told him to serve Śrī Gaura-gopāla. In memory of those days the **Sāñjhī** festival is still held there (*Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* 43).

## His line

He gave dīkṣā to **Nṛsiṁhavallabha**, founder of the Mitra-ṭhākura family of Mayanāḍāla. Descendants of his disciples live at Kāndaḍā; **Kālācānd Ṭhākura** of this family was famous for the tāla and style (mān) of the **Manoharaśāhī** kīrtana. Verse 130 of the *Padyāvalī* may be his.
