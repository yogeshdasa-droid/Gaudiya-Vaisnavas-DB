## In short

**Ananta Ācārya**, a brāhmaṇa of Vṛndāvana of Gadādhara Paṇḍita's branch ("Ananta Ācārya, Kavidatta, Miśra Nayana", *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 12.80), was the **adhikārī of Śrī Govindadeva**: "the disciples of Gadādhara Paṇḍita Gosāñi … the adhikārī of Govinda, Śrī Anantācārya" (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 13). His disciple **Haridāsa Paṇḍita** told Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja to write the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā: **Sudevī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 165).

## Guru-praṇālī

Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi → Gadādhara Paṇḍita → Ananta Ācārya → Haridāsa Paṇḍita → Rādhākṛṣṇa Cakravartī (*Abhidhāna*).

## In Vṛndāvana

With other devotees he went out to receive **Vīrabhadra Prabhu** when he came to Vṛndāvana (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 13.313–314).

## Others of the name

Yadunātha dāsa names three Ananta Ācāryas in his *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* (8, 39, 47); the *Abhidhāna*'s list of Gadādhara's branch also has a second Ananta Ācārya and one "of the Gaṅgā bank". It is not known which is meant in the *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā*. A poet **Ananta** wrote one song in the *Padakalpataru* (no. 2285); the *Abhidhāna* says his identity is not settled (Ananta Ācārya, Ananta dāsa or Ananta Paṇḍita?). **Ananta Caṭṭarāja**, called Śrī **Kaṇṭhābharaṇa**, has his own record. An Ananta Ācārya of Advaita's branch is named in Ādi 12.58.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Sudevī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 165).
