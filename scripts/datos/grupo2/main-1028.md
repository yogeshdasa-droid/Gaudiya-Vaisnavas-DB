## In short

Śrī **Nayanānanda Miśra** (Nayanānanda Ṭhākura), son of **Vāṇīnātha**, Gadādhara Paṇḍita's younger brother, was Gadādhara's nephew and dear disciple, "**Miśra Nayana**" of his branch (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 12.80). Gadādhara left him the deity of **Gopīnātha** and his *Gītā* with a verse in Lord Caitanya's handwriting; his descendants still serve them at **Bharatpur** near Kāndi (Murshidabad). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Nitya-mañjarī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 196, 207).

## Family

Vāṇīnātha (also called Jagannātha) had two sons, **Nayanānanda** and **Hṛdayānanda**. Nayanānanda was married.

## Gadādhara's legacy

When Gadādhara went to Purī, Nayanānanda took charge of the deity of **Rādhā-Gopīnātha** installed by him at Bharatpur. At the end, the *Prema-vilāsa* says, Gadādhara called him to his bed: "I give into your care Śrī Gopīnātha and this *Bhagavad-gītā*, in which Mahāprabhu wrote one verse in His own hand while I was copying it on palm leaves." He also gave him the small Gopīnātha he wore at his neck, not to be parted from the Lord. The Gosvāmīs of Bharatpur still show the two verses in the Lord's hand in the manuscript of the *Gītā*.

## His name

The texts say his earlier name was **Dhruvānanda**: when Gaura and Gadādhara sang in ecstasy, he wrote down at once what he heard, and they, pleased, named him Nayanānanda (*Padasamudra*). (The *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* names a **Dhruvānanda** separately in the same branch, Ādi 12.79: "the best of the branches, Dhruvānanda and Śrīdhara Brahmacārī".) He was at the festival of **Kheturī**. He was a fine poet; no book of his is found, but some of his songs are in the *Padakalpataru*. He wrote a *Gaura-Gadādharāṣṭaka* (*Abhidhāna*, «Gadādhara Paṇḍita»).

## Others of the name

**Nayanānanda Kavirāja** of Śrīkhaṇḍa, disciple of Raghunandana Ṭhākura (author of the *Akiñcana-sarvasva*); **Nayanānanda Ṭhākura** of Maṅgaladihi (author of the *Kṛṣṇa-bhakti-rasa-kadamba*, 1652 Śaka); **Nayanānanda Deva**, grandson of Rasikānanda (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). They have their own records.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Nitya-mañjarī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 196, 207; *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* 10).
