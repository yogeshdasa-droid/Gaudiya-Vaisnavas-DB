## In short

**Haridāsa Paṇḍita** (Paṇḍita Haridāsa) of Vṛndāvana, the beloved disciple of **Ananta Ācārya** (of Gadādhara Paṇḍita's line), was the **head of the service of Śrī Govindadeva**. He told **Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja** to write the last pastimes of Lord Caitanya: "he very mercifully ordered me to describe the last līlā of Gaurāṅga" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 8.65).

## His qualities

"The head of the service is Śrī Paṇḍita Haridāsa, whose fame and qualities are known in all the world: gentle, tolerant, peaceful, generous, grave, sweet in speech and deed, very steady; he respects all and does good to all; his heart has no crookedness, envy or violence. The fifty qualities of Kṛṣṇa live in his body. … He has firm faith in Caitanya and Nityānanda and great joy in Caitanya's life; he sees the good in Vaiṣṇavas, never the faults, and pleases them in body, mind and words. Always he hears the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, and by his favour all the Vaiṣṇavas hear it. In his talks he lights up the assembly like the full moon" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 8.54–64).

## Guru-praṇālī and disciples

Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi → Gadādhara Paṇḍita → Ananta Ācārya → Haridāsa Paṇḍita → **Rādhākṛṣṇa Cakravartī**, who praises him at the beginning of his *Daśa-ślokī-bhāṣya*: "I worship day and night the guru named Śrī Haridāsa, an ornament of the Vārendra brāhmaṇas, the adhikārī of Govindadeva, abode of every auspicious quality". **Kṛṣṇadāsa Premī**, a disciple of Bhūgarbha, to whom Rūpa Gosvāmī had first given the service of Govinda, handed it on to him (*Sādhana-dīpikā* 1). The *Bhakti-ratnākara* tells how Govinda showed that He was ruled by his love, and that he received **Vīrabhadra Prabhu** in Vṛndāvana (13.312–314).

## Not to be confused

Not **Haridāsa Ṭhākura** (Nāmācārya), nor **Haridāsa Brahmacārī** of Gadādhara's branch, nor **Haridāsa Svāmī** of the Nimbārka line, nor **Haridāsa Śiromaṇi**, Narottama's disciple.
