## In short

**Haridāsa Bābājī Mahāśaya**, disciple of the well-known **Dayāla dāsa Bābājī** of Vṛndāvana (#193), did bhajana for seven years at **Ariṭ-grāma**, ten at **Govinda-kuṇḍa**, five at the village of **Pāiṭhā** and his last six at **Jatipurā**. **Advaita dāsa Bābājī** described him as one who lived by Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī's *Sva-niyama-daśaka*.

## As Advaita dāsa saw him

"Haridāsa Bābājī behaved as the *Sva-niyama-daśaka* of Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī directs. Thinking himself the lowest of the low, he humbly kept his distance from everyone. He wandered all over Vraja looking for Lord Kṛṣṇa's footprints. If someone brought him prasāda, he took it with great reverence and put it in a vessel in his room; he ate just a particle of it with some dry mādhukarī, but coming out he would tell the people he had eaten it all. His room was completely dark, and in it were many pots, each holding water sanctified by the feet and mouths of numerous Vaiṣṇava saints. The rest of the prasāda he carried into the forest and left under a tree, for to give it directly to anyone would have been an obstacle to his bhajana."
