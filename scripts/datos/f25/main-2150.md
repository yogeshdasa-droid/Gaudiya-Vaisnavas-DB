## In short

**Jagannātha Gajapati Nārāyaṇa Deva** is the name of rulers of the **Kimedi** estates in the Ganjam district of South India — **Parlākimedi**, Peddakimedi and Cinnakimedi — whose three zamindār families claim descent from one royal house, the **Keśarī-vaṁśa**. In the genealogy kept by the zamindār of Parlākimedi, **Jagannātha-nārāyaṇa Deva** ruled 1766-1806 and **Jagannātha Gajapati-nārāyaṇa Deva** 1843-1850 (Viśvakoṣa VI, 598).
