## In short

**Rāmadāsa Viśvāsa**, a kāyastha, a devotee of Rāma and a great scholar drawn to Vaiṣṇavism, met **Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa**, son of Tapana Miśra, on his way to Lord Caitanya, and served him devotedly all the way to Nīlācala. The Lord, knowing that some pride of learning remained in his heart, did not show him His grace at once. He settled at **Purī** and taught the *Kāvya-prakāśa* to the sons of the Paṭṭanāyakas (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 13).

## On the road

Meeting Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa and his attendant, Rāmadāsa began to serve him with such devotion that Raghunātha was embarrassed. At Nīlācala Raghunātha spoke of him to Lord Caitanya, but the all-knowing Lord did not bestow His mercy on him straight away, for He saw that within his heart Rāmadāsa still had some vanity about his learning.
