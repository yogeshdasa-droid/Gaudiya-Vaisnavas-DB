## In short

**Rāmacandra dāsa Bābājī** lived in a concrete hut across from Raghunāthgañj at **Jaṅgīpur** (Murshidabad). Seeing that the Gaurāṅga community of his day had no patrons to sustain it, he inspired **Mahārāja Maṇīndracandra Nandī** of Kāśimbāzār to serve the Vaiṣṇavas on a large scale. He passed away in **1907**, at more than 110, soon after a meeting with **Kāminī Kumār Ghoṣ**.

## The meeting

In 1907 Kāminī Kumār Ghoṣ spent two hours with him in his garden at Jaṅgīpur. Though over 110, he looked no more than seventy. Asking about Kāminī's guru, Rāmacandra found that they were related in spiritual lineage. Soon after this meeting he passed away.
