## In short

**Navadvīpacandra Gosvāmī Vidyāratna**, a paṇḍita of **Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu's family**, wrote the **Vaiṣṇavācāra-darpaṇa**, the **Vaiṣṇava-vrata-dina-nirṇaya** and the **Aruṇodaya-vedhe Janmāṣṭamī-parityāgera vidhi**, and explained vaidhī and rāgānugā bhakti in simple Bengali for those who could not read Sanskrit. His descendants still serve **Sonār Gaurāṅga** and other Deities in **Śrīvāsa's house** at Navadvīpa.

## The Śaṅkarācārya-vijaya

In **1867** the Asiatic Society of Bengal asked him to edit the *Śaṅkarācārya-vijaya*; on his retirement he had corrected thirty-three chapters, and he handed the work over to Jayanārāyaṇa Tarkapañcānana.
