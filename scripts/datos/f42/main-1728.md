## In short

**Viśveśvara Ācārya** (Viśveśvarānanda Ācārya) was the father of **Mādhavācārya**, who married **Gaṅgā**, Lord Nityānanda's daughter. A close friend of **Bhagīratha Ācārya** of the same village, when he lost his wife he left his son in the care of Bhagīratha's wife **Jayadurgā**, took sannyāsa and settled at Kāśī (Prema-vilāsa 21). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Divākara**, the sun-god (Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā 113; Vaiṣṇava-vandanā of Jīva 135, of Devakīnandana 51, of Vṛndāvana dāsa 46).
