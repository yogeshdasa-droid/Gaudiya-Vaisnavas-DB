## In short

**Kāśīśvara Vaiṣṇava** of Kāśī was a disciple of **Candraśekhara Vaidya**, in whose house Lord Caitanya taught Sanātana Gosvāmī. When Candraśekhara and the other devotees of Kāśī had passed away, he went on guarding and serving the place as his guru had told him. When **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya** and **Narottama** came, he took them to all the places of the Lord's pastimes there (Prema-vilāsa 10).
