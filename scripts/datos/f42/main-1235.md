## In short

**Rāma**, a poor brāhmaṇa from South India, fasted seven days for the grace of Lord Jagannātha shortly before Lord Caitanya's disappearance. Failing, he resolved to drown himself in the sea, but met **Vibhīṣaṇa**, who gave him good counsel; following him, Rāma found himself before Lord Caitanya, and Vibhīṣaṇa gave him great wealth (Caitanya-maṅgala, Śeṣa 4.4.91).
