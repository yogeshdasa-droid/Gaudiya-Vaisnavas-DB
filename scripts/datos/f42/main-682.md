## In short

**Kapilendra Deva** is said to have founded the **Gajapati** royal house of Orissa; he ruled from 1435 to 1470, with his capital at Cuttack, and was the father of **Puruṣottama Deva** (#1131). His edicts are found in the temples of Jagannātha at Purī, of Bhubaneswar and of Kūrmadeva in Ganjam.

## His edicts at Purī

They order utensils for Lord Jagannātha's service, offerings of ornaments, dances by Āndhra dancers between the evening worship and the baḍa-śṛṅgāra, and the recitation of **Jayadeva's Gīta-govinda**.
