## In short

**Mādhava Miśra** (Mādhavācārya), a Vaidika brāhmaṇa, son of **Kālidāsa** and **Vidhumukhī-devī** (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Ādi 12.64, 10.119; Bhakti-ratnākara 9.394, 10.373), studied for a time at **Lord Gaurāṅga's** Sanskrit school. When his widowed mother tried to get him married, he fled to Vṛndāvana and came back only after her death. On his second pilgrimage to Vṛndāvana he may have gone with Nityānanda (Balarāma dāsa), the author of the Prema-vilāsa.
