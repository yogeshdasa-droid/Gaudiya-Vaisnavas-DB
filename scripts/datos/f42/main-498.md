## In short

**Hīrā** — «Lakṣahīrā», later **Kṛṣṇadāsī** — was the courtesan patronised by **Rāmacandra Khān** of Kāgajapukuriyā near Benāpola (#1245), who paid her a lakh of coins to break the sādhana of **Haridāsa Ṭhākura**. Under Haridāsa's holy influence she became a devout Vaiṣṇavī.

## After

A house was built for her at Gayaḍā-Rājapura near Kāgajapukuriyā; traces of the canal by which Rāmacandra Khān used to go to her house by boat can still be seen (*Jaśohar-Khulnār Itihās*, pp. 364-365).
