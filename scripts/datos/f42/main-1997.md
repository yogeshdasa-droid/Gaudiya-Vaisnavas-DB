## In short

**Padmanābha**, great-grandson of **Jagadguru Sarvajña** and paternal great-grandfather of **Rūpa, Sanātana** and **Anupama**, left Karṇāṭa after a family feud and settled in the kingdom of **Śikhareśvara**; in old age he built a house at **Navahaṭṭa** (Naihāṭī) on the Bhāgīrathī, much helped by King **Danujamardana** (#186). Of his eighteen daughters and five sons, the youngest son, **Mukunda**, was the father of **Kumāradeva**, father of Rūpa, Sanātana and Anupama.

## Sources

The record's text (Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna); the records of Danujamardana (#186) and Rājā Gaṇeśa (#285), at whose court he is said to have held high office. Not Padmanābha Cakravartī, father of Lokanātha Gosvāmī.
