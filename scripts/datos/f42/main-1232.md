## In short

**Rāma Rāya Gosvāmī**, of the family of a disciple of Lord Nityānanda and of the line of the poet **Jayadeva**, wrote the **Gaura-vinodinī vṛtti**, a commentary on Lord Caitanya's Śikṣāṣṭaka, the **Gaura-gītā** and some 4,000 padas in Vrajabuli, and put the Gīta-govinda into Vrajabuli verse. **Nābhājī** mentions him in the Bhaktamāla, and his descendants live at Bihārīpaḍā in Vṛndāvana. A pada of the Padakalpataru (1844) bears the name Rāma Rāya.
