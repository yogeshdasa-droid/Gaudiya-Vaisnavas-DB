## In short

**Bhaiyā Devakīnandana**, a wealthy Faujdār in the Nawab's government and once a follower of Tantric practice, married the daughter of a Vaiṣṇava and through her became a devotee, taking spiritual guidance from the descendants of **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya** at **Mālihāṭī** (Bhaktamāla 17). The Deity **Śrī Nandadulāla** he served is still at Kiśoranagara-Jālālapura.
