## In short

**Kṛṣṇadāsa**, a disciple of **Pūjārī Ṭhākura**, left Gauḍa and settled in Vṛndāvana (Prema-vilāsa). When he and **Rāmadāsa**, a disciple of Bhūgarbha Ṭhākura, set out on pilgrimage from Vṛndāvana to Purī, **Jīva Gosvāmī** and **Lokanātha Gosvāmī** told them to go by Kheturī, Yājigrāma and Gopīvallabhapura, to bring news of **Narottama, Śrīnivāsa** and **Śyāmānanda** and to see how respectfully the three treated other Vaiṣṇavas (Prema-vilāsa 17).
