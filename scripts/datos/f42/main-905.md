## In short

**Mahārāja Maṇīndracandra Nandī** of **Kāśimbāzār** (1860–1929), a humble king free from the desire for sense pleasure, spent enormous sums to spread the Vaiṣṇava faith, build temples, restore holy places and recover forgotten Vaiṣṇava books. He walked barefoot in the nagara-saṅkīrtana, and pleased the Vaiṣṇava world by publishing the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam with a Bengali translation and notes. **Rāmacandra dāsa Bābājī** of Jaṅgīpur (#1239) had moved him to serve the Vaiṣṇavas.
