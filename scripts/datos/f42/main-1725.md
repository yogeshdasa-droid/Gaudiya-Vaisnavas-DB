## In short

**Viśvarūpa**, the elder brother of **Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu**, was **Baladeva** (Saṅkarṣaṇa) in Kṛṣṇa-līlā (Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā 58-60). Long before the Lord's sannyāsa he took sannyāsa at **Kāśī** from **Śrī Kṛṣṇa Bhāratī** with the name **Śaṅkarāraṇya**, and left this world at the temple of **Śrī Viṭhṭhalanātha** at Paṇḍharpur, where his samādhi is said to be.

## Paṇḍharpur

In the course of His travels in the South, Lord Caitanya came to Paṇḍharpur and met **Raṅga Purī**, who told Him all he knew of Viśvarūpa.
