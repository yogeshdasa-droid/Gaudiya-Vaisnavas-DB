## In short

**Haridāsa**, a well-known scholar of bhakti-śāstra and a relative of **Viṭṭhaleśvara** (#1729), son of Vallabhācārya, wrote many short Sanskrit works on bhakti-tattva (Viśvakoṣa, vol. 22, p. 488).

## His works

*Aiśvarya-vivaraṇa*; *Kāmākhyā-doṣa-vivaraṇa*; *Ṭippanyāśaya*; a ṭīkā on Vallabhācārya's *Navaratna-prakāśa*; *Nirodha-lakṣaṇa-vivṛti*; *Vedānta-siddhānta-kaumudī*; *Śruti-kalpadruma*; *Śloka-pañcaka-vivaraṇa*; *Siddhānta-rahasya-vṛtti-kārikā*; *Sevana-bhāvanā-kāvya*; *Sevā-phala-stotra-vivṛti*; *Svamārga-dharma-vivaraṇa*.
