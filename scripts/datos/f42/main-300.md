## In short

**Gaṅgānārāyaṇa** («Gaṅgārāma») **Caudhurī**, grandson of **Cakrapāṇi Caudhurī** of Śrīkhaṇḍa, of the circle of Raghunandana Ṭhākura, was the grandfather of **Madana**, who put the Govinda-līlāmṛta into Bengali verse, and of **Rāmagopāla** (#1265), who compiled the *Rasa-kalpavallī*; Rāmagopāla's son **Pītāmbara** wrote the *Rasa-mañjarī*.

## The family

Cakrapāṇi (with his brother Mahānanda) → Nityānanda → Gaṅgārāma → Śyāmarāya → Madana and Rāmagopāla → (Rāmagopāla's son) Pītāmbara (*Baṅga-bhāṣā o Sāhitya*).
