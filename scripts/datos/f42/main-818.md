## In short

**Kutubuddīn**, a Muslim dacoit chief, tried to attack the party of **Jāhnavā-devī**, Nityānanda's wife, on her way to Vṛndāvana. By her power the dacoits ran here and there all night without finding them; at dawn they understood what had happened and took shelter at Jāhnavā's feet (Prema-vilāsa 9).
