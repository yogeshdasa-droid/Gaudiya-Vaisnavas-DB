## In short

Kholāvecā Śrīdhara (Śrīdhara Paṇḍita) was a poor brāhmaṇa of Navadvīpa who lived by selling banana leaves, stalks and flowers (khola), and one of the dearest servants of Śrī Caitanya: "Kholāvecā Śrīdhara was the Lord's dear servant; the Lord always joked with him" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.67). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was the joking cowherd boy **Kusumāsava**, also called **Madhumaṅgala**: "kholāvecatayā khyātaḥ paṇḍitaḥ śrīdharo dvijaḥ / āsīd vraje hāsya-karo yo nāmnā kusumāsavaḥ" (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 133). He is also counted among the twelve gopālas.

## His life

He lived at the edge of Nadia (Māyāpur) and had a stall in the bazaar at Bāmanpukur. He was very honest: he asked a fair price and would not haggle, so as to save his time for the holy name. Half of what he earned went to the worship of Mother Gaṅgā, and he lived on the rest. In his little hut with a leaking roof he chanted the holy name loudly through the last part of the night, and his neighbours abused him: "This fellow can't fill his belly with rice, and we lose our sleep from his hungry moaning" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 9.148).

**The haggling.** Sent by His mother, young Nimāi would come to buy bananas, radishes and greens, offer half the price, and the two would pull the vegetables back and forth while people gathered to laugh. "Why do you snatch them, brother Śrīdhara? I know you have hidden wealth. You still don't know who I am? I am the father of the Gaṅgā you worship" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 9.173). Śrīdhara put his hands over his ears crying "Viṣṇu! Viṣṇu!", and in the end gave everything for free. Every day the Lord ate his banana stalks and flowers from his leaf plates.

**At his house.** "You chant Hari all day and worship Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa — why are you so poor? The people who worship Caṇḍī and Durgā lack nothing." — "Ṭhākura, I am not fasting, and I wear some cloth. The king in his palace and the bird in the tree — everyone's days pass by the will of the Lord" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 12.189–190). "Who do you think I am?" — "A brāhmaṇa, a part of Viṣṇu." — "No, I am a cowherd boy, and the glories of your Gaṅgā come from Me." — "You don't even respect Mother Gaṅgā! Go home now and don't quarrel with me."

**The water pot.** Once, dancing in the kīrtana, the Lord came to Śrīdhara's broken hut and drank with great pleasure from his old dented iron pot, so patched that no thief would take it. Śrīdhara cried "I am finished!" and fainted; the Lord said: "Today I have got devotion to Kṛṣṇa by drinking water from Śrīdhara's pot," and wept; Nityānanda, Gadādhara, Advaita, Śrīvāsa and all the devotees rolled on the ground.

**The mahā-prakāśa.** On the day the Lord revealed Himself in Śrīvāsa's house, He sent for Śrīdhara in the middle of the night; the devotees found him chanting loudly, and he fainted at the mention of the Lord, so they carried him on their shoulders. The Lord showed him His form as Śyāmasundara with the flute, Balarāma at His right, Lakṣmī offering betel, and Brahmā and Śiva singing hymns. The Lord gave him the power to praise Him and offered him the eight mystic perfections; Śrīdhara asked only: "That brāhmaṇa who snatched my banana leaves, who quarrelled with me — may He be my Lord birth after birth" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 9.190–235). "What Śrīdhara got by selling bananas and radishes, the richest men never see in millions of ages."

**The last night.** On the day before the Lord left for sannyāsa, Śrīdhara brought Him a gourd; the Lord had Śacī Mātā cook it with milk and ate it with the devotees: "How can I refuse what you bring?" Later Śrīdhara went to Purī every year with the Bengali devotees.

He is named by Murāri Gupta (4.17.8), in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Ādi 1.11), Jayānanda's *Caitanya-maṅgala* and the *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā*s of Jīva, Devakīnandana and Vṛndāvana dāsa.

## Disappearance

The Gauḍīya calendar keeps it on the **pūrṇimā of Jyeṣṭha**, the day of Snāna-yātrā (as «Śrīdhara Paṇḍita»).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Kusumāsava** (Madhumaṅgala), the cowherd boy who made Kṛṣṇa laugh (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 133).

## Other persons of the same name

- Śrīdhara of Nityānanda's branch (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.48).
- Śrīdhara Brahmacārī of Gadādhara Paṇḍita's branch.
- Śrīdhara Svāmī, author of the *Bhāvārtha-dīpikā* on the Bhāgavatam.
