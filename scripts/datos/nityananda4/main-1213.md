## In short

Raghunātha Vaidya Upādhyāya was a branch of Śrī Nityānanda: "Raghunātha Vaidya Upādhyāya, by whose sight love for Kṛṣṇa awakens" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.26; *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5.726).

## Life

By Lord Caitanya's order he went from Purī to Bengal with Nityānanda Prabhu (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5.231), and on the way he was overcome by the mood of **Revatī**, Balarāma's consort: "Raghunātha Vaidya Upādhyāya, the great soul, became Revatī in person" (Antya 5.239). He was present at the great festival of **Kheturī**.

## Other persons of the same name

- Raghunātha Vaidya of Śrī Caitanya's branch, the Lord's companion in Nīlācala (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.126) — his own record.
- Raghunātha Vaidya, disciple of Narottama Ṭhākura (*Prema-vilāsa* 20) — his own record.
