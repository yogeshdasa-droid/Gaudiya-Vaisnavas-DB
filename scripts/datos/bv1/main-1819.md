## In short

Śrīla Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī (Vaṁśī dāsa, c. 1838–1915) was renunciation itself. A grain merchant who left home when his wife died, he lived about thirty years in Vṛndāvana and then in Navadvīpa, wearing cloth thrown away at the burning ghāṭ and eating rice soaked in Gaṅgā water. He received the bābājī dress from **[[Śrīla Bhagavat dāsa Bābājī#ficha-1920|Bhāgavata dāsa Bābājī]]**, disciple of **[[Śrī Jagannātha dās Bābājī#ficha-1828|Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī]]**, and took **[[Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura#ficha-128|Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura]]** as his śikṣā guru, hearing the Bhāgavatam from him at Godruma. His only disciple was **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**.

## Life

- Born in a vaiśya family at Vāgyāna (Bāgjānā) near Ṭepākholā on the Padmā (Faridpur, now Bangladesh). As a householder, Vaṁśī dāsa, he traded in grain.
- After his wife died he went to Vṛndāvana, took the bābājī dress from Bhāgavata dāsa Bābājī and lived there in strict bhajana for about thirty years, visiting the holy places of Vraja and Gauḍa.
- Then he came to Navadvīpa-dhāma and lived under trees and by the Gaṅgā, chanting day and night, cooking in discarded earthen pots. He refused the invitation of the Mahārāja of Kāsimbāzār, who wanted him to live in his palace, and kept away from those who came only for show.
- He gave dīkṣā to Bimala Prasāda (Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī) at Godruma in 1900, after refusing him twice to test him.
- He left the world in 1915 at Kuliyā (Navadvīpa town), on **Kārtika Śukla Daśamī**, the day of Utthāna Ekādaśī (Gauḍīya calendar). Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī buried him there himself; his samādhi was moved in 1932 to Śrī Caitanya Maṭha, Māyāpura.

## Other versions

- Some texts list his gurus as «Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura» and «Bhāgavata dāsa Bābājī» without saying which is which: Bhāgavata dāsa gave him the bābājī dress (veśa); Bhaktivinoda was his śikṣā guru.
- His long biography is in the book «Two Beyond Duality», with Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī's.
