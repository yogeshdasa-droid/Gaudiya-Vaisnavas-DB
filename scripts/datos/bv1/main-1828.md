## In short

Śrīla Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī, «Vaiṣṇava-sārvabhauma» — the emperor of the Vaiṣṇavas — was the śikṣā guru of **[[Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura#ficha-128|Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura]]** and confirmed the birthplace of Śrī Caitanya at Māyāpura, dancing there at a very great age.

> gaurāvirbhāva-bhūmes tvaṁ nirdeṣṭā saj-jana-priyaḥ / vaiṣṇava-sārvabhaumaḥ śrī-jagannāthāya te namaḥ
> «I bow to Śrī Jagannātha, the emperor of the Vaiṣṇavas, dear to the saintly, who showed where Śrī Caitanya appeared.»

## Life

- Born in the Mymensingh district of Bengal. Disciple of **[[Madhusūdana dāsa bābājī#ficha-1848|Madhusūdana dāsa Bābājī]]** of Sūrya-kuṇḍa, who was in the line of Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa through Uddhava dāsa. He lived long in Vṛndāvana, known there as perfect in bhakti.
- In **1880** Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura met him in Vṛndāvana and received his instructions; they met again at Āmalājoḍā (Bardhamān) in Phālguna, where they kept Ekādaśī awake with kīrtana and Bhaktivinoda founded his Prapannāśrama.
- He lived at Koladvīpa (Kuliyā, Navadvīpa town); in **1893** he went to Surabhi-kuñja in Godruma. He revealed the **Yogapīṭha**, Śrīvāsa-aṅgana and other lost places of Māyāpura, and gave Bhaktivinoda the service of Giridhārī. He asked the young Bimala Prasāda (Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī) to prepare the Vaiṣṇava calendar: the start of the Navadvīpa Pañjikā.
- In old age, nearly blind and unable to walk, he was carried in a basket by his strong servant **Bihārī dāsa**, yet raised his arms in kīrtana; he is said to have lived to about 135. He had Bihārī spend all the donations on rasagollās for the cows of Navadvīpa, would not let the dogs of the dhāma be driven from his plate, and called professional Bhāgavatam reading «prostitution» and an offence to the holy name.
- He gave the bābājī dress to **[[Śrīla Bhagavat dāsa Bābājī#ficha-1920|Bhāgavata dāsa Bābājī]]**, who gave it to Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī.
- He left the world in 1894 (another text: 1895, Bengali 1302), on **Phālguna Śukla Pratipat** (Gauḍīya calendar). His bhajana-kuṭīra and samādhi are at Koladvīpa (Navadvīpa town).

## Other versions

- One text makes him a disciple of Jagadānanda Gosvāmī of Śṛṅgāravaṭa, with the bābājī dress from Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābā of Govardhana.
- The Paramparā names Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa above him because generations are skipped (Baladeva → Uddhava dāsa → Madhusūdana dāsa → Jagannātha dāsa).
