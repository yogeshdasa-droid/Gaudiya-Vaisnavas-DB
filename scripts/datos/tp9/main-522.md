## In short

**Jagadānanda dāsa Bābājī Mahāśaya** was a great paṇḍita among the Vaiṣṇavas of Vraja in the nineteenth century — "a paṇḍita like him had no equal in the Vaiṣṇava community of his time" — who lived at **Rādhākuṇḍa** and **Sūrya-kuṇḍa**. Probably born in a brāhmaṇa family of the **Faridpur** district (Bangladesh), at eighteen or nineteen he took bheka (veśa) from **Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī**, the mahānta of an ākhaḍā at **Samādiā** in the same district.

## Śāstra and the siddhas

Those who delight in the study and discussion of śāstra are different in their ways from those who prefer bhajana. Jagadānanda was close to **Siddha Jayakṛṣṇa Bābā of Kāmyavana**, **Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābā of Govardhana** and **Siddha Madhusūdana Bābā of Sūrya-kuṇḍa**, yet never felt drawn to the bhāva they showed. With Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa he often had mock quarrels in their talks; after Kṛṣṇadāsa's passing he said that he no longer found in iṣṭa-goṣṭhī with anyone the joy those quarrels had given him.

He was very strict about right conduct. Once a descendant of an ācārya came from Gauḍa-maṇḍala to hear Hari-kathā, and the two talked from evening to morning without a wink of sleep.

## Baḍa Prabhu of Katwa

Baḍa Prabhu of Katwa, of Uthali (Dhaka), a descendant of Advaita Prabhu, came to see him with a woman attendant. Jagadānanda invited him into his kuṭīra beside the samāja of Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī, but Baḍa Prabhu stayed outside: "I worship Kṛṣṇa, but I am not worthy to enter your kuṭīra, for I have not controlled my passions." Seeing his humility, Jagadānanda said: "Anyone who followed Baḍa Prabhu's ways would bring disaster on himself — and anyone who criticised him would be ruined just the same."

## The night walk

Before living at Rādhākuṇḍa he stayed for some time at Sūrya-kuṇḍa. One moonlit evening he set out from Rādhākuṇḍa for Sūrya-kuṇḍa; though he kept to the right path, it was dawn when he arrived. The Siddha Bābā of Sūrya-kuṇḍa, surprised, asked where he came from at such an hour, and the two sat down to discuss how Vraja-maṇḍala contracts and expands.

## His students

Many studied śāstra with him. One was **Advaita dāsa Paṇḍita**, a teacher of the *Hari-nāmāmṛta-vyākaraṇa* and a famous singer of the **Garāṇhāṭī** kīrtana introduced by Narottama Ṭhākura, from whom the author of these lines heard Jagadānanda's life.

## Sources

The record's texts, told by his student Advaita dāsa Paṇḍita. He stands in the Guru Paramparā under Siddha Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī of Kālnā; his text says only "Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī, mahānta of an ākhaḍā at Samādiā", so that line is marked as disputed. The Jagadānanda Bābājī of Jagadīśa dāsa Bābājī's record, a disciple of Siddha Nityānanda dāsa who died in Vṛndāvana, may be another person.
