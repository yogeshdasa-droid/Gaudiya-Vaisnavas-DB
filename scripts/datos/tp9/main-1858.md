## In short

**Rāmadāsa Bābājī** of Navadvīpa, born **Rādhikācaraṇa Gupta** at Konarpur (Faridpur), was the companion of **Prabhu Jagadbandhu** in his boyhood, the disciple of **Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa Deva**, and after his guru's passing the leader of his community: he saved the samādhi-temple of Haridāsa Ṭhākura in Purī, founded a home for the sick and old in Navadvīpa, and published his guru's life in six volumes, the *Carita-sudhā*.

## Childhood

His father, **Durgācaraṇa Gupta**, son of Rāmakānāi Gupta, lived at **Konarpur** (Pālaṅg police station, Faridpur), an excise inspector with a rented house in Faridpur; the family held great festivals and worshipped the Ananta-deva śālagrāma. Rādhikācaraṇa was born on **22 Caitra** (the text says 1896; see «Dates»), the eighth son of Durgācaraṇa and Satyabhāmā Devī. His nurse and her daughter Śyāmā lived with the family for years; the children honoured the nurse as a mother and held her śrāddha on a large scale. From childhood he loved music and would run to it and listen on Śyāmā's lap; at school he stood first every year and remembered everything after hearing it once. He could not bear suffering: when he saw the place of the animal sacrifice before the goddess covered with blood, he wept and fainted, and the sacrifices — and then the family's Durgā-pūjā — were stopped. He joined his brother Jatīndra Mohan's party of devotional singers, and once, in a storm, bravely pulled his uncle out of their fallen house.

## With Jagadbandhu

**Jagadbandhu**, then at Faridpur, was charmed when he heard Rādhikā sing and longed to meet him; Rādhikā met him and surrendered to him. After passing his primary examination he was sent to a Sanskrit school to study grammar, but went instead to sing with Prabhu Jagadbandhu. From then on he meditated after bathing and ate nothing not offered to the Lord. Jagadbandhu, who never spoke the name "Rādhikā", called him **Sārikā**, and in Vṛndāvana **Rāma** or **Rāmī**. He became Jagadbandhu's chief assistant in preaching the holy name — at a great kīrtana festival at Faridpur, and in Pabna — though his family disliked it. With him he went to Navadvīpa for an eclipse, when it overflowed with great devotees (Bijoy Kṛṣṇa Gosvāmī, Premānanda Bhāratī, Vrajavala, Kṛṣṇānanda Svāmī, Haribolānanda Svāmī); at a message from Jagadbandhu he left home for Vṛndāvana, and lived near the old Govinda temple and in the palace of the King of Chatrisgaḍh near Rādhābāg.

## Disciple and leader

At Cuttack **Śrī Rādhāramaṇa** (Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa Deva) initiated him in the Gaura-mantra and urged him to nāma-saṅkīrtana. After Rādhāramaṇa's death the whole community was in his care. He saved the temple of **Haridāsa Ṭhākura** in Purī from auction with 3,000 rupees and restored its service; set up in Navadvīpa a home where the old, orphans and the distressed were treated; held every year at **Pānihāṭi** the festival of Mahāprabhu's coming; took a house in Calcutta for preaching the name; and when the **Barāhanagara Pāṭabāḍī** came into his care, founded there a library of rare manuscripts, old pictures, sculptures and waters of the holy places. Dependence on the Lord and His name was the root of his preaching; he was humble and ready to help any distressed person, to revive an old temple or the worship of an old Deity.

## Dates

The record's dates do not agree with each other: it gives his birth as 1896, yet tells of him in 1889, 1891, 1893 and 1894; and it gives his guru's death and his works as 1805, 1807, 1811, 1814, 1821 and 1833 AD, impossible in his life. They must be misprints (the birth perhaps earlier; the later ones for the 1900s, or Bengali years).

## Sources

The record's texts. He stood in the Guru Paramparā under Rādhāramaṇa Gosvāmī, the 16th-century disciple of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa; his guru was Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa Deva, and he now stands under him (07-11-2026).
