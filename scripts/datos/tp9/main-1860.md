## In short

**Ramahari dāsa Bābājī** (born 1847 at Śāntipura) was a lover of Gaura-kīrtana and a scion of **Narahari Sarakāra's** family. He took shelter of **Siddha Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī** at Navadvīpa, who had him initiated by **Rādhikāvilāsa Ṭhākura** of Śrīkhaṇḍa and himself gave him veśa; he lived in Vraja — twelve years at Rādhākuṇḍa, then at Lālā Bābu's kuñja at Vaṁśīvaṭa — and left this world in meditation on a **Bhādra śukla caturthī**.

## Life

From childhood he was drawn to spiritual life and delighted in kīrtana. When it grew stronger his worried parents married him off, but he stayed absorbed in the holy name, and to escape worldly troubles he went to Navadvīpa for the mercy of the Vaiṣṇavas. There he met **Siddha Jagannātha dāsa** at the **Bhajana-kuṭīra** and fell at his feet. Seeing his eagerness, Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī arranged his initiation from **Śrī Rādhikāvilāsa Ṭhākura of Śrīkhaṇḍa**, who was then living at the Bhajana-kuṭīra, and then himself gave him veśa and the name **Ramahari dāsa**. As his family home was near and his relatives troubled him, he went to Vṛndāvana: twelve years at Kosi-ghāṭ, **Rādhākuṇḍa**, then the ṭhaurā of Keśī-ghāṭ, **Lālā Bābu's kuñja at Vaṁśīvaṭa**.

He loved everyone alike. He was especially fond of the *Caitanya-maṅgala* kīrtana and had his disciples read Locana dāsa's dhāmālī every day. As a scion of Narahari he was naturally drawn to Narahari, and anyone who said Gaura's name he embraced warmly. His favourites were the disciples of Siddha Jagannātha dāsa; he and **Rāmadāsa Bābājī** sang kīrtana together regularly. Anyone from Navadvīpa he welcomed with a garland and sandal paste, took on his lap and questioned about Gaurāṅga's Navadvīpa; if the answers did not satisfy him, he sent the newcomer back at once to see Nitāi-Gaura and Sītānātha and to keep the company of Gaura's devotees.

When **Rājarṣi Bahādur** was living at Śrī-kuṇḍa he too stayed at Lālā Bābu's kuñja. One day, after taking the prasāda of Śrī Rādhā-vinoda, Ramahari was overwhelmed and began to dance crying "Gaura, Gaura"; the old Mathurā dāsa Bābājī, Vrajavala Mahāśaya (Bijoy Kṛṣṇa Gosvāmī's disciple), Giridhārī dāsa Bābājī, Mādhava dāsa Bābājī and many others joined the dancing kīrtana.

## His departure

At noon on a Bhādra śukla caturthī he told his disciples: "Śrī Gaurāṅga and His associates are dancing in kīrtana in Śrīvāsa's courtyard; I must go there at once. Bathe me and put on my tilaka." When they had done it, he sat with closed eyes in meditation and left this world. The text gives the year as 1807 AD, surely a misprint, since he was born in 1847. His disciple was **Mādhava dāsa Bābājī**.
