## In short

Śrīpati Paṇḍita was a brother of **Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita** and a branch of the Caitanya tree. With his brothers Śrīvāsa, Śrīrāma and Śrīnidhi he lived in Navadvīpa and served Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu with his whole family (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.9; *Abhidhāna*, entry «Śrīpati»).

## Family

He was one of the five sons of **Jaladhara Paṇḍita**, a Vaidika brāhmaṇa of Śrīhaṭṭa who settled with his wife in Navadvīpa: Nalina, Śrīvāsa, Śrīrāma, Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi (*Prema-vilāsa* 23–24; *Abhidhāna*, entries «Jaladhara Paṇḍita» and «Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita»). The family had houses in Navadvīpa and at Kumārahaṭṭa. Nalina's daughter Nārāyaṇī was the mother of Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura.

## Life

"Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and Śrīrāma Paṇḍita, two brothers, two branches known to the world; **Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi their two brothers**; the four brothers with their servants and maidservants, in whose house the Lord always performed saṅkīrtana. The four brothers with their whole family serve Caitanya and know no other god" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.8–11).

Every day the four brothers sang the names of Kṛṣṇa, worshipped Him and bathed in the Gaṅgā three times, and their kīrtana annoyed the skeptics of Navadvīpa (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 11). After the Lord's sannyāsa they lived at Kumārahaṭṭa and went every year with Śrīvāsa to see the Lord in Purī. For the life of the family see the record of Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita.

The *Bhakti-ratnākara* (10) names a Śrīpati and a Śrīnidhi Paṇḍita among the mahāntas who came to the great festival of Kheturī; Vyāsācārya looked after the house where they stayed (*Abhidhāna*, entry «Vyāsācārya»). The book does not say whether they are Śrīvāsa's brothers.

## Other persons of the same name

Another Śrīpati was a disciple of Rasikānanda (*Rasika-maṅgala*, Paścima 14.160; *Abhidhāna*).
