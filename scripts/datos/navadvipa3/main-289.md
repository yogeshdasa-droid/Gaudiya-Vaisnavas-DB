## In short

Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita was the teacher of grammar of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in Navadvīpa and a branch of the Caitanya tree. "Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita was very dear to the Lord; by remembering him all bondage is destroyed" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.29). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Sāndīpani Muni**, Kṛṣṇa's teacher; Vasiṣṭha, the guru of Śrī Rāmacandra, is also present in him (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 53).

## The Lord's teacher

Gaṅgādāsa kept a ṭola (school) in Navadvīpa. "In the place of Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita the Lord studied grammar; by hearing it only once He learned by heart all the sūtras and their commentaries" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 15.5). Mukunda Datta studied in the same ṭola with Nimāi, and the two always argued about the śāstra (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 11.28–30; *Abhidhāna*, entry Mukunda Datta). Seeing the extraordinary intelligence of his pupil, Gaṅgādāsa was filled with joy (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 8.31–37).

The *Caitanya-maṅgala* of Locana dāsa names **Sudarśana Paṇḍita** as the Lord's other teacher: "With Sudarśana and Gaṅgādāsa, those paṇḍitas, the guru of the world studied, for their good" (*Caitanya-maṅgala*, Ādi, p. 64; *Abhidhāna*, entry Sudarśana); the Vaiṣṇava-vandanā bows to "the guru Viṣṇu, Gaṅgādāsa and Sudarśana" (*Nāmāmṛta-samudra* 61).

## After Gayā

When the Lord came back from Gayā He was full of a love never seen before, and when He explained the lessons He saw Kṛṣṇa in every word. Gaṅgādāsa heard of it, smiled, blessed Him and told Him how the books should be explained (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 1.120–284).

The Lord's meeting with **Nityānanda Prabhu** at the house of Gaṅgādāsa is told in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Madhya 8.25; so the record's list and the *Abhidhāna*). In the great revelation (mahā-prakāśa) the Lord reminded Gaṅgādāsa of how He had saved him from danger: once, fleeing at night with his family from the fear of the king, he found no boat at the ghāṭa, and the Lord, appearing as a boatman, took them across the Gaṅgā (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 9.109–120).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Sāndīpani Muni**, the teacher of Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma in Avantī; Vasiṣṭha Muni, the guru of Śrī Rāmacandra, is also included in him (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 53; *Abhidhāna*).

## Other persons of the same name

The *Abhidhāna* also has Gaṅgādāsa Datta and Gaṅgādāsa Rāya, disciples of Narottama Ṭhākura (*Prema-vilāsa* 20; *Narottama-vilāsa* 12), the Gaṅgādāsa disciple of Rasikānanda (*Rasika-maṅgala*, Paścima 15), Nirloma Gaṅgādāsa, and Baḍu Gaṅgādāsa: they are other persons, with their own records.
