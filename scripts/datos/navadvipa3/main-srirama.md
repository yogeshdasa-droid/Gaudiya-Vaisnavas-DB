## In short

Śrīrāma Paṇḍita — also called **Rāmāi** — was the younger brother of **Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita** and, with him, one of the great branches of the Caitanya tree: "Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and Śrīrāma Paṇḍita, two brothers, two branches known to the world" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.8). He was always with the Lord in kīrtana. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Parvata Muni**, the dear friend of Nārada (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 90).

## Family

He was one of the five sons of **Jaladhara Paṇḍita**, a Vaidika brāhmaṇa of Śrīhaṭṭa who settled with his wife in Navadvīpa: Nalina, Śrīvāsa, Śrīrāma, Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi (*Prema-vilāsa* 23–24; *Abhidhāna*, entries «Jaladhara Paṇḍita» and «Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita»). The family had houses in Navadvīpa and at Kumārahaṭṭa.

"Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi their two brothers; the four brothers with their servants and maidservants, in whose house the Lord always performed saṅkīrtana. The four brothers with their whole family serve Caitanya and know no other god than Gauracandra" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.8–11).

## Life

Every day the four brothers sang the names of Kṛṣṇa, worshipped Him and bathed in the Gaṅgā three times, and their kīrtana annoyed the skeptics of Navadvīpa (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 11).

- When the Lord revealed Himself to the devotees, He sent Rāmāi to Śāntipura to tell **Advaita Ācārya** (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 6.9–71).
- He went with the Lord singing kīrtana, and in the drama of Kṛṣṇa-līlā performed at the house of **Candraśekhara Ācārya** he took part with Śrīvāsa — Śrīvāsa as Nārada, Śrīrāma as his disciple, the snātaka (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 18.11–53).
- When the Lord came to Kumārahaṭṭa after His sannyāsa, He told Śrīrāma Paṇḍita to serve his elder brother (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5.66).

After the Lord's sannyāsa the brothers left Navadvīpa and lived at Kumārahaṭṭa, and every year they went with Śrīvāsa to see the Lord in Purī. For the life of the family see the record of Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Parvata Muni**, the dear friend of Nārada Muni (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 90).

## Other persons of the same name

The *Abhidhāna* distinguishes several Rāmāis: (1) Rāmāi, the servant of the Lord in Purī, Payoda in Kṛṣṇa-līlā, who with Nandāi helped Govinda (his own record); (2) this Śrīrāma Paṇḍita; (3) the blind Rāmāi, disciple of Vīrabhadra Gosvāmī, who got his sight at the festival of Narahari Sarakāra's disappearance in Śrīkhaṇḍa (*Prema-vilāsa* 19); (4) Rāmāi Gosāi (Rāmacandra), dear to Jāhnavā Mātā, who brought the Deities of Kānāi-Balāi to Bengal. Another Śrīrāma Paṇḍita was a branch of Advaita Ācārya: "Vijaya Paṇḍita and Śrīrāma Paṇḍita" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.65).
