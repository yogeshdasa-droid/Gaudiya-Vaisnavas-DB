## In short

Rāmāi was a servant of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in Jagannātha Purī and a branch of the Caitanya tree. With **Nandāi** and **Govinda** he did all the work of the Lord's house (*Abhidhāna*, entry «Rāmāi»). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Payoda**, a servant of Kṛṣṇa (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 139).

## Service

"Rāmāi and Nandāi, both servants of the Lord, always served together with Govinda. Every day Rāmāi filled twenty-two big pots of water; Nandāi served by the order of Govinda" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.143–144). They were the twentieth and twenty-first among the Lord's devotees in Purī.

In the *Śrī Caitanya-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* attributed to Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja (manuscript in the Orissa State Museum, catalogue no. L 470 {B}) Rāmāi is named with other followers such as Siṁha Bhaṭṭa, Kamala Bhaṭṭa and Nandāi.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Payoda**, a servant of Kṛṣṇa (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 139).

## Other persons of the same name

He is not Śrīrāma Paṇḍita (Rāmāi), Śrīvāsa's younger brother, whose text was in this record before and now has its own record; nor the blind Rāmāi, disciple of Vīrabhadra, nor Rāmāi Gosāi (Rāmacandra), dear to Jāhnavā Mātā (*Abhidhāna*, entry «Rāmāi»).
