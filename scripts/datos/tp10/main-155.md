## In short

Śrīla **Bhakti Bibudha Bodhāyana Mahārāja** is the president and ācārya of the **Śrī Gopīnātha Gauḍīya Maṭha**, where he succeeded his spiritual master, Śrīla **Bhakti Pramoda Purī Gosvāmī Ṭhākura**. He was born **Asīm Dās** on **21 August 1964** in the village of **Kanpur**, West Bengal.

## Family and childhood

His grandfather, Śrīpāda **Madana-mohana Dās Adhikārī**, a direct disciple of Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura, led the whole family to Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavism. As a child he saw sādhus of the Gauḍīya Maṭha come regularly to visit his grandfather — among them his future guru, Bhakti Pramoda Purī Gosvāmī. Every year the family went to Śrī Māyāpur for the festivals of Śrīla Prabhupāda and Mahāprabhu, and these visits gave him a deep wish for a holy life, though his parents insisted he first finish his studies.

## Brahmacārī and sannyāsī

While writing his final school examinations he received the **Nṛsiṁha mantra** from his guru. He took **harināma** in **1986** and was named **Acyutānanda Dās**. In **1990** he joined his guru's new institution, the **Gopīnātha Gauḍīya Maṭha**, and took on much of its management: he helped open branch maṭhas at **Cakra-tīrtha** in Jagannātha Purī and in **Vṛndāvana**, and to publish its monthly journal **Bhāgavata-dharma**, to which he also wrote. Pleased with him, his guru gave him **sannyāsa** on **Gaura-pūrṇimā 1993**, with the name **Tridaṇḍi Bhikṣu Bhakti Bibudha Bodhāyana**.

On Gaura-pūrṇimā **1997** Bhakti Pramoda Purī Gosvāmī, then ninety-eight, suddenly announced that Bodhāyana Mahārāja would be his successor as president and ācārya of the maṭha; two years later, at his guru's departure, he took over those duties. Since then he has taught the message of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in the footsteps of his guru and of Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī, travelling to speak all over the world.

## His writings in this record

The record's long text gathers his lectures and articles: a Bengali poem and translation, *Śrī Śrī Guru-pāda-padmera Praśasti* and *Śrī Guru-caritāmṛta*, in praise of his guru on his hundredth appearance day (born 1898 at Gaṅgānandapura, Jessore; Promode Bhūṣaṇa, then Praṇavānanda Brahmacārī…); *Does taking a śikṣā guru mean rejecting one's dīkṣā guru?* (on Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura and Vipina-vihārī Gosvāmī, and on Śyāmānanda and Hṛdaya Caitanya); *Pretentious devotion*; *Surrender to Kṛṣṇa gives life*; *The story of Ekalavya*; *A dangerous example*; *Pure devotion and competitive spirit*; *The real guru and the real śiṣya*; *Love and mutual understanding*.
