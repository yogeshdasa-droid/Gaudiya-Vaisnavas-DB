## In short

**Śrīpāda Bhakti Bhāvana Viṣṇu Mahārāja** (B. B. Viṣṇu Mahārāja), born in the United States, was initiated by Śrīla **A. C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda** as **Bhavānanda Rāya dāsa Brahmacārī**, received sannyāsa from **B. G. Narasiṁha Mahārāja** and his name and title from Śrīla **Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī**. He lives at the **Śrī Narasiṁha Caitanya Maṭha** in Mysore and compiled the book *Our Affectionate Guardians*.

## Life

In 1970 he graduated from Clarkson College of Technology with a Master of Science in physics. In **1975** he met Śrīla Prabhupāda in **Los Angeles** and surrendered to him. He studied the *Bhagavad-gītā* and *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* closely, received harināma from Prabhupāda after a few months, and soon after brāhmaṇa initiation. He became known as a preacher in saṅkīrtana (book distribution), leading travelling parties of brahmacārīs to Las Vegas and many other cities.

After Prabhupāda's departure in November 1977 he went on preaching in the western United States, and from 1981 at universities in Chicago. In **1982** he and **B. G. Narasiṁha Mahārāja** began the **Museum of Vedic Culture**, travelling for five years across the country, preaching at cultural festivals and distributing books to the Indian community, with many pilgrimages to India. In **1986**, in Hawaii, on Gaura-pūrṇimā, he received sannyāsa and the title Svāmī from Narasiṁha Mahārāja.

At Gaura-pūrṇimā **1987** he went to Navadvīpa and stayed a month at the feet of Śrīla **Śrīdhara Mahārāja**, hearing his instructions; in May of the following year Śrīdhara Mahārāja gave him the name **Bhakti Bhāvana Viṣṇu**. After Śrīdhara Mahārāja's departure in August 1988 he went to Vṛndāvana with Narasiṁha Mahārāja to found the **Rūpānuga Bhajana Āśrama**; a centre opened in Trivandrum and the **Śrī Narasiṁha Caitanya Maṭha** at Mysore (Śrīraṅgapatna).

Humble and affectionate to the Vaiṣṇavas, firm in guru-niṣṭhā, he was associate and Sanskrit editor of the *Clarion Call* and *Gauḍīya Vedānta* magazines, associate editor of *Śaraṇāgati* online and Gosāi Publishers, and wrote many articles and websites. When the text was written he was fifty-four, living at the Narasiṁha Caitanya Maṭha and visiting the United States and Europe.
