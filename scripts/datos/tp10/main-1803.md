## In short

**Tridaṇḍi Svāmī Śrīla Bhaktiratna Sādhu Mahārāja "Gaurāṅgapada"** preaches the glories of **Śrī Nityānanda-Gaurāṅga** and their names, mainly by writing, translating and publishing books. He was born on **22 November 1971** at **Ahmedabad** (Gujarat) in a Gujarati (Audīcya-sahasra) brāhmaṇa family. His śikṣā-guru is Śrīla A. C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda; his dīkṣā-guru, **Bhaktiśāstrī Parampada Prabhu**; his sannyāsa-guru, **Bhakti Kumuda Santa Gosvāmī** — both of them disciples of Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura.

## Life

He grew up at Matunga, Mumbai, where his parents' daily kīrtana and bhajana of Kṛṣṇa nourished his inclination from childhood. As a student of the five-year integrated M.Sc. in chemistry at the **IIT Powai**, he found in its library Śrīla Prabhupāda's *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*; reading it he learned to read Bengali and from the first day chanted thirty-two rounds of the mahā-mantra, and felt a strong spontaneous attraction to Śrī Gaurāṅga. With the devotees who came to IIT he inspired many other students, who have since given their lives to Kṛṣṇa consciousness.

After his degree he joined ISKCON and led brahmacārīs in book distribution — 600 sets of the Gujarati *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* (12 volumes) in a week, 3,500 Marathi *Gītās* in a day at Pandharpur — and was for some years second in the world. As production manager of the **Bhaktivedanta Book Trust** in Mumbai for more than five years, he nearly tripled its production. He studied the books of Prabhupāda and the previous ācāryas, and spoke in English, Hindi and Gujarati, also on the television series *Ātmā*.

Meditating on Prabhupāda's instruction to take sannyāsa for preaching, with the blessing of his dīkṣā-guru (then 101 years old) he accepted sannyāsa from Bhakti Kumuda Santa Mahārāja (then 89) at **Behala**, Calcutta. He preaches in Mumbai through house programmes, *Caitanya-bhāgavata* saptāhas and study groups, wanting not temples or funds but to spread the glories of Nitāi-Gaura, and has founded the **Gaurāṅga Book Fund** to publish books at cost price. He spreads the chanting of the **Nityānanda** mantra and the four-syllable Gaura-gopāla mantra **"Gaurāṅga"** with the mahā-mantra ("Gaurāṅga-mantra-dīkṣā"), and took the title **Gaurāṅgapada** so that people would chant the Lord's name when addressing him.

## Books

*Caitanya-bhāgavata* in English (400 pp.); a condensed *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* (500 pp.); *Mahā-mantra Yoga — the Complete Science of Chanting*; *Our Eternal Home — Navadvīpa*; *The Top 100 Names of God*; lectures on MP3 and video CD.
