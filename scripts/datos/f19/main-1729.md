## In short

**Viṭṭhalanātha** («Viṭṭhaleśvara», died 1508 Śaka, 1585), the second son of **Vallabhācārya** and head of the **Vallabha sampradāya**, also worshipped Lord Gaurāṅga. After the two Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas to whom Mādhavendra Purī had left the service of **Śrī Gopāla** of Govardhana passed away, he was made the Deity's priest in consultation with **Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī** and others (Bhakti-ratnākara 5.815). He served Gopāla at **Gāṭhuli**; that Gopāla is now Śrī Nāthajī of Nāthadvāra.

## Śrī Gopāla

The Caitanya-caritāmṛta (Madhya 4) tells how Mādhavendra Purī revealed and installed Gopāla on top of Govardhana; how Lord Caitanya wished to see Him but would not climb the hill, so Gopāla came down to give Him darśana; and how Mādhavendra first served Him himself and then handed the service to two Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas. When they died, Viṭṭhaleśvara was appointed. His name is in Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī's *Śrī Gopāla-stava-rāja* (Stavāvalī, 13-14) and in Viśvanātha Cakravartī's *Śrī Gopāla-devāṣṭaka* (7).

To escape the wrath of the Muslim rulers, Gopāla was kept hidden for a month in Viṭṭhalanātha's house at Gāṭhuli, and many devotees came there for His darśana (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 18.47). The opulent worship of Gopāla at Nāthadvāra is found nowhere else in India.

## With the Gosvāmīs

When Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī suffered from indigestion, Viṭṭhaleśvara called two physicians to treat him (Bhakti-ratnākara 5.577); when **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya** came to Gāṭhuli on his pilgrimage, Viṭṭhalanātha welcomed him warmly (Bhakti-ratnākara 5.804).

## His works

A commentary on the *Premāmṛta-rasāyana* ascribed to Lord Caitanya; the **Vidvan-maṇḍana**; and, for his own sampradāya, the **Brahma-sūtrāṇubhāṣya-pūrti**, **Vivṛti-prakāśa**, **Nibandha-prakāśa-pūrti** and **Śṛṅgāra-rasa-maṇḍana**.
