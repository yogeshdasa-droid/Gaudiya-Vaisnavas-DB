## In short

Śrī **Tapana Miśra** was the father of **Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī** and one of the Lord's three devotees in **Vārāṇasī**: "In Vārāṇasī the Lord had three devotees: Candraśekhara Vaidya, Tapana Miśra, and Raghunātha Bhaṭṭācārya, Miśra's son" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.152-153). He was a branch of Lord Caitanya.

## The dream in East Bengal

He was a brāhmaṇa of a village on the bank of the Ganges (the Padmā) in East Bengal — Rāmapura, says the Abhidhāna — who studied many scriptures but could not decide what is the goal of life and the means to reach it: "In that country lived a brāhmaṇa named Tapana Miśra, who could not ascertain the goal and the process; with many scriptures and many statements his mind was bewildered" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 16.10-11). When Nimāi Paṇḍita was touring East Bengal, Tapana Miśra dreamt early one morning that a divine person told him to go to Nimāi Paṇḍita, who was the Supreme Lord Himself and would teach him sādhya-sādhana; he woke up weeping. He found the Lord and surrendered at His feet, and the Lord taught him that in Kali-yuga the way is the saṅkīrtana of the holy name. When Tapana asked to go with Him to Navadvīpa, the Lord told him to go at once to Vārāṇasī, where they would meet again, and embraced him; Tapana fainted in ecstasy (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 14; Murāri Gupta 4.1.15).

## Vārāṇasī

Tapana Miśra went with his family to Kāśī. Years later, on His way to Vṛndāvana, the Lord came to Vārāṇasī; Tapana saw Him bathing at Maṇikarṇikā, was amazed — he had known Him in his own country in another dress — and the Lord embraced him (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 17.83-100). On both visits, and during the two months the Lord spent in Kāśī on His way back, He took His meals in Tapana Miśra's house and stayed with **Candraśekhara Vaidya**. It was by Tapana Miśra's efforts that the Lord showed His mercy to the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs of Kāśī; he was at the Bindu-mādhava temple when the Lord delivered **Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī** and the others.

His son **Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa** became one of the six Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana (Abhidhāna).
