## In short

**Bijalī Khān** was a young Pathān prince who met Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu on the road from Vṛndāvana to Prayāga and, with his soldiers, became a Vaiṣṇava: "They became famous as the Pathān Vaiṣṇavas … that Bijalī Khān became a mahā-bhāgavata, and his greatness was known at all the holy places" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 18.210-212). The learned Muslim teacher of his party was named **Rāmadāsa** by the Lord.

## On the road to Prayāga

After visiting Vṛndāvana the Lord crossed the Yamunā with His companions and was going through the forest towards Prayāga. Resting under a tree, He heard a cowherd boy playing the flute, fell unconscious in ecstatic love and lay with foam at His mouth and His breath stopped (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 18.161-162). Just then ten Pathān horsemen came by with their prince, Bijalī Khān. Seeing a sannyāsī of extraordinary beauty lying unconscious with four or five men around him, they thought these men had drugged him with dhuturā to rob him, got down, tied the Lord's companions and were about to cut them down with their swords. The Bengalis (Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya and the others) were very afraid, but the Mathurā brāhmaṇa was not: "We are the guardians of this sannyāsī." When the Pathāns were not satisfied, **Kṛṣṇadāsa Rājput** said: "My house is in this village; I have two hundred Turks and a hundred cannons. If I call, they will come at once, plunder your horses and saddles and kill you all" (Madhya 18.173-174). The Pathāns untied the devotees.

## The teacher becomes Rāmadāsa

When the Lord came back to external consciousness and began to chant the holy name loudly, the Pathāns asked Him whether these men were thieves. "No, they are My companions. I have a disease and sometimes fall unconscious; they look after Me." Charmed by the sight of Him and His words, they began to talk of scripture. Their leader, dressed as a Muslim saint and learned in the Koran, asked about the impersonal and personal views; the Lord answered from the Koran itself that the Lord is full of all opulence, His complexion dark, and that the only way to be freed from this world is to serve Him and worship His lotus feet (Madhya 18.177-190). The teacher said: "Seeing You, my tongue says Kṛṣṇa's name; my pride in my learning is gone. Please tell me the goal and the means," and fell at His feet. "Get up; you have taken the name of Kṛṣṇa: the sins of millions of births are gone and you are pure. Say Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa!" (Madhya 18.203-206). The Lord gave him the holy name and named him **Rāmadāsa**.

## The Pathān Vaiṣṇavas

Seeing this, **Bijalī Khān** too fell at the Lord's feet saying "Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa," and the Lord instructed him; all the Pathāns became renounced Vaiṣṇavas, known everywhere as the "Pathān Vaiṣṇavas", travelling and singing the glories of the Lord, and Bijalī Khān became a great devotee, honoured at all the holy places (Madhya 18.201-212; Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna, «Rāmadāsa Pāṭhāna»).
