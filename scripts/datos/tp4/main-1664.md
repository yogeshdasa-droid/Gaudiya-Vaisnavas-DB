## In short

Śrī **Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa** (Vyeṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa) of **Śrīraṅgam**, a Vaiṣṇava of the Śrī-sampradāya of Rāmānujācārya, was the host of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu for the four months of the rainy season (Cāturmāsya) during the Lord's pilgrimage in the South, and the father of **Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī**; his brothers were **Trimalla Bhaṭṭa** and **Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 9.82-86).

## Four months with the Lord

At his earnest request the Lord stayed in his house. He worshipped **Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa**, and seeing his pure devotion the Lord was very pleased; living together day after day they became friends and sometimes laughed and joked together.

## Lakṣmī and the rāsa dance

Once the Lord said: "Your worshipable Lakṣmī always stays on Nārāyaṇa's chest and is the most chaste woman in creation; My Lord is Kṛṣṇa, a cowherd boy tending cows. Why does such a chaste wife want to associate with My Lord?" Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa answered that Kṛṣṇa and Nārāyaṇa are one, Kṛṣṇa's pastimes only more relishable, so her chastity was not broken: "If she wanted to enjoy with Kṛṣṇa, what fault is there?" The Lord went on: "I know there is no fault in her, but why could she not enter the rāsa dance, when the authorities of the Vedas (the śrutis) could?" — "I cannot enter into this mystery; You are Kṛṣṇa Himself, and only the one You enlighten can understand." Then the Lord explained: "Whoever worships the Lord following the residents of Vraja attains Him in Vraja as the son of Nanda Mahārāja. The personified Vedas worshipped Kṛṣṇa in the mood of the gopīs, took bodies like theirs in Vraja and entered the rāsa. Lakṣmī wanted Kṛṣṇa while keeping her own form as Lakṣmī, and did not follow the gopīs."

Until then Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa had thought that Nārāyaṇa is the Supreme Lord and His worship the highest, as the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas hold; the Lord, seeing it, spoke so much in jest to correct it: "Do not doubt any more: Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Personality of Godhead; this is the conclusion of the Vedas." Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa said: "I am an ordinary fallen soul and You are Kṛṣṇa Himself. It is by the mercy of Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa, whom I have served, that I have seen Your feet; now I understand that devotional service to Kṛṣṇa is the highest worship," and fell at the Lord's feet, and the Lord embraced him. After the Lord's company he worshipped **Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 9.158-159).

When Cāturmāsya was over the Lord took leave. Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa wanted to leave his home and go with Him, and the Lord could send him back only with great effort; at the parting he fell unconscious.

## Family

His son was **Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī**, one of the six Gosvāmīs of Vṛndāvana; his brother Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī was Gopāla Bhaṭṭa's guru (in the Guru Paramparā of this library).
