## In short

**Rasikānanda**, a Vaiṣṇava poet, wrote pada **2227** of the **Padakalpataru**, on Lord Gaurāṅga's acceptance of sannyāsa. He may be Rasikānanda Prabhu (#1908); the text does not say.
