## In short

**Haridāsa**, a padakartā, has six songs in the **Padakalpataru**; the finest is no. **3014**: «nācite nā jāni tabu nāciye gaurāṅga bali'» — «I do not know how to dance, yet I dance saying Gaurāṅga; I do not know how to sing, yet I sing» (Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna, «Haridāsa» 4).
