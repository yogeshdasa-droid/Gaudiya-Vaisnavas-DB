## In short

**Kavicandra**, son of **Kavi Karṇapūra** and a resident of **Khaṇḍaghoṣa**, translated the **Gīta-govinda** into Bengali verse.

## Sources

The record's text. Two other Kavicandras once in this record — the one of Lord Caitanya's branch and the one who taught Rasikānanda as a child (#2104) — have their own records.
