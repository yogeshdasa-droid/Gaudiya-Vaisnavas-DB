## In short

**Anaṅgabhīmadeva II**, king of Orissa of the Gaṅgā dynasty, completed the present **temple of Lord Jagannātha** at Purī, begun by King Coḍagaṅgadeva on the ruins of Indradyumna's temple, and built its rampart and the temples of **Vimalā-devī** and **Lakṣmī-devī**; the stone edict behind the jewelled altar dates the completion to **1119 Śaka (1197)**. He also gave many land grants for Jagannātha's worship and festivals.

## The temple

The temple of Lord Jagannātha first built by Indradyumna had fallen into ruin, and in 1078 King **Coḍagaṅgadeva** set out to build a new one on the ruins and partly completed it; Anaṅgabhīmadeva finished the work. The *Gaṅgā-vaṁśānucarita* records the same.

Before building these temples he installed a Deity of **Nārāyaṇa** (Śubha-lakṣmī-nārāyaṇa) in the Nārāyaṇa-chātā maṭha near the Baḍa-daṇḍa, standing to the north-east of the gateway to ward off unforeseen disaster.
