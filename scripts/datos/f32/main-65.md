## In short

**Āula Manohara dāsa**, a saint who lived long after Lord Caitanya, had many disciples and is honoured with an annual festival on **Makara-saṅkrānti** at **Bāḍaṅgañj** (Hooghly). It is said he was a mantra-disciple of **Jāhnavā Mātā** and a childhood friend of **Jñānadāsa** of Kāndrā, and that he moved **Vipra Paraśurāma** to take veśa.

## Life

He lived a long life. It is said that on 17 Pauṣa 1657 Śaka (about 2 January 1735) he left Bāḍaṅgañj for Vṛndāvana. His remains were enshrined in three places: Bāḍaṅgañj, near Goghāṭ police station (Ārāmbāg, Hooghly); Gokulnagar, next to Jayapura, six miles from Viṣṇupura (Bankura); and Sonāmukhī (Bankura). He had many disciples and visited the village schools regularly to teach religion to the pupils. He and Jñānadāsa of Kāndrā were friends from childhood and lived together until Jñānadāsa's death.

It is said that he was a mantra-śiṣya of Mā Jāhnavā; it is not certain whether this Manohara dāsa compiled the *Pada-samudra*.
