## In short

A **woman of Orissa**, whose name is not known, unable to see Lord Jagannātha for the crowd, climbed the **Garuḍa-stambha** in the temple at Purī — putting her foot on Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu's shoulder. The Lord stopped Govinda from pulling her down and praised her eagerness: "Jagannātha has not given Me so much eagerness" (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 14.22-30).

## At the Garuḍa column

While He lived at Purī, Lord Caitanya went daily at the usual time to see Jagannātha, standing behind the Garuḍa column while thousands of people in front of Him saw the Deity. One day this woman, unable to see because of the crowd, climbed the column, placing her foot on the Lord's shoulder. His servant **Govinda** hastily got her down, but Śrī Caitanya chastised him: "O ādi-vasyā (uncivilized man), do not forbid this woman to climb the Garuḍa-stambha. Let her see Lord Jagannātha to her satisfaction."

When she came to her senses she quickly got down and, seeing Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, begged forgiveness at His lotus feet. Seeing her eagerness, the Lord said: "Lord Jagannātha has not given so much eagerness to Me. She has absorbed her body, mind and life in Jagannātha, and so did not know she was putting her foot on My shoulder. Alas! How fortunate this woman is! I pray at her feet that she favour Me with her eagerness to see Lord Jagannātha."
