## In short

**Makaradhvaja** was **Sukeśī** in Vraja (Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā 168). He may be one of the other Makaradhvajas — Makaradhvaja Kara (#2165) or Makaradhvaja Paṇḍita, that is Gopāla Guru (#344) —; the book gives them apart.
