## In short

**Jagannātha dāsa**, called **«Atibaḍī»**, a brāhmaṇa of **Kapileśvara** (Purī district), son of **Bhagavān Paṇḍā** and **Pārvatī-devī**, born on the **śukla aṣṭamī of Bhādra**, translated the **Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam** into Oṛiyā in the *navākṣara* metre. Because the translation contained some adverse remarks on bhakti-tattva, Lord Caitanya, displeased, told him: "You seem to think yourself above the munis and ṛṣis, since you criticize their views" — and from then on he was called Atibaḍī («too great»), and his followers call themselves the **Atibaḍī sampradāya**.

## Life and works

He passed away at sixty. The *Brahmāṇḍa-bhūgola*, *Prema-sādhana* and *Dūtibodha* are also said to be his.
