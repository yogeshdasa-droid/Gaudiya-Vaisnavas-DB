## In short

**Kānāiyā Vipra**, a Vrajavāsī loved by the Vaiṣṇavas of Vraja, always lived near **Sanātana Gosvāmī**; his mother was devoted to **Rūpa and Sanātana**. When **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya** left Vṛndāvana with the Gosvāmīs' books, Kānāiyā embraced him in tears (Bhakti-ratnākara 3.373, 386-387). After Rūpa and Sanātana passed away, unable to bear the separation, he resolved to give up his life.

## His mother's house

Kānāiyā's mother had great affection for Rūpa and Sanātana: she often invited them for bhikṣā and regularly sent flowers, sandal-paste and other things to their kuṭīras. It is said that one day Sanātana came to beg at her door when no one was at home, and the Supreme Lord Himself, in the form of Kānāi, gave him bhikṣā.
