## In short

**Abhirāma dāsa** is named as the author of two pairs of works: the **Govinda-vijaya** and **Kṛṣṇa-maṅgala** (Baṅgīya Sāhitya Sevaka), and the **Pāṭa-paryaṭana** and **Abhirāma Ṭhākurera Śākhā-nirṇaya**, the booklets on the śrīpāṭas and on the branch of Abhirāma Ṭhākura. He is not Abhirāma Ṭhākura (Abhirāma Gosvāmī) himself.

## The Pāṭa-paryaṭana

Of himself he says only: «Remembering the lotus feet of Śrī Ratneśvara, Abhirāma dāsa wrote this in brief.» He summarised the book *Pāṭa-nirṇaya* and made it the base of his *Pāṭa-paryaṭana*: «I have written this summary taking information from the book Pāṭa-nirṇaya, which covers the same subject in great detail. Thus ends the book Pāṭa-paryaṭana compiled by Abhirāma dāsa.» The *Pāṭa-nirṇaya* is still unpublished as a book, though Ambikācaraṇa Brahmacārī printed it in the *Sāhitya-pariṣat Patrikā*; a study of it would reveal much about Vaiṣṇava temples and devotees.
