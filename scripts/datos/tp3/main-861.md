## In short

Śrī **Mādhava Ghoṣa**, the brother of Vāsudeva and Govinda Ghoṣa, was the great kīrtana singer of Mahāprabhu and Nityānanda. He is a branch of Lord Caitanya (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.115) and was later counted in Nityānanda's branch too: "Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa was a principal performer of kīrtana; Nityānanda Prabhu danced to his singing" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.18). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Rasollāsā**, one of the sakhīs who sang Viśākhā's songs (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 188).

## The singer

Śrīla Prabhupāda writes that Mādhava Ghoṣa "was expert in performing kīrtana; no one within this world could compete with him. He was known as the singer of Vṛndāvana and was very dear to Nityānanda Prabhu." With his brothers — uttara-rāḍhīya Kāyasthas whose father had lived at Kumārahaṭṭa before they came to Navadvīpa — he sang before the Lord, and Caitanya and Nityānanda danced; at the Ratha-yātrā of Purī the three brothers sang in the party led by Govinda Ghoṣa, with Vakreśvara Paṇḍita dancing (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 13.42-43). Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura says the three are expansions of Śrīmatī Rādhikā's body, in mādhurya-rasa. Twelve padas of his are known (Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna).

## With Nityānanda in Bengal

"By the Lord's order Nityānanda went to Gauḍa, and three went with Him: Rāmadāsa, Mādhava and Vāsudeva Ghoṣa; Govinda remained with the Lord" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.117-118). At the temple of **Gadādhara dāsa** at Eṛiyādaha he sang Kṛṣṇa's **dāna-līlā** while Nityānanda danced holding the Deity of Bāla-Gopāla (in Gadādhara dāsa's record). Every year he came with his brothers to Purī for the Ratha-yātrā.

## Dāiṅhāṭ

Later the brothers lived apart, and Mādhava's Śrīpāṭa is said to be at **Dāiṅhāṭ**; but the Abhidhāna notes that no trace of him remains there, and the place is known as the Śrīpāṭa of Mukunda Ghoṣa.

## Sources

Most of the record's texts tell of all three brothers: Govinda's Gopīnātha and Vāsudeva's songs and Deity at Tamluk are in their own records.
