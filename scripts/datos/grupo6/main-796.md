## In short

**Kṛṣṇaprasāda dāsa Bābājī** («Guḍhiḍi Bābā»), the veśa-disciple of **Siddha Nityānanda dāsa Bābājī** of Madana-mohana Ṭhaurā, served for twenty years the family of **Gallujī Mahārāja**, the most devoted of the priests of Rādhā-ramaṇa, by his guru's order.

## Life

After dīkṣā he asked how to live; his guru said: "You are simple, not fit to study śāstra or speak on rasa-tattva: go and serve Gallujī; you will attain everything by serving him." He served Gallujī, his family, the servants and the animals, always carrying some child, so that he chanted aloud standing. When Gallujī died he lived two or three years on mādhukarī at the Gopāla temple of Rasagherā, facing Rādhā-ramaṇa. He was called "Guḍhiḍi Bābā" because he dragged a torn quilt behind him so that his footprints would be wiped out and no one could take their dust. Later he lived at Puchri near Govinda-kuṇḍa (Govardhana) and at Kāmyavana; he lay prostrate before every Vaiṣṇava and would not rise until the other had (the record).
