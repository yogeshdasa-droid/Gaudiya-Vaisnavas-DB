## In short

**Siddha Madhusūdana dāsa Bābājī** of **Sūrya-kuṇḍa** in Vraja gave the **bābājī veśa** to **Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī**. A kulīna boy who fled to Vṛndāvana on his wedding night, he received a mantra by the Yamunā from an unknown mahānta **of Gaṅgāmātā's line**; Śrī Rādhā herself saved him at Rādhā-kuṇḍa and told him to live at Sūrya-kuṇḍa (the record).

## Life

**Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābā** of Govardhana and **Siddha Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa** of Kāmyavana would not teach him without knowing his guru's name; at Rādhā-kuṇḍa Rādhā saved him, told him to live at Sūrya-kuṇḍa and never to give that mantra to anyone. Ill, he was healed by Rādhā in the form of a girl of Vraja, and became known as "Siddha". He taught many but gave no dīkṣā. His samādhi is at Sūrya-kuṇḍa. Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī's record places him in the line of Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa through Uddhava dāsa.

## Appearance and disappearance

**Disappearance:** Mārgaśīrṣa śukla aṣṭamī, at Sūrya-kuṇḍa (Gauḍīya calendar).

## Sources

The record's text and the records of Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī and Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura.
