## In short

**Siddha Gauracaraṇa dāsa Bābājī** of **Dāujī** (Mahāvana), born at Talakhāḍi (Jessore) in a family that claimed descent from Lokanātha Gosvāmī's, took shelter of **Siddha Caitanya dāsa Bābājī** of Navadvīpa (some say of **Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī** of Kālnā), and did bhajana for twenty years in a cave near Baladeva's temple at Dāujī, in the mood of a friend (sakhya).

## Life

Inspired as a boy by the glories of Lokanātha and Narottama, he left home for Navadvīpa and stayed long with Caitanya dāsa, who let him go to Vraja with a warning: "if you become attached you will never come back". At Dāujī, Baladeva told him in a dream to stay in a nearby cave; when he tried to leave Vraja at dawn he found he had been walking round Baladeva-kuṇḍa all night. He called himself Śrīdāmā's younger brother and Rādhā's elder brother, chanted on a very long mālā, and went out only for mādhukarī; it is said Dāujī fed him butter and sugar. A low-caste Bengali waited a year at his cave; Gauracaraṇa let him in on finding an old *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* tied to his waist, heard it from him for a year weeping, and gave him dīkṣā and veśa as **Dayāla dāsa**, his first disciple. Absorbed in Gaura, he left the name of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa for "Śrī Gaurāṅga Nityānanda Śrī Advaitacandra, Gadādhara Śrīvāsādi Gaura-bhakta-vṛnda" (the record). **Rāmakṛṣṇa dāsa Bābājī** learnt music from him at Varṣāṇā.

## Disciples

Dayāla dāsa; a Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī (the record does not say whether he is the great Jagannātha dāsa of Navadvīpa); Kailāsa dāsa.
