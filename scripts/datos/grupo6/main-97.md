## In short

**Siddha Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī** of **Kālnā** (Ambikā-Kālnā), a disciple of **Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī** of Govardhana, came from Orissa but always spoke Bengali. He set up the worship of **Nāma-brahma** at Kālnā, which still goes on. Reserved, he rarely spoke of rāgānugā bhajana, even to his disciple **Jagadīśa dāsa**; his disciples also included **Viṣṇu dāsa** and **Prāṇakṛṣṇa dāsa**.

## Stories

When the pūjārī of Nāma-brahma ran away with the ornaments he would not call the police: "Nāma-brahma did not want to wear them"; months later the man came back with them, and he said the deity had changed His mind "like a *phakke*". He had a poor wood-seller paid double, then kept vendors out of his sight; he sent away a devotee who had thrown out a snake from the āśrama, and would not eat until the snake had its share. A cat shared his prasāda when bhajana kept him up late. He called the ecstatic behaviour of Caitanya dāsa Bābājī of Navadvīpa *phakke* (frivolous). He did not let prasāda touch an unwashed floor, and touched flowers offered to him to his head with care (the record).

## Appearance and disappearance

**Disappearance:** Kārtika śukla aṣṭamī.
