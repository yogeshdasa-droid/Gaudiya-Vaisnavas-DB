## In short

**Siddha Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa Bābājī** of **Kāmyavana** first introduced the tradition of **rāgānugā bhajana** practised today by the renunciants and householders of Vraja. By paramparā he belonged to the line of **Gaṅgāmātā Gosvāminī**; he took dīkṣā in the mantra of Lord Caitanya. Siddha **Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī** of Govardhana and Siddha **Madhusūdana dāsa** of Sūrya-kuṇḍa were loyal to him.

## Life

He was a close friend of Mahānta **Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī** of Rāmadiyā (Faridpur), the veśa-guru of the great Jagadānanda dāsa Paṇḍita Bābājī of Rādhā-kuṇḍa (Jagadīśa Bhaṭṭācārya Sārvabhauma); when they parted after a month together both fell unconscious. While he did bhajana at Bicellivāsa in Kāmyavana, **Navakiśora Gosvāmī**, of Nityānanda's family, came with the deities of **Rādhā-Madanamohana**; in a dream they told Navakiśora that they would now take the Bābājī's service, and he served them from then on. In the *Bhāgavatam* sessions even the tuft of his hair stood on end; a roar of love from him cracked the roof of his kuṭīra, still to be seen. He chanted all night; sometimes he ate great quantities of prasāda, sometimes fasted for days.

## Teaching

He would teach rāgānugā bhajana — service in loyalty to the siddha gurus in a siddha-gopī body — only to one who knew his guru-praṇālī; he sent a young bābājī who served him back to Bengal to learn it. When **Kṛṣṇadāsa** of Govardhana came to him troubled by passion after years of service to Govindajī in Jaipur, he told him: "green wood will not catch fire: the material desires must first dry up by renunciation" (the record).

## Appearance and disappearance

**Disappearance:** Caitra śukla dvādaśī.
