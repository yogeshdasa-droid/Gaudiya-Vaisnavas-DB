## In short

**Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī** of **Govardhana** («Siddha Bābā»), born **Baṭakṛṣṇa** in a Kānungo family of Orissa, learnt bhajana in Vṛndāvana from **Vaiṣṇava Caraṇa dāsa** of Brahma-kuṇḍa, the compiler of the *Padakalpataru*, in the line of **Narottama Ṭhākura**. He served Govindajī in Jaipur, was taught renunciation by **Siddha Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa** of Kāmyavana, and lived at Govardhana as the great siddha of his time; his disciples included **Balarāma dāsa Bābājī**, **Nityānanda dāsa Bābājī** and **Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī** of Kālnā.

## Life

His father, **Sanātana Kānungo**, married twice; the second wife, **Jārī**, daughter of Maṅgarāja, had three sons — Rāmacandra, Prasādī and **Baṭakṛṣṇa**. When Sanātana died Jārī became satī; before mounting the pyre she gave her eldest the title Maṅgarāja, told the second to keep the household, and asked the youngest, then twelve, to live as a Vaiṣṇava in Vṛndāvana. At sixteen he walked to Vraja and studied two more years; he took lessons in bhajana from Vaiṣṇava Caraṇa dāsa. After his teacher's death he went to **Jaipur** to see Govindajī, and the king gave him the service of guarding the deity's doors; he served eight or ten years.

## Green wood

One day after eating at the palace he was seized by passion; he left Jaipur at once for **Kāmyavana** and asked Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa why this had happened after serving Govinda and eating His prasāda. "When you cut green wood and soak it, will it catch fire at once? The wood must dry. To the degree the soul is dried of material attachment, it enters bhakti-rasa" (citing the *Govinda-bhāṣya* and *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 3.70, Antya 6). He then lived in strict renunciation at Govardhana.

## Disciples

**Balarāma dāsa Bābājī** of Jhāḍu-maṇḍala, **Nityānanda dāsa Bābājī** of Madana-mohana Ṭhaurā (his second disciple), **Bhagavān dāsa Bābājī** of Kālnā; **Giridhārī dāsa** took veśa from a Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī of Govardhana in 1890. Manohara dāsa found him still alive when he came to Kusuma-sarovara (c. 1886).

## Others of the name

Not **Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī of Rāṇabāḍī** (Kṛṣṇaprasāda Caṭṭopādhyāya of Bankura), nor **Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī of Navadvīpa**, nor **Akiñcana Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī** (20th century) — their own records.
