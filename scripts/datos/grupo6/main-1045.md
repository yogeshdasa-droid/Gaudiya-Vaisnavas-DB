## In short

**Siddha Nityānanda dāsa Bābājī** of **Madana-mohana Ṭhaurā** in Vṛndāvana, of the family of **Gadādhara Paṇḍita**, a Rāḍhī brāhmaṇa, was the **second disciple of Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī** of Govardhana and called **Balarāma dāsa Bābājī** his elder brother. He knew the whole *Govinda-līlāmṛta* by heart and taught many how to do bhajana; **Rāmakṛṣṇa dāsa Paṇḍita Bābājī** was his mantra-disciple, and Gaura-śiromaṇi Mahāśaya was also initiated by him.

## With his guru

He lived with his guru in Vṛndāvana, studied the *Hari-nāmāmṛta* and then the *Bhāgavatam* with **Madhusūdana Gosvāmī**, priest of Rādhā-ramaṇa, and served his guru, then over a hundred years old. At twenty-five, troubled at night, he went toward a maidservant's room, fell into a pit and lay there three days; then he went to chant by the Mānasa-gaṅgā. His guru held a festival for the old Vaiṣṇavas to bless him and sent him to do bhajana at Govardhana. When his guru was leaving the world he called him; Nityānanda did not believe the message and came too late. For a year he worshipped an empty seat for his guru; one day the guru appeared: "Why did you not come that day? Now I can do nothing for you."

## Bhajana

From then he kept silence, chanted a lakh of names a day, slept two hours, and spoke the name very clearly and sweetly. Simply seeing him people began to say "Kṛṣṇa"; problems were solved before they were asked. His last five years were at Govardhana near Govinda-kuṇḍa; telling **Advaita dāsa Bābājī** that his guru had said he must suffer one more year, he went to the Mānasa-gaṅgā in Kārtika, before the Niyama-sevā, and passed away on **Rāsa-pūrṇimā**.

## Disciples

Rāmakṛṣṇa dāsa Paṇḍita Bābājī; Kṛṣṇaprasāda dāsa («Gudhiḍi Bābā»), his veśa-disciple; Gaura dāsa; Narottama dāsa Adhikārī; Gaurakiśora dāsa; Nṛsiṁhānanda Bhaṭṭa of Karnataka; Jagadānanda dāsa of Orissa took veśa from him.

## Appearance and disappearance

**Disappearance:** Kārtika pūrṇimā (Rāsa-pūrṇimā), by the Mānasa-gaṅgā.
