## In short

**Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī of Navadvīpa** (1818–1919) — born **Keśava** (also called Kiśora) in the Navaśākha clan at **Śrīhaṭṭa** — took dīkṣā at Navadvīpa from **Siddha Caitanya dāsa Bābājī** and, after his wife's death, veśa from **Dīnahīna dāsa Bābājī** of Śrīhaṭṭa. He did bhajana at Purī, in Vṛndāvana, at Śrīkhaṇḍa and at Navadvīpa, always at his guru's orders, and passed away chanting Gaura's name before his guru's samādhi.

## Householder

From childhood he showed deep love for Vaiṣṇavism. At thirty he went to Navadvīpa and received dīkṣā from Siddha Caitanya dāsa Bābājī. He wanted to take the vow of renunciation, but the Siddha Bābā, knowing he was married, asked him to stay with his wife, and he lived ten more years as a householder.

## Bābājī

After his wife died in **1858** he went on pilgrimage. Walking to Purī, he took veśa from Dīnahīna dāsa Bābājī of Śrīhaṭṭa and received the name **Kṛṣṇadāsa**. He did sādhana at Purī for fourteen years and bhajana in Vṛndāvana for twenty-four, then returned to Gauḍa-maṇḍala. His guru sent him to **Śrīkhaṇḍa**, where he stayed seven years; then he did bhajana at Navadvīpa near the famous bhajana-kuṭīra. Again at the Siddha Bābā's command he walked to Vṛndāvana, and there received the news of his guru's death; he came back to Navadvīpa for the last rites, and stayed there until **1326 BS (1919)**, when he passed away chanting the name of Gaura before the Siddha Bābā's samādhi.
