## In short

**Navadvīpacandra dāsa** (died 1902), born in **East Bengal**, was the inseparable disciple of **Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa Bābājī**, whom he met at Navadvīpa near the hermitage of Śrī Nṛsiṁha-deva. The embodiment of humility, he delivered many fallen souls by his preaching; sent by his guru to Vṛndāvana, he passed away there in **1902**, remembering his guru's lotus feet.

## Meeting his guru

A Vaiṣṇava lady told him of Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa Bābājī, who was living near the hermitage of Śrī Nṛsiṁha-deva at Navadvīpa, and he went for his darśana. The moment they met, a great love arose between them; they embraced happily, and from that day they were inseparable.

## Disciple

He was so close to his guru that he easily perceived Rādhāramaṇa's inner thoughts, and through his preaching many fallen souls were delivered. On Rādhāramaṇa's advice he went to Śrī Vṛndāvana, and there in 1902 he left this world, remembering the lotus feet of his guru, Śrī Rādhāramaṇa-deva.
