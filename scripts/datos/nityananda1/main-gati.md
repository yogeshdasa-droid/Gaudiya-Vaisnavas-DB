## In short

Sundarānanda Ṭhākura was the son and disciple of **Gatigovinda Ṭhākura**, and so a grandson of **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya**: "The chief disciples of Śrī Gati Prabhu were his sons — Śrī Kṛṣṇaprasāda Ṭhākura of grave heart, Śrī Sundarānanda and Śrī Hari Ṭhākura: three sons, three disciples, three heroes of devotion" (*Karṇānanda*; *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*, entry «Sundarānanda» 2).

## Other persons of the same name

He is not the gopāla Sundarānanda Ṭhākura of Haldā Maheśpur, Sudāmā in Kṛṣṇa-līlā, of Śrī Nityānanda's branch — the two were mixed in one record until 05-11-2026.
