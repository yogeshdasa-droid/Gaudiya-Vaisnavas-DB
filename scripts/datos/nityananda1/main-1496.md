## In short

Sundarānanda Ṭhākura was one of the **twelve gopālas** and Lord Nityānanda's most intimate servant: "Sundarānanda, another branch of Nityānanda, was His servant at heart; in his company Nityānanda tasted the life of Vraja" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.23). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Sudāmā** sakhā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 127). His Śrīpāṭa is **Haldā Maheśpur** in the Jessore district (now Bangladesh); some say Bodhkhānā.

## Life

He was a brahmacārī and never married; the descendants of his brother and of his disciples still live at Maheśpur, where only his birthplace can be seen today. An ocean of the mellows of love for Kṛṣṇa, in prema-unmāda he brought a live crocodile out of the water, and he made kadamba flowers blossom on a jambīra (lemon) tree: "I worship Sundarānanda Ṭhākura, who made kadamba flowers bloom on a jambīra tree" (*Vaiṣṇava-vandanā*). "At Haldā Maheśpur is Sundarānanda's home; know for certain that Sundarānanda was Sudāmā" (*Pāṭa-paryaṭana*). Some of his disciples, it is said, drove wild tigers out of the forests and gave them the holy name.

His **samādhi** is in Vṛndāvana, and his Deity, **Śrī Kālācāṅd**, is served in the "new" Govindajī temple there.

He is named in Murāri Gupta's *Kṛṣṇa-caitanya-caritāmṛta* (4.22.11), in the *Caitanya-maṅgala* of Jayānanda and of Locana, in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6.474) and in the *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā*s of Jīva, Devakīnandana and Vṛndāvana dāsa.

## Disappearance

On the **Kārtika pūrṇimā**.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Sudāmā**, one of Kṛṣṇa's cowherd friends (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 127).

## Other persons of the same name

- **Sundarānanda Ṭhākura, son of Gatigovinda** — grandson of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya, son and disciple of Gatigovinda (*Karṇānanda* 5; *Abhidhāna*). His own record.
- Sundarānanda Paṇḍita of Bhāṅgāmoḍā, disciple of Abhirāma Gosvāmī (*Pāṭa-paryaṭana*).
- Sundarānanda, disciple of Śyāmānanda (Gopīvallabhapura).
- Sundarānanda Vidyāvinoda (20th century).
