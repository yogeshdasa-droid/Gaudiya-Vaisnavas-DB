## In short

Uddhāraṇa Datta Ṭhākura was one of the **twelve gopālas** and an intimate servant of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu: "The great devotee Uddhāraṇa Datta, the eleventh of the twelve gopālas, served Nityānanda's feet in every way" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.41). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Subāhu** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*). Born in a wealthy suvarṇa-vaṇik (gold-merchant) family of **Saptagrāma**, he became the servant of Nityānanda, and through him the whole vaṇik community was purified.

## Birth and family

He was born in **1403 Śaka (1481 CE)** in the rich town of Saptagrāma (Hooghly). His father was **Śrīkara Datta**, his mother **Bhadrāvatī**, and his son **Śrīnivāsa Datta**. His forefather **Bhaveśa Datta** came from Ayodhyā to Suvarṇagrāma to trade in 975 Śaka and married Bhāgyavatī, sister of Kāñjilāla Dhara, whose son Umāpati Dhara sat with the poet Jayadeva and Govardhanācārya in the court of King Lakṣmaṇasena. Bhaveśa's son Kṛṣṇa Datta was a scholar; his son was Śrīkara (*Abhidhāna*, with a family tree).

## Life

He was the **dewan** (manager of the estate) of a king of **Naihāṭī** ("Nairājā"), a krośa and a half north of Katwa; he lived at **Uddhāraṇapura**, north of Naihāṭī, which is said to be named after him. Remains of the king's palace are seen at Pātāihāṭ.

**With Nityānanda.** After staying at Khaḍadaha, Nityānanda Prabhu came with His associates to Saptagrāma, the place of the seven ṛṣis, and bathed at the **Triveṇī**, where the Gaṅgā, Yamunā and Sarasvatī meet. Uddhāraṇa was staying in a temple on the bank. "With body, mind and words, free from falsehood, he worshipped the feet of Nityānanda … birth after birth Nityānanda is the Lord, and birth after birth Uddhāraṇa is His servant. All the vaṇik families were purified by him, there is no doubt." Nityānanda went from door to door through Saptagrāma chanting the holy names, and the vaṇiks took shelter at His feet; the bliss once felt in Nadia was felt again there (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5.443–461; Antya 6).

He was so dear to Nityānanda that when the brāhmaṇa bhaṭṭācāryas at Sūryadāsa Paṇḍita's house asked the Lord who cooked for Him, He said: "Sometimes I do; when I cannot, Uddhāraṇa cooks."

**Renounced or householder?** According to the *Abhidhāna* and B. V. Tīrtha, he gave up great wealth, wife and children, and travelled with Nityānanda as His servant (at the age of 26, B. V. Tīrtha). Bhaktivedānta Svāmī writes that he remained a householder all his life (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 11.41, purport).

Towards the end of his life he left Saptagrāma and settled at Uddhāraṇapura. Jāhnavā Mātā later visited his house at Saptagrāma, after he had left this world (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 11.775).

## Places

- **Saptagrāma** (near Triśbighā station): the temple where he served a six-armed Deity of Mahāprabhu, with Nityānanda on the right and Gadādhara on the left, Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Govinda on another throne, and his picture below. An old Mādhavī-latā there is said to have been planted by Nityānanda Himself. The suvarṇa-vaṇik community restored the Śrīpāṭa; Bhaktivedānta Svāmī, whose family belonged to the same community, visited it as a child and again in 1967.
- **Uddhāraṇapura** (near Katwa): the Nitāi-Gaura Deities he installed, and his **samādhi** to the west of the temple; the masonry ghāṭa on the Ganges is said to be his work. A festival is held there every year.
- His śālagrāma is served in the house of Śrīnātha Datta; an old carved image of him is worshipped daily in the temple of Jagamohana Datta of Bālī (Hooghly).

Other accounts: the *Pāṭa-paryaṭana* says he lived at Kṛṣṇapura near Hooghly ("Uddhāraṇa Datta lived at Kṛṣṇapura … know Subāhu to be his former name"); Jayakṛṣṇa says he was born at Śāntipura.

## Disappearance

At the age of 60, in **1463 Śaka (1541 CE)**, on the **kṛṣṇa trayodaśī** — of Pauṣa according to the texts and the Gauḍīya calendar, of Agrahāyaṇa according to the *Abhidhāna*. His descendants are spread over Hooghly, Calcutta and other places.

Haridāsa Nandī wrote a biography, *Uddhāraṇa Ṭhākura* (1925). He is also named in Murāri Gupta (4.22.22), *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Antya 6.62, the *Jagannātha-maṅgala* of Gadādhara dāsa, and the *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā*s of Jīva, Devakīnandana and Vṛndāvana dāsa.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Subāhu**, one of the cowherd boys of Vṛndāvana.
