## In short

Śrīla **Viśvanātha Cakravartī Ṭhākura** (Mahāmahopādhyāya), the great ācārya of the Gauḍīyas after the Gosvāmīs and their followers, wrote the **Sārārtha-darśinī** commentary on the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* and the *Gītā*, commentaries on most works of the Gosvāmīs, and many books of his own (*Mādhurya-kādambinī*, *Rāga-vartma-candrikā*, *Kṛṣṇa-bhāvanāmṛta*…). He was in the line of **Narottama Ṭhākura**: "the name Viśvanātha, the lord of the world, for showing the path of bhakti; the name Cakravartī, for being the ruler of the circle of devotees". He sent **Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa** to Galtā to defend the Gauḍīya line.

## Birth and line

He was born in **1576 Śaka** (others: 1586) at **Devagrāma**, Sāgardīghi police station, Murshidabad; father **Rāmanārāyaṇa Cakravartī**, a Rāḍhī brāhmaṇa; his elder brothers were Rāmabhadra and Raghunātha. After his first studies at Devagrāma he studied bhakti-śāstra at **Saidābād**. In the *Saṅkalpa-kalpadruma* he gives his guru-praṇālī: **Kṛṣṇacaraṇa Cakravartī** of Gāmbhīlā (Bāluchar), of Narottama's branch — son of **Rāmakṛṣṇa Ācārya** of Saidābād and adopted son of **Gaṅgānārāyaṇa Cakravartī** — was his parama-guru, and Kṛṣṇacaraṇa's son **Rādhāramaṇa Cakravartī** his **dīkṣā-guru**. Kṛṣṇacaraṇa taught bhakti-śāstra at Saidābād, and Viśvanātha read the *Bhāgavatam* with him; it is said he wrote the *Bindu*, the *Kiraṇa* and the *Kaṇā* there (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*).

## Leaving home

He married young but had no attachment. It is told that, when he had gone to Vṛndāvana, his guru sent him home once, and he spent one night with his wife giving her the nectar of the *Bhāgavatam*, and left at dawn. In Vṛndāvana he became the leader of the Vaiṣṇava community of his time; in due course he took the dress of a renunciant and the name **Harivallabha** (others say he never took veśa) (*Abhidhāna*). He lived at Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Vṛndāvana and installed **Śrī Gokulānanda**. It is said that where he wrote the *Bhāgavatam* the rain did not fall, and that much later Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī pulled his manuscripts dry out of the Mānasa-gaṅgā after three or four days under water.

## Galtā

When the Gauḍīya priests of the temples of Jaipur were put out as being "of no sampradāya", he, already old, sent **Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa** with **Kṛṣṇadeva Sārvabhauma** to Jaipur; Baladeva defeated the opponents at Galtā, established the Gauḍīya seat again and wrote the *Govinda-bhāṣya* (*Abhidhāna*, «Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa»).

## Works

Commentaries: the **Sārārtha-darśinī** on the *Bhāgavatam* (completed 1704) and on the *Gītā*; *Ānanda-candrikā* on the *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi*; *Bhakti-sāra-pradarśinī* on the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*; *Bhakta-harṣiṇī* on the *Gopāla-tāpanī*; on the *Brahma-saṁhitā*; *Mahatī* on the *Dāna-keli-kaumudī*; *Sukha-vartinī* on the *Ānanda-vṛndāvana-campū*; *Subodhinī* on the *Alaṅkāra-kaustubha*; on the *Haṁsadūta*, the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* and the *Prema-bhakti-candrikā*. His own: *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-bhāvanāmṛta*, *Gaurāṅga-līlāmṛta*, *Aiśvarya-kādambinī*, *Stavāmṛta-laharī*, *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu-bindu*, *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi-kiraṇa*, *Bhāgavatāmṛta-kaṇā*, *Rāga-vartma-candrikā*, *Mādhurya-kādambinī*, *Camatkāra-candrikā*, *Kṣaṇadā-gīta-cintāmaṇi*, *Saṅkalpa-kalpadruma* and others (*Abhidhāna*).

## Disappearance

He disappeared at **Rādhā-kuṇḍa** on **Māgha śukla pañcamī**; his samādhi was at Pātharpurā in Vṛndāvana and has been moved to the **Gokulānanda** temple. His descendants still live at Bāluchar (*Abhidhāna*). The record dates him c. 1638–1708 (Madhusūdana Tattvavācaspati: born 1633–1638), with the *Sārārtha-darśinī* finished in 1704.

## Appearance and disappearance

**Appearance:** 1576 Śaka (others 1586) (*Abhidhāna*).
**Disappearance:** Māgha śukla pañcamī (Vasanta-pañcamī), at Rādhā-kuṇḍa (*Abhidhāna*; Gauḍīya calendar).
