## In short

Śrī **Narahari Cakravartī** (**Ghanaśyāma dāsa**), the author of the **Bhakti-ratnākara** and the **Narottama-vilāsa**, was the cook of **Śrī Govindajī** in Vṛndāvana — so he was called **"Rasuiyā Pūjārī"** — and a disciple of **Nṛsiṁha Cakravartī**: "my worshipable Lord is Śrī Nṛsiṁha Cakravartī; birth after birth may I serve those feet" (*Narottama-vilāsa* 13). His father **Jagannātha** was a disciple of Viśvanātha Cakravartī.

## Life

He was born at **Reñāpur** (Reñāgrām), Murshidabad; the *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna* says at the beginning of the 17th century, though his father's being Viśvanātha's disciple points to the end of it. He remained unmarried until his parents died, then went to Vraja by Govindajī's order and served as His cook most of his life. He was at once a fine cook, singer, musician and great devotee.

## His books

*Bhakti-ratnākara*, *Narottama-vilāsa*, *Śrīnivāsa-caritra*, *Gīta-candrodaya*, *Chandaḥ-samudra*, *Gaura-carita-cintāmaṇi*, *Nāmāmṛta-samudra*, *Paddhati-pradīpa*, *Saṅgīta-sāra-saṅgraha*. The *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* and *Caitanya-maṅgala* do not tell the lives of Lokanātha, Prabodhānanda or Gopāla Bhaṭṭa, nor of the three later mahājanas Śrīnivāsa, Narottama and Śyāmānanda; he wrote them, and the lives of the ācāryas and devotees after Mahāprabhu, in the *Bhakti-ratnākara* and *Narottama-vilāsa*. The fifth wave of the *Bhakti-ratnākara* (the round of Vraja-maṇḍala) and the twelfth (the round of Navadvīpa) are an invaluable map of those places (*Abhidhāna*). His Gaura songs are hard to tell from those of Narahari Sarakāra.

## Ghanaśyāma

He signed his songs "Ghanaśyāma" or "Narahari dāsa Ghanaśyāma dāsa"; the record «Ghanaśyāma Cakravartī» (#328) was joined to this one (Whisper, 06-11-2026). The *Abhidhāna* has also a Ghanaśyāma Cakravartī of Budhuri, grandson of Govinda Kavirāja, to whom the Nawab gave sixty bighas for his songs (author of the *Govinda-rati-mañjarī*), and a Ghanaśyāma dāsa disciple of Śrīnivāsa — others.
