## In short

Paramahaṁsa **Akiñcana Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī Mahārāja**, disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, was famous for chanting the holy name day and night and for his kīrtana. A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda called him akiñcana (without possessions) and a paramahaṁsa.

## Life

- Born in East Bengal; he took up Kṛṣṇa consciousness in college, joined the Gauḍīya Maṭha and took initiation from Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī.
- His only service in the maṭha was chanting Hare Kṛṣṇa. When his godbrothers complained, his guru said his chanting was fine, told them to encourage him and even to advertise for men who would chant all day and night; he ordered that the bābājī be given every facility.
- After his guru's departure he is said to have lived in seclusion at Haridvāra; later he wandered between Māyāpura (Navadvīpa in winter) and Vṛndāvana (Bon Mahārāja's bhajana-kuṭīra, four or five months every hot season, chanting in a cool cave room in the afternoons). He led kīrtana at 4:30 am, weeping without losing his voice, and answered everything with a laughing «Hare Kṛṣṇa!». He never made disciples, built temples, collected money or criticised anyone; he took garlands of mahāprasāda and took them off at once.
- He sang the kīrtana at A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī's sannyāsa in Mathurā (1959); told to chant more, he understood that Svāmī Mahārāja would spread the holy name worldwide, and later called him the greatest servant of the holy name.
- Near the end a doctor listening to his heart heard the mahā-mantra instead of the heartbeat. He left the world on **Vaiśākha Kṛṣṇa Pañcamī** (Gauḍīya calendar); his samādhi is at Nandagrāma, near Sanātana Gosvāmī's bhajana-kuṭīra.
