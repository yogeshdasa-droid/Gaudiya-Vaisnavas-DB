## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Vilāsa Tīrtha Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Kuñjabihārī Vidyābhūṣaṇa** («Kuñja-dā») — was the manager of the Gauḍīya Maṭha from its beginning and the right hand of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]** in building the maṭhas; after his guru's departure he became ācārya of **Śrī Caitanya Maṭha**, Māyāpura, keeping the Yogapīṭha and most of the maṭhas in India.

## Life

- He met Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī when he was still living in solitary bhajana and helped him come out to preach; with him he carried the body of Gaura Kiśora dāsa Bābājī (1915). He worked in the post office in Calcutta (and in Mesopotamia, now Iraq) and gave his money and time to the maṭha; with Ananta Vāsudeva he found the first Calcutta maṭha at Ultadingi Road.
- His guru, once very ill, asked for «Kuñja-dā»; when he came, his guru embraced him weeping, and was better (told by Bon Mahārāja).
- In 1934 he performed his mother's śrāddha by the smārta system instead of the Vaiṣṇava one, against his guru's request, which grieved Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī.
- After 1937 the mission split between his followers and those of Ananta Vāsudeva (Bhakti Prasāda Purī); he remained ācārya of Śrī Caitanya Maṭha until his departure, and wrote many books. After him Śramaṇa Mahārāja, then Bhakti Hṛdaya Bon Mahārāja led the original mission.
- His appearance: **Caitra Śukla Saptamī**; his departure: **Āśvina Kṛṣṇa Dvitīyā** (Gauḍīya calendar).

## Other versions

- One text says Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī foresaw that his smārta śrāddha would «be the cause of the downfall of the Gauḍīya Maṭha»; another, written by a disciple of A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī, defends him as the bona fide successor at Māyāpura.
