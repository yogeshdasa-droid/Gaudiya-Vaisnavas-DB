## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Kevala Auḍulomi Mahārāja (Pramoda Bihārī Guha Ṭhākurtā, 1895–1982), disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, led the Navadvīpa parikramā for seven years and from 1954 was president-ācārya of the **Gauḍīya Mission**. He was the brother of [[Śrīla Bhakti Prajñana Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1798|Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī]].

## Life

- Born in 1895, in Agrahāyaṇa, on the kṛṣṇa aṣṭamī, at Bānāripāḍā (Barisal). Father Śaratcandra Guha Ṭhākurtā, mother Bhuvanamohinī, both very pious. Calm from childhood; graduated with distinction from Calcutta University (1919), studied philosophy at Kāśī for some months and worked as a teacher.
- He had the darśana of Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura, which inspired him to study the śāstras. Taking shelter of Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī, he was named **Patitapāvana dāsa Brahmacārī** and served the Deities; later his guru gave him tridaṇḍi-sannyāsa as **Bhakti Kevala Auḍulomi**, and he preached all over India. He was one of those chosen to preach abroad.
- After his guru's departure (1937), Bhakti Prasāda Purī (Ananta Vāsudeva), ācārya of the Gauḍīya Mission, gave him in March 1940 the Navadvīpa-dhāma parikramā; for seven years he led it and was head of the Navadvīpa maṇḍala and Śrī Caitanya Maṭha. Member of the Paricaryā committee (1943); translated the Caitanya-śikṣāmṛta into English.
- Preached in Mymensingh, Nārāyaṇgañj and Dhaka during Ūrja-vrata; in October 1953 took the paramahaṁsa dress. Led the great saṅkīrtana of the Śrī Rūpa Gauḍīya Maṭha at the Prayāga Kumbha-melā (January 1954).
- After Bhakti Pradīpa Tīrtha's departure (December 1953) he became **president-ācārya of the Gauḍīya Mission** on 16 February 1954. Built a temple at Cirulia (Midnapore) and works at Kiśorapura (Vṛndāvana), Godruma, Patna, Purī and Lucknow; began the yearly Vraja-maṇḍala parikramā (1957–60).
- Left the world in 1982.
