## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Pramoda Purī Gosvāmī Mahārāja (Pramoda Bhūṣaṇa Cakravartī, 1898–1999), disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, was for many years editor of the Gauḍīya Maṭha's papers and lived to the age of 101, one of the last direct disciples of his guru. He founded the Śrī Gopīnātha Gauḍīya Maṭha.

## Life

- Born on 8 October 1898, on **Āśvina Śukla Caturthī** (Gauḍīya calendar), at Gaṅgānandapura (Jessore, now Bangladesh), in a brāhmaṇa (Cakravartī) family.
- Bhaktiratnākara Ṭhākura introduced him to Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura's books. He studied chemistry (B.Sc.) at Bangabasi College, Calcutta University, and worked at the Post Commission in Calcutta, where he met Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī.
- Dīkṣā on **Janmāṣṭamī 1923** at the Ultadanga Gauḍīya Maṭha (Pranavānanda Brahmacārī). He travelled with his guru and the senior sannyāsīs and became editor of the Gauḍīya Maṭha's publications.
- He took tridaṇḍi-sannyāsa from his godbrother **Bhakti Gaurava Vaikhānasa Mahārāja** at Cāṁpāhāṭi, by an order of his guru in a dream.
- Very affectionate to Bhaktivedānta Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja, who regarded him as his own guru.
- He left the world on 22 November 1999 at 2:07 am, aged 101, on **Kārtika Śukla Caturdaśī** (Gauḍīya calendar).

## Other versions
