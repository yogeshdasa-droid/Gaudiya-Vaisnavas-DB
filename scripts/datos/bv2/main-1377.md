## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Sāraṅga Gosvāmī Mahārāja (Atulacandra Bandyopādhyāya, d. 1962), of the family of Nityānanda Prabhu, gave up a high government post to serve **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, who sent him to London as «missionary in charge» in Europe. After his guru's departure he took sannyāsa from [[Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī#ficha-1802|Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Mahārāja]] — the first to receive it from him — and founded the **Śrī Gauḍīya Saṅgha**.

## Life

- Born in the Nityānanda-vaṁśa, as Atulacandra (Bandyopādhyāya / Gosvāmī). On his first visit to Śrī Caitanya Maṭha he was introduced to his guru by Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Mahārāja, his lifelong friend. In 1919 Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī visited his house at Dhanbad.
- He left his post and home, and raised support for the maṭhas among merchants of Calcutta, Delhi and Bombay.
- After Bon Mahārāja came back to India, his guru sent him to London. In 1937 he met the King and Queen at Buckingham Palace and spoke on the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra at the World Congress of Faiths in Oxford. He went through hunger, lived for a time in a park, and found a golden Deity of Adhokṣaja Viṣṇu in a drain pipe; that same day a letter with money from his guru arrived. His guru told him at Howrah station: «If someone in Europe wants initiation, give it» — and he initiated an Australian disciple during his guru's lifetime. With Bhakti Pradīpa Tīrtha he opened the London centre in the house given by Miss Daisy Bowtell (Vinoda-vāṇī dāsī).
- Founded the Śrī Gauḍīya Saṅgha with about thirty branches (twenty-two temples by another text); recovered **Imli-talā** in Vṛndāvana and built the Gaura-Nityānanda temple at Nandana Ācārya's house in Māyāpura, where the two Lords first met. Gave sannyāsa to some forty disciples, published about twenty books, edited the weekly Gauḍīya and started an English Sajjana-toṣaṇī. Joined Śrīdhara Mahārāja's Navadvīpa parikramā with Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara and Madhusūdana Mahārāja.
- Left the world in 1962 on **Jyeṣṭha Kṛṣṇa Pratipat** (Gauḍīya calendar; his appearance: Phālguna Kṛṣṇa Ṣaṣṭhī). His samādhi is in his maṭha at Māyāpura, his puṣpa-samādhi at Imli-talā.

## Other versions

- His surname: Bandyopādhyāya (one text), Gosvāmī (another, as a member of Nityānanda's family).
- Number of his temples: thirty branches of the Gauḍīya Saṅgha (one text) or twenty-two (another).
