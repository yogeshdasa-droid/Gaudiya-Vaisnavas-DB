## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Prasāda Purī Gosvāmī — **Ananta Vāsudeva** Paravidyābhūṣaṇa (1895–1958) — was, with Sundarānanda Vidyāvinoda, one of «the two hands» of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**: he wrote down his commentaries (the Anubhāṣya of the Caitanya-caritāmṛta, the Gauḍīya-bhāṣya of the Caitanya-bhāgavata and of the Bhāgavatam) and composed the praṇāma-mantras of the guru-varga. After his guru's departure he was elected ācārya of the Gauḍīya Maṭha. He was the younger brother of [[Bhakti Pradip Tīrtha#ficha-122|Bhakti Pradīpa Tīrtha]].

## Life

- Born on **Bhādra Śukla Ṣaṣṭhī** 1302 (25 August 1895), one of the five sons of Rajanīkānta Vasu and Bidhumukhī of Sandvīpa-Hātiyā (Noākhālī). Called Ananta; loved kīrtana and at eight could recite parts of the Rāmāyaṇa, Mahābhārata and Gītā.
- On the day after his guru's sannyāsa (1918) he received dīkṣā as **Ananta Vāsudeva Brahmacārī**; later the title Paravidyābhūṣaṇa. A śruti-dhara: he remembered everything his guru said about the Bhāgavatam and supplied verses when asked. He would not eat until his guru told him to. With Kuñjabihārī he found the house at 1 Ultadingi Road, the first Calcutta maṭha; from 1919 he ran the Bhāgavata Press. When his guru turned fifty he began the **Vyāsa-pūjā** and in 1929 composed the praṇāma-mantras («nama oṁ viṣṇu-pādāya…») for Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī, Gaura Kiśora, Bhaktivinoda and Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī. His guru said: «What I have not explained, Vāsudeva will explain»; once he dreamed of his guru as Nayana-mañjarī.
- After 1 January 1937 he was elected **president-ācārya** of the Gauḍīya Maṭha and Mission and initiated over a hundred people that evening; he preached in Dhaka, Rangoon (1938) and at the Haridvāra Kumbha-melā. The mission later split between his followers and those of Bhakti Vilāsa Tīrtha.
- He took tridaṇḍi-sannyāsa at **Gayā** on Āṣāḍha kṛṣṇa pañcamī 1346 (1939) as **Bhakti Prasāda Purī**, then lived with only a kaupīna, barefoot, without metal pots, sleeping on the floor. From 1945 he published the works of the Gosvāmīs; in 1954 he made Bhakti Kevala Auḍulomi ācārya of the Gauḍīya Mission and withdrew to Vṛndāvana.
- He left the world on **8 March 1958** at the kuñja of Śrī Rādhāramaṇa in Vṛndāvana, asking the devotees to chant «Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Śacī-suta Gaura guṇa-dhāma».

## Other versions

- One text of Bhakti Vilāsa Tīrtha's record says that in the dispute after 1937 «Ananta Vāsudeva fell down». His record here keeps both names: Ananta Vāsudeva (until 1939) and Bhakti Prasāda Purī.
