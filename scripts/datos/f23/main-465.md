## In short

**Haricaraṇa dāsa Bābājī** did bhajana at **Rādhā-kuṇḍa** after **Gaura Śiromaṇi** passed away, and later at the chatrī of **Kusuma-sarovara**, while **Rāmakṛṣṇa Ṭhākura Bābājī** was at the Śyāma-kuṭīra. Rāmakṛṣṇa Ṭhākura sent him **Balavantarāo Bhaiyā Sāheb**, stepbrother of the Mahārāja of Gwalior, to learn bhajana; the Bhaiyā Sāheb built the temple of **Rādhākāntajīu** at Kusuma-sarovara and the «Rādhākānta fund» for Vaiṣṇavas. He was a contemporary of **Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa Deva** and saw much of his līlā in Vṛndāvana.

## Bhaiyā Sāheb

Balavantarāo Bhaiyā Sāheb, having received the seed of bhakti by some grace, came to Rāmakṛṣṇa Ṭhākura to learn bhajana and sādhana; Rāmakṛṣṇa Ṭhākura, thinking Haricaraṇa dāsa the most competent teacher for such a student, sent him to him. The Bhaiyā Sāheb stayed with Haricaraṇa dāsa and in time built a temple and a garden near Kusuma-sarovara for the service of **Rādhākāntajīu** and of the Vaiṣṇavas. For devotees living far from Vraja he set up a permanent monthly grant, and a **Rādhākānta fund** in Government securities, under a managing committee chaired by the Collector of Mathurā, to make sure the funds went to the sevā and the monthly grants were paid; the work, the record says, goes on efficiently today.
