## In short

**Dayāla dāsa Bābājī** of **Vṛndāvana** was known all over Vraja and Gauḍa-maṇḍala for his renunciation. His only possessions were a cotton wrapper, an earthen pot and a kaupīna; no one could tell from his looks his age or where he was born. He was a friend of **Kāminī Kumār Ghoṣ**, some of whose family were cured of incurable illnesses by his grace.

## On the Yamunā ghāṭ

About three o'clock one winter afternoon he sat down on a ghāṭ of the Yamunā, covered his head with his cotton wrapper and sat motionless as a statue until two o'clock the next afternoon. At times he kept silence; at other times he wandered endlessly round Vṛndāvana.
