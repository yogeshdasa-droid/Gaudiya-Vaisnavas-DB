## In short

**Mānasiṁha** (died 1614), son of **Bhagavān dāsa** of the Pṛthvīrāja family of **Āmber**, Mansabdār of 5,000 under **Emperor Akbar** and Subahdār of Bengal, Bihar and Orissa from 1600, built the beautiful temple of **Śrī Śrī Govindadeva** in Vṛndāvana. Of a Vaiṣṇava family and a Vaiṣṇava himself, he is called «Viṣṇu-padāmbuja-bhṛṅga» (a bee at Viṣṇu's lotus feet) in Kavikaṅkaṇa's *Caṇḍī*.

## Govindadeva and Kāśī

Before leaving for Bengal he arranged the abhiṣeka and the service of Govindadeva. On his way to Bengal he built at **Kāśī** the temple of Rāmajī, the Māna-sarovara and a liṅga of Maheśvara Mahādeva. He died in 1614 (Growse, *Mathurā*, p. 145). (At his recommendation the Emperor of Delhi also granted 409 bighās to the Gopīnātha temple of Ananta Purī at Baḍabeluna: see #47.)
