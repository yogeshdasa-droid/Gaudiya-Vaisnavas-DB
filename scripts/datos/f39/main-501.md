## In short

**Hiraṇya dāsa** (Hiraṇya Majumdār), a kāyastha, brother of **Govardhana Majumdār**, the rulers of **Saptagrāma**, was the paternal uncle of **Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī** (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 16.217-219).

## The Pāṭabāḍī at Kṛṣṇapura

At **Kṛṣṇapura** on the bank of the Sarasvatī, in Saptagrāma, there is a pāṭabāḍī that people call «Śrī Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmīra Pāṭabāḍī»; perhaps Hiraṇya dāsa and his family lived in a palace somewhere there. In it Haridāsa dāsa found a drum of olden days, made from the trunk of a palm tree, for the instrument called *dāmāmā*. When the Muslims invaded, the family Deity **Rādhā-Govinda** was taken elsewhere; the temple and Deity now at Khenksiyālī in Chinsurā are believed to have belonged to Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī's father.
