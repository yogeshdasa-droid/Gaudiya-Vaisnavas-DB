## In short

**Premānanda Bhāratī** — born **Surendranātha Mukhopādhyāya** in Calcutta in **1779 Śaka (1857)** — took sannyāsa and preached **Caitanya Mahāprabhu**'s teaching in **Europe and America**, where he initiated **5,000** men and women, founded **«The Society of Kṛṣṇa Consciousness»** in New York and was the first to install **Śrī Rādhā-Govinda** in the West. He came back to Calcutta in 1909 with four American disciples, and died in **1914**.

## His disciples

His disciple **Kṛṣṇa Gopāla Duggul** of the Punjab published the 6,000-page *Śrī Nimāicāṅd* in Urdu.
