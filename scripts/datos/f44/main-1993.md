## In short

**Jāhnavī-devī** was the mother of **Kāśīnātha (Kāśīśvara) Paṇḍita of Cātarā** (#1832). She went to **Lord Caitanya** at Jagannātha Purī and came back with her son (Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna, «Jāhnavī Devī»).

## Sources

The record's notes and text. Not Jāhnavā Mātā, Śrī Nityānanda's wife.
