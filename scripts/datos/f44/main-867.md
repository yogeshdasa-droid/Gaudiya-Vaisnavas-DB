## In short

There were **two devotees named Mādhava** in **Vṛndāvana**, both of whom had come from Bengal; nothing more is known of them (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 13.51).

## Sources

The record's text. Five other Mādhavas once in this record (Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi's friend, the one of Nityānanda's branch, Rasikānanda's disciple, the poet and the Oṛiyā disciple of Gadādhara) have their own records.
