## In short

**Jagannātha dāsa**, a poet of **Gilābāḍī** (Maldah), wrote the **Bhakta-caritāmṛta** in four volumes, taking the **Bhaktamāla** as his source.

## Sources

The record's text. Two other Jagannātha dāsas once in this record — the padakartā (#2099) and the one of Pāthara Hājaṅga — have their own records.
