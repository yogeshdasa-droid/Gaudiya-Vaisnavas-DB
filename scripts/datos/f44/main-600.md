## In short

**Jayagopāla dāsa**, a disciple of **Sundarānanda**, was the father of **Balarāma dāsa** of the famous **Maṅgala Ṭhākura** family of **Kāṇḍaḍā** (Burdwan); he wrote the **Kṛṣṇa-vilāsa** and the **Jñāna-pradīpa**.

## Sources

The record's text. Which Sundarānanda was his guru is not said. Another Jayagopāla dāsa, guru of Ghanaśyāma dāsa and author of the Bhakti-bhāva-pradīpa, has his own record (#2127).
