## In short

**Giridhara dāsa** was one of the two translators into Bengali of the **Manaḥ-śikṣā** of Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī — the other was **Yadunandana dāsa** (Pāṭabāḍī manuscript, Anurāgavallī 24 ka, kha).
