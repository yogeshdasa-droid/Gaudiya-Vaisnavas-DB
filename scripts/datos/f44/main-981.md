## In short

**Narahari Viśārada** — also known as **Maheśvara Viśārada** —, a famous scholar of Gauḍa and a contemporary of **Nīlāmbara Cakravartī**, was the father of **Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma** and of **Vidyā-vācaspati** of Vidyānagara (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 6.53; Caitanya-bhāgavata Madhya 21.6).
