## In short

**Jāhnavā-devī** was the wife of **Puruṣottama dāsa Ṭhākura**, son of Sadāśiva Kavirāja, and the mother of **Kānu Ṭhākura** (Śiśu Kṛṣṇadāsa, #677). She died soon after his birth, and **Nityānanda** took the child to Khaḍadaha (Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna, «Jāhnavā Devī» 2).
