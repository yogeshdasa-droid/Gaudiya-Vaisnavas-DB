## In short

**Sītānātha dāsa Bābājī** was a simple, broad-hearted Oṛiyā gopa who did bhajana in a hut on the north side of **Śrīnāthajī's temple at Govinda-kuṇḍa** (Govardhana). Unlettered, speaking only Oṛiyā all his life, he sang the glories of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa and Śrī Vṛndā in his own language; by worshipping Tulasī he had the darśana of **Śrī Vṛndā-devī**. **Advaita dāsa Bābājī** was with him at the end.

## His day

Before dawn he bathed in Govinda-kuṇḍa, then danced and sang kīrtana before Giridhārī for five hours at a stretch, worshipped and bathed Tulasī-devī, and went back to Śrīnāthajī's temple to pray in tears. At noon he went out for mādhukarī in the heat, without shoes or umbrella, gave a little of it to every Vaiṣṇava of the area and returned to his hut. He knew many beautiful songs by heart, and his sweet singing overwhelmed everyone with love.

He bathed twice a day in Govinda-kuṇḍa, the first time at three in the morning. When **Śrī Advaita Bābājī** told him that was not a proper hour, he answered: "I make no difference of the hour — whenever Śrīnāthajī breaks my sleep, I get up and bathe." Even two or three days before his death, completely bedridden, he was still seen bathing in the kuṇḍa in the early morning.

## Tulasī

On his last day he asked Advaita dāsa to carry him before Śrīnāthajī, to bathe him and mark him with tilaka, to bathe the Tulasī tree and give him that water, and asked whether everyone had received Śrīnāthajī's prasāda. As soon as Advaita dāsa put the Tulasī-water in his mouth, he breathed his last. It is said that a dry, almost dead Tulasī he watered would be fresh and green the next day.
