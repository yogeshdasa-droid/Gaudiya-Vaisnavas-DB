## In short

**Hare Kṛṣṇa dāsa Bābājī** was a bābājī of Vraja whose life was a model for lovers of Kṛṣṇa-bhajana: he did everything his body needed at a run, to lose no time from bhajana. In old age he lived at **Nūtana-gherā near Rādhā-kuṇḍa**, taking prasāda sent from **Rājarṣi Vanamālī Rāya**'s temple of Rādhā-vinoda, and he passed away sitting on his āsana on the night of **Śarat-pūrṇimā**.

## Running

He rose very early and chanted his rounds; then, water-pot in hand, ran for his ablutions and bath, rushed back to put on tilaka with a broken mirror, offered a few sprigs of tulasī to Girirāja at the raised tulasī plant, took his begging cloth and ran off for mādhukarī. In no time he was back to study a hand-written translation of the *Catuḥ-puṣpāñjali*, quickly ate something from his mādhukarī and sat down to bhajana until evening; then again, running all the while, his ablutions and bath, and bhajana again. He ate nothing at night, and no one knew whether he slept at all.

## Advice to Kāminī Ghoṣ

Hearing of his absorption, **Kāminī Kumār Ghoṣ** came to him: "Since childhood I have always felt drowsy, so I cannot do bhajana. Please bless me." The Bābājī answered: "Does a newly married couple fall asleep easily? Your drowsiness will go if, in your meditation, you bring before your mind an ornamented siddha-deha and try to make out the ornaments on each limb." When Kāminī asked whether he might come now and then for darśana of his feet: "Yes — but don't stay long."

## His passing

A few months later, on **Śarat-pūrṇimā** (the full moon of Āśvina), the Vaiṣṇavas of Vraja, after their kīrtana-parikramā of Śrī Kuṇḍa, brought him pāyasa-bhoga and found that he had passed away sitting on his āsana.
