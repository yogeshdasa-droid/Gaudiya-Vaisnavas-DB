## In short

**Viśvarūpa dāsa Bābājī**, a great scholar, renounced and sparing of words, did bhajana as head of the **ṭhaurā of Siddha Ṭoṭārāma dāsa Bābājī** in Śrī Vṛndāvana. With **Rājarṣi Vanamālī Rāya Bāhādur**'s help, and years of begging and hardship, he had **Śyāma-kuṇḍa** cleaned and restored to its beauty — for the first time, it was said, since Śrī Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī.

## Śyāma-kuṇḍa

While Vanamālī Bābu was living at Śrī Kuṇḍa, the Vaiṣṇava committee there drained Śyāma-kuṇḍa to clean it, but ran out of money and could not finish. Viśvarūpa went to see, and on seeing the state of the kuṇḍa he wept, and resolved that it must be cleaned and the money found. The Rājarṣi gave five thousand rupees; the Bābājī made him cashier and supervisor, deposited with him whatever he collected by begging, and the Rājarṣi's secretary, **Kāminī Bābu**, began the work.

Everyone wondered at his determination. He never spent a single paisa of the fund on himself — not even his fare from Vṛndāvana to Śrī Kuṇḍa, nor postage. "If someone asks me to do some dishonest work but gives money for restoring Śrī Kuṇḍa, I will do that dishonest work for him," he would say — but Śrī Śyāmasundara, the life of the Vaiṣṇavas, never let him do any such thing. For two or three years he went through great hardship and personal loss, and in time the work was finished and Śrī Śyāma-kuṇḍa was restored to its original beauty.
