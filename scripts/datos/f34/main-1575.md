## In short

**Upendra Miśra**, of the śrīpāṭa of **Baḍagaṅgā** in Śrīhaṭṭa (Sylhet), was the paternal grandfather of **Lord Caitanya**; in Vraja-līlā he was **Parjanya Gopa** (Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā 35). His wife was **Kalāvatī-devī**, and of their seven sons **Jagannātha Miśra** was the fifth.

## The Lord's visit

When Lord Gaurāṅga was living as a householder He once visited His grandfather's house at Baḍagaṅgā in Sylhet, going along the bank of the Padmā by the villages of Faridpura, Bikramapura, Bādarpura, Egārasindur, Baitālagrāma, Bhiṭādiyā and others. According to Prema-vilāsa 24, Upendra had collected palm leaves to copy a manuscript on Caṇḍī when the Lord suddenly arrived; he went in excitedly to tell his wife, and coming back a few moments later found the manuscript already copied.

## His sons

Kaṁsāri, Paramānanda, Padmanābha, Sarveśvara, Jagannātha, Janārdana and Trailokyanātha. (Jayānanda, p. 87, gives Upendra's name by mistake as Janārdana Miśra.)
