## In short

**Raghunātha Śiromaṇi** («Kāṇā Śiromaṇi», «Kāṇabhaṭṭa»), born at **Pañcakhaṇḍa**, a student of **Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma**, became the great logician of **Navadvīpa** and set up there the first school of Nyāya, in the cattle-shed of the wealthy Hari Ghoṣ. The Advaita-prakāśa (p. 54) tells that Lord Caitanya threw His own commentary on Nyāya into the Gaṅgā so that Raghunātha's commentary on the same subject could spread.

## Life

His family line was Īśāna — Vidyunmālī — Govinda; Govinda had two sons, Raghupati and Raghunātha. Raghunātha studied at Navadvīpa and was later invited to Mithilā. When he had won great distinction in Navadvīpa, King Pratāparudra of Orissa took Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma to Orissa.

## His works

*Cintāmaṇi-dīdhiti*; *Padārtha-khaṇḍana*; *Ātma-tattva-viveka*; ṭīkās on the *Guṇa-kiraṇāvalī* and *Nyāya-līlāvatī*; *Nānārtha-vāda*; *Prāmāṇya-vāda*; *Kṣaṇabhaṅgura-vāda*; *Malimluca-viveka* and others (*Navadvīpa-mahimā*, pp. 130-147).
