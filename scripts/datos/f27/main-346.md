## In short

**Gopāla Bhaṭṭācārya**, younger brother of **Bhagavān Ācārya** («Khañja» Bhagavān), an able statesman with no interest in managing the estate, studied Vedānta at Benares and came back a Māyāvādī. Lord Caitanya felt no joy in meeting him, and when Bhagavān asked **Svarūpa Dāmodara** to hear Gopāla's commentary on Vedānta, Svarūpa refused; the next day Bhagavān sent his brother home (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 2.89-100).

## At Nīlācala

After his studies at Benares Gopāla came to Bhagavān Ācārya's house at Nīlācala, and Bhagavān took him to meet Lord Caitanya. Knowing him to be a Māyāvādī, the Lord felt no jubilation, but because he was Bhagavān's brother He showed pleasure in meeting him.

One day Bhagavān Ācārya said to Svarūpa Dāmodara: "My younger brother Gopāla has come home after studying Vedānta; please hear his commentary." Svarūpa, somewhat angry yet loving, answered: "You have lost your intelligence in Gopāla's company, and so you are eager to hear Māyāvāda. When a Vaiṣṇava hears the Śārīraka-bhāṣya, the Māyāvāda commentary on the Vedānta-sūtra, he gives up the attitude that the Lord is the master and the living being His servant, and thinks himself the Supreme Lord." The next day Bhagavān Ācārya asked Gopāla to go back to his own district.
