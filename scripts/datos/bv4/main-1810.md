## In short

Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Vāmana Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Santoṣa**, then **Sajjana-sevaka Brahmacārī** — grew up from boyhood in the maṭha under **[[Śrīla Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1798|Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Mahārāja]]**, received harināma from **[[Śrīla Bhakti Siddhānta Sarasvatī#ficha-124|Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī]]**, and after 1968 was president-ācārya of the **Śrī Gauḍīya Vedānta Samiti**. Known as a «dictionary of ślokas», he was one of its three pillars with **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Trivikrama Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1552|Trivikrama]]** and **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Nārāyaṇa Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1809|Nārāyaṇa]]** Mahārāja, who called him his śikṣā-guru.

## Family

- Born on **23 December 1921** at **Piljaṅgā** (Khulna district, East Bengal). Father: **Śrī Satiścandra Ghoṣa** (Sarveśvara Dāsādhikārī after dīkṣā from Keśava Mahārāja); mother: **Śrīmatī Bhagavatī Devī**, a strict disciple of Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī. His paternal uncle became **Bhakti Kuśala Nṛsiṁha Mahārāja**, one of Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī's first disciples.
- A boy of extraordinary memory: he knew every school book he had read by heart.

## Life

- On **2 March 1931**, after the Navadvīpa parikramā, his mother entrusted him to **Vinodabihārī Brahmacārī** (Keśava Mahārāja), manager of the maṭha; he studied at the Bhaktivinoda Institute in Māyāpura (headmaster Bhakti Pradīpa Tīrtha), learning ślokas of the *Gauḍīya Kaṇṭhahāra*, Gītā and Bhāgavatam for a piece of chocolate each, and carried out the latrine pots of the maṭha on his head.
- **1936**: harināma from Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī as **Sajjana-sevaka Brahmacārī**.
- When the senior devotees who sided with Keśava Mahārāja were jailed on a false murder charge after 1937, the fifteen-year-old boy, left alone, ran the temple, briefed the lawyers, attended court and cooked for the prisoners twice a day; Keśava Mahārāja gave him **dīkṣā** through the prison bars, his first dīkṣā. The «victim» had died falling from a tree, and the judge apologised. He took the sacred thread as a naiṣṭhika brahmacārī from **[[Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1667|Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara Mahārāja]]**.
- He preached in Bengal with **[[Śrīla Bhakti Dayita Mādhava Gosvāmī#ficha-1794|Bhakti Dayita Mādhava]]** and Bhakti Bhūdeva Śrautī Mahārāja; from 1940 he was his guru's secretary everywhere, and from **1948** he did all the work of the *Śrī Gauḍīya Patrikā*. His hand, crushed in the printing press, healed completely. Shy of speaking to outsiders, he loved telling children stories. In Assam he silenced a hostile sect by reciting fifty ślokas proving that Mahāprabhu is Bhagavān.
- **Sannyāsa**: **Gaura Pūrṇimā 1952**, at Navadvīpa, from Keśava Mahārāja.
- He republished the Gītā with Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa's commentary, *Jaiva-dharma*, *Prema-pradīpa*, *Prabandhāvalī*, *Śaraṇāgati*, *Navadvīpa-bhāva-taraṅga*, *Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu – His Life and Precepts*, *Śrī Caitanya-śikṣāmṛta*, *Śrī Dāmodarāṣṭaka* and more.
- **1968**: president-ācārya of the Samiti after his guru's departure; he opened centres at Purī, Tura (Meghalaya), Dhubri, Guwahati and Silchar (Assam). Grave and quiet, he never accepted praise for his explanations and never spoke of others' faults.
- In 1993 he told Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja that their guru had kept a special service for him within three years — the preaching in the West that began in 1996.
- He left this world in **November 2004**; a mūrti of him was installed at Navadvīpa on 14 March 2006.
- Appearance: **Pauṣa Kṛṣṇa Navamī**; departure: **Kārtika Śukla Tṛtīyā** (Gauḍīya calendar).

## Other versions

- Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja's lecture (1998) says he was born in **1916** in **Jessore**, the **eldest** of four sons, and came to the maṭha at **nine**; the biography says 1921, Khulna, the **second** of four.
- The founding of the Gauḍīya Vedānta Samiti: 1940 (biography), 1941 or 1943 (other texts).
