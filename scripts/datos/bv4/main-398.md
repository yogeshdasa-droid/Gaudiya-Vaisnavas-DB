## In short

Śrīla Gour Govinda Svāmī Mahārāja — **Braja-bandhu** of the Giri family of Gadāi-giri — was an Oriyā disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda#ficha-125|A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda]]**, who gave him sannyāsa in 1975 and sent him to build the Kṛṣṇa-Balarāma temple at **Bhubaneswar**; a profound speaker on the Bhāgavatam and on the meeting of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa as Jagannātha.

## Family

- Born on **2 September 1929** at **Jagannātha-pura**, near Purī (Orissa); he grew up at **Gadāi-giri**. The Giri family have been famous kīrtana singers in Orissa since Śyāmānanda Prabhu's time; three hundred years ago the king of Orissa wrote in the Jagannātha temple register that the kīrtana party of Gadāi-giri should sing for Lord Jagannātha.
- His grandfather, a paramahaṁsa who only chanted and wept before the family Deity **Gopāla Jīu**, taught him to count on his fingers with the mahā-mantra.

## Life

- From six he worshipped Gopāla and ate only what was offered to Him; by eight he had read the Gītā, Bhāgavatam and Caitanya-caritāmṛta and recited the Oriyā Bhāgavata, Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata to the villagers at night.
- After his father's death (1955) he supported the family; he married **Śrīmatī Vasantī Devī**, took his B.A. (second in Utkal University) and B.Ed. studying at night, and was a schoolteacher, speaking of Kṛṣṇa to his pupils, some of whom later became his disciples.
- On **8 April 1974**, aged 45, he left home with a Gītā and a begging bowl, wandered for a year along the Gaṅgā and in the Himalayas debating with māyāvādīs, and walked to Vṛndāvana.
- There he met Bhaktivedānta Svāmī, who asked him: «Have you taken sannyāsa? Then I will give you sannyāsa.» In **1975**, at the opening of the Kṛṣṇa-Balarāma temple, Vṛndāvana, he gave him initiation, the name **Gaura Govinda** and sannyāsa, and sent him to Orissa to translate his books into Oriyā and build a temple on land donated in a jungle at Bhubaneswar.
- He lived in a tea dealer's storeroom and with road workers, collected door to door, and built a mud hut with his own hands; in early **1977** Svāmī Mahārāja refused the state guest house to stay seventeen days in that hut, and laid the foundation stone on Nityānanda's appearance day — his last founded project. The **Śrī Śrī Kṛṣṇa-Balarāma temple** of Bhubaneswar opened in **1991**.
- Meek in person, he «roared like a lion» in his Bhāgavatam classes, explaining one sentence for hours and quoting scripture for everything.
- On **9 February 1996**, Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī's appearance day, at 6 pm, he was explaining to two visitors why Mahāprabhu stayed in Purī, describing the separation and meeting of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in which Kṛṣṇa became Jagannātha, when he left this world.
- Books (from his talks): *Simplicity and Faith*, *How to Find a Sādhu*, *The Embankment of Separation* and others.
- Appearance: **Bhādra Śukla Dvitīyā**; departure: **Phālguna Kṛṣṇa Pañcamī** (Gauḍīya calendar).
