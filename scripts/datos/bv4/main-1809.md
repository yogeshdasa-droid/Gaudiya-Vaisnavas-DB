## In short

Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Nārāyaṇa Gosvāmī Mahārāja — **Nārāyaṇa Tivārī**, initiated **Gaura-Nārāyaṇa**, titled **Bhakta-bāndhava** — was a disciple of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1798|Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Mahārāja]]**, for decades in charge of the **Keśavajī Gauḍīya Maṭha** in Mathurā and of the yearly Vraja-maṇḍala parikramā, translator of Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura into Hindi, and the intimate friend who gave sannyāsa rites and samādhi to **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda#ficha-125|A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī]]**; from 1996 he preached all over the world.

## Family

- Born on the **new-moon day of Māgha, 1921**, at **Tivārīpura** (Buxar district, Bihar), a village of learned brāhmaṇas on the Gaṅgā, where Rāma and Viśvāmitra killed Tāṭakā.
- Father: **Paṇḍita Baleśvaranātha Tivārī**; mother: **Śrīmatī Lakṣmī Devī**; Trivedī brāhmaṇas and Vaiṣṇavas of the Śrī Sampradāya. Called **Bholānātha** as a child for his peaceful nature; he knew the Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata stories by heart.

## Life

- At Buxar High School he was always first or second and a state athletics champion. After college he joined the police. At **Sahibganj** (Bihar) he met **Narottamānanda Brahmacārī** (later Bhakti Kamala Madhusūdana Mahārāja) and other preachers of the Gauḍīya Vedānta Samiti, heard Bhāgavatam all night and began chanting a lakh of names daily.
- Transferred to Rājmahal, near Rāmakeli, he corresponded with Keśava Mahārāja and at the end of **1946** left his post, parents, wife and all.
- **Gaura Pūrṇimā 1947**, after the Navadvīpa parikramā: harināma and dīkṣā from Keśava Mahārāja as **Gaura-Nārāyaṇa**; soon the title *Bhakta-bāndhava*, «friend of the devotees». He travelled everywhere with his guru as his servant, nursed his servant Anaṅgamohana, and heard his guru say that few non-Bengalis grasp Mahāprabhu's prema-tattva as he did.
- **Sannyāsa**: **Gaura Pūrṇimā 1952**, as Bhaktivedānta Nārāyaṇa Mahārāja. From **1954** in charge of the new **Keśavajī Gauḍīya Maṭha**, Mathurā, half the year there and half in Bengal; vice-president of the Samiti and editor of its Hindi publications and the monthly *Śrī Bhāgavata Patrikā*.
- He met **Abhaya Caraṇa** in Calcutta (1947 or 1948), went with his guru to his League of Devotees in Jhansi, and in **1955** brought him to live at the Keśavajī Maṭha; on **17 September 1959** he performed the fire sacrifice at his sannyāsa. He sent him the first mṛdaṅgas and karatālas for America, Deities and books; they corresponded until 1977. At the end Svāmī Mahārāja asked him to help his disciples and to place him in samādhi, which he did on 14 November 1977.
- **1968**: he performed his guru's samādhi rites and began organising the yearly **Kārtika Vraja-maṇḍala parikramā**.
- Hindi translations: *Jaiva-dharma*, *Śrī Caitanya-śikṣāmṛta*, *Śrīman Mahāprabhu kī Śikṣā*, *Bhakti-tattva-viveka*, *Upadeśāmṛta*, *Śikṣāṣṭaka*, *Manaḥ-śikṣā*, *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu-bindu*, *Gauḍīya Kaṇṭhahāra*, the Gītā with Viśvanātha Cakravartī's commentary, *Vaiṣṇava-siddhānta-mālā* and more; his talks were translated into English, French, Spanish, German and other languages.
- His śikṣā-gurus: **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Vāmana Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1810|Vāmana]]** and **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Trivikrama Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1552|Trivikrama]]** Mahārāja, **[[Śrīla Bhakti Hṛdaya Bon Mahārāja#ficha-1795|Bon Mahārāja]]** (whom he knew from 1946) and Bhaktivedānta Svāmī; he was blessed by **[[Bhakti Pramoda Purī Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1799|Bhakti Pramoda Purī Mahārāja]]**.
- From **1996** (first visit to New Vraja, California) he preached in America, Europe, Latin America, Australia, Asia and the Pacific. Founding member of the WVA.
- He left this world in **2010**.
- Appearance: **Māgha Amāvasyā** (Maunī Amāvasyā); departure: **Pauṣa Kṛṣṇa Navamī** (Gauḍīya calendar).

## Other versions

- Birth date: «19 February 1921, on the amāvasyā» (one text) or «7 February 1921, the new moon of Māgha» (another); only the second fits the new moon.
- In a lecture he says he and Trivikrama Mahārāja received sannyāsa on Gaura Pūrṇimā **1954**; the biographies say **1952**.
