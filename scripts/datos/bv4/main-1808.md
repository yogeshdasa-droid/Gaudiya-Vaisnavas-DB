## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Vallabha (Ballabh) Tīrtha Gosvāmī Mahārāja was the disciple and successor of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Dayita Mādhava Gosvāmī#ficha-1794|Bhakti Dayita Mādhava Gosvāmī Mahārāja]]** as president-ācārya of the **Śrī Caitanya Gauḍīya Maṭha** (from 1979), founder-ācārya of **GOKUL** (1997) and vice-president of the World Vaiṣṇava Association.

## Life

- Born on **24 April 1924** at **Amlapatti, Goalpara** (Assam). After a master's degree in philosophy at Calcutta University he joined the Śrī Caitanya Gauḍīya Maṭha in **1947**, served as its secretary and took **sannyāsa in 1961**.
- **1979**: by his guru's nomination he became president-ācārya. He honoured **[[Bhakti Pramoda Purī Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1799|Bhakti Pramoda Purī Mahārāja]]** as his śikṣā-guru, who asked him to preach all over the world.
- From **1997** he toured America (addressing the United Nations in New York), England, Holland, Italy, Austria, Germany, France, Spain, Russia, Slovenia, Ukraine, Australia, Singapore, Bali and Hawaii, with three or four programmes a day; dialogues with theologians of many faiths; a BBC radio interview (2000).
- In August 1999, when his tridaṇḍa was lost at Miami airport, he would neither eat nor drink nor leave until it arrived, saying his guru was with him in the tridaṇḍa and the japa-mālā; when it came: «Now I have got back my life!»
- Known for strict adherence to śāstra («example is better than precept»), humility and affection; he worked eighteen hours a day.
- Books in English: *Śuddha Bhakti*, *Sages of Ancient India*, *A Taste of Transcendence*, *Śrī Caitanya: His Life and Associates*, *Dāśāvatāra*, a *Kārtika Handbook*.
- Appearance: **Caitra Śukla Navamī** (Rāma Navamī); departure: **Vaiśākha Kṛṣṇa Navamī** (Gauḍīya calendar).
