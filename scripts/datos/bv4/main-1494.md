## In short

Śrīla Bhakti Sundara Govinda Dev-Gosvāmī Mahārāja was the dearest disciple and successor of **[[Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva Gosvāmī#ficha-1802|Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Mahārāja]]**, president-sevaite-ācārya of the **Śrī Caitanya Sārasvata Maṭha** (Navadvīpa) from 1986, who toured the world 24 times and opened over a hundred centres on six continents.

## Family

- Born on **18 December 1929** at **Bāmunpāḍā** (Burdwan district, West Bengal), in a brāhmaṇa family of followers of Nityānanda Prabhu; as a boy he served the family Deities **Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Gopīnātha**, installed by Vīracandra Prabhu.

## Life

- At **seventeen** he met Śrīdhara Mahārāja, who at once declared he would prepare him to be the future ācārya of his maṭha. Within two years he received the title **«Vidyā-rañjana»**; he memorised hundreds of songs and thousands of verses (the whole Gītā and Caitanya-caritāmṛta, much of the Bhāgavatam).
- He set up the press and the magazine *Śrī Gauḍīya Darśana*, led pilgrimages, started the yearly **Navadvīpa-dhāma parikramā** of the maṭha (1948), arranged the installation of **Śrī Śrī Gāndharvā-Govindasundara** and built their domed temple; he was also his guru's doctor and confidant. Only he sang *Śrī Rūpa-mañjarī-pada* for his guru.
- Godbrothers of his guru — Bhakti Sāraṅga, **[[Śrīla Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1798|Bhakti Prajñāna Keśava]]**, **[[Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara Gosvāmī Mahārāja#ficha-1667|Bhakti Vicāra Yāyāvara]]**, **[[Śrīla Bhakti Dayita Mādhava Gosvāmī#ficha-1794|Bhakti Dayita Mādhava]]**, Bhakti Kamala Madhusūdana Mahārāja — encouraged him; so did Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī's sister and Bhavānī Didi, the sister of Bhaktivedānta Svāmī.
- In the late 1940s and early 1950s he stayed every year with **[[Śrīla Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda#ficha-125|A.C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī]]** at the Calcutta preaching centre of the maṭha, learning Gītā verses and cooking for him; he was the first distributor of *Back to Godhead*. In the mid-1970s Svāmī Mahārāja invited him to tour the world with him; he declined because of his guru's health.
- In the early 1980s Śrīdhara Mahārāja accepted Western disciples only after he promised to care for them. **1985**: sannyāsa as **Bhakti Sundara Govinda** («my Deity is Govinda and my kuṇḍa is Govinda»); **1986**: president-sevaite-ācārya. His guru said: «In many ways he is more qualified than I am… a natural paramahaṁsa.»
- After 1988 he toured the world 24 times in eighteen years, published over 350 titles in twenty languages, and stressed humility, tolerance and giving honour to others.
- He left this world on **27 March 2010**; his samādhi mandir, the «Temple of Love and Affection», is at the Śrī Caitanya Sārasvata Maṭha, Navadvīpa. He named **Bhakti Nirmala Ācārya Mahārāja** his successor.
- Books: *Religion of the Heart*, *Dignity of the Divine Servitor*, *Golden Reflections*, *Divine Guidance* and others.
