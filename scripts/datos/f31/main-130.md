## In short

**Mahāmahopādhyāya Bhārata Sena** («Bhārata Mallika»), a vaidya scholar of the mid-seventeenth century, wrote commentaries on the great Sanskrit kāvyas and on the Amarakoṣa, works on grammar and Āyurveda, and a **Subodhā ṭīkā on the Gīta-govinda** that the record ranks, for its depth of rasa, above the commentaries of Nārāyaṇa dāsa, Rāṇā Kumbha and Śaṅkara Miśra.

## His works

- Commentaries on the *Kirāta*, *Kumāra(-sambhava)*, *Ghaṭakarpara*, *Naiṣadha*, *Nalodaya*, *Amarakoṣa*, *Bhaṭṭi*, *Meghadūta*, *Śiśupāla(-vadha)* and others.
- *Candraprabhā* and *Ratnaprabhā*: the ancient history of the vaidya caste.
- *Kārakollāsa*, in anuṣṭubh, 107 sections, written, scholars say, in the manner of Jīva Gosvāmī's *Harināmāmṛta-vyākaraṇa*.
- The **Subodhā ṭīkā** on Śrī Gīta-govinda (an incomplete manuscript, chapters 2 to 7, Baṅgīya Sāhitya Pariṣat ms. 39).
- *Drutabodha*, on grammar (Sanskrit Sāhitya Pariṣat ms. 490, 490A), with his own ṭīkā, *Drutabodhinī*.
- On Āyurveda: *Ratna-kaumudī* and *Sāra-kaumudī*.
