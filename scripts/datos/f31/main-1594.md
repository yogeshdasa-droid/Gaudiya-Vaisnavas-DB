## In short

**Vaiṣṇava dāsa Bābājī Mahāśaya of Kosī**, a Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava contemporary of **Siddha Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa Bābājī** of Kāmyavana, lived under the trees with an iron fire-tongs in his hand and had perfection of speech: by his word **Pūrṇānanda Gosvāmī** of Śṛṅgāra-vaṭa had three sons, and at his request the King of Jodhpur built the temple of **Rādhā-Govinda** at Kosī. Told by **Advaita dāsa Bābājī**.

## Under the trees

He always carried an iron fire-tongs, lived beneath the trees and kept his begging bag hung on a branch. **Pūrṇānanda Gosvāmī**, son of Nityānanda of Śṛṅgāra-vaṭa, had no sons; by the words Vaiṣṇava dāsa spoke he had three — **Nṛsiṁhānanda**, **Premānanda** (#1115) and **Yādavānanda**.

Hearing of his power, the **King of Jodhpur** came and begged him to ask for any favour. The Bābājī refused again and again, saying he needed nothing, but at last asked the king to build a temple and install Deities of **Rādhā-Govinda**; the king did, and the temple still stands at Kosī. **Siddha Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābā of Govardhana** would fall flat before Vaiṣṇava dāsa, as he did before Siddha Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa.
