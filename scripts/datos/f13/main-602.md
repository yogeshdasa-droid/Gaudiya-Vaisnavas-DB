## In short

**Jayagopāla Tarkālaṅkāra** (1775–1844), of **Bajarāpura** (Nadia, now Jessore), was the foremost philologist of his time, professor of literature at the Sanskrit College and the teacher of Vidyāsāgara and of the missionaries Carey and Marshman. He revised the first printed Bengali **Rāmāyaṇa** of Kṛttivāsa and **Mahābhārata** of Kāśīrāma dāsa (Śrīrāmapura), and put **Bilvamaṅgala**'s verses into Bengali.

## Life

His father, **Kevalarāma Tarkapañcānana**, was sabhā-paṇḍita at the court of the Rājā of Nāṭor. Jayagopāla studied at Kāśī and became a master of sāhitya-śāstra. He married first in 1795; after his father died at Kāśī in 1803 he faced hard times, until in 1805, at thirty, he found work under **Mr. Carey**. By sheer merit he became professor of literature at the Sanskrit College; **Vidyāsāgara, Tārāśaṅkara, Madanamohana** and **Śrīśacandra** were his students, and he stood foremost among the paṇḍitas of the Supreme Court. He married again at fifty-six; a Vārendra brāhmaṇa, he had no children of his own and adopted a son. His brothers and their sons were all learned in Nyāya, Jyotiṣa and Sāhitya.

He passed away on the **second lunar day of the bright fortnight of Caitra, 1844**.

## The Bengali epics

Carey and Marshman learned Sanskrit and Bengali from him, and then set up the Bengali press at Śrīrāmapura, publishing the first Bengali Rāmāyaṇa and Mahābhārata as revised by Jayagopāla. The versions still current in Bengal bear the stamp of his poetry; he served the poorer Bengalis by giving them the holy books in their language — though by revising the old Rāmāyaṇa he also hid its ancient Bengali. A hymn at the start of the Virāṭa-parva of the Śrīrāmapura Mahābhārata, «said to be by Vyāsa», is in no other edition and may be his own.

## Other works

The Bengali **Hari-bhakty-ātmikā** from Bilvamaṅgala's Sanskrit verses — at the end of which a śloka names **Maheśacandra Caṭṭopādhyāya** of Bajarāpura, apparently at whose command he did it; a Persian dictionary, the **Pārsī Abhidhāna**; and **Ṣaḍ-ṛtu-varṇana**, a set of small poems.
