## In short

**Lakṣmaṇa Paṇḍita**, of **Cātrā** near Śrīrāmapura (Hooghly), was the nephew and disciple of **Kāśīnātha Paṇḍita**, the associate of Lord Gaurāṅga — that is, Kāśīśvara (Kāśīnātha) Paṇḍita of Cātarā (#1832), not Kāśīnātha of Navadvīpa —; **Rudra Paṇḍita** of Vallabhapura was his brother. He married at **Saibonā** (24 Parganas), where his descendants still live and hold a festival every Māgha.

## The three Deities

It is said that **Vīrabhadra**, Nityānanda Prabhu's son, had three Deities carved from one stone and installed them: **Śrī Śyāmasundara** at the śrīpāṭa of Khaḍadaha, **Rādhāvallabha** at Vallabhapura and **Nandadulāla** at Saibonā. The *Vaiṣṇavācāra-darpaṇa*, however, says that the Nandadulāla of Saibonā was installed by the famous Madhu Paṇḍita Mahārāja of Saibonā.
