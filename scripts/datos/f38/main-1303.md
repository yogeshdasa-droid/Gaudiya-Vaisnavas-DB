## In short

**Rasikamohana Vidyābhūṣaṇa** (born 1848, according to the Digdarśanī), of the family line of **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya**'s second daughter, lived more than a hundred years. One of the foremost devotees of Lord Gaurāṅga, learned in Eastern and Western studies, he wrote many Vaiṣṇava books, edited a number of Vaiṣṇava periodicals and was an eloquent interpreter of the Vaiṣṇava śāstras.

## His works

*Rāya Rāmānanda*; *Svarūpa Dāmodara*; *Caraṇa-tulasī*; *Vidyāpati*; *Caṇḍīdāsa*; *Sādhana-saṅketa*; *Śrī Rūpa-Sanātana*; *Śrī Vaiṣṇava*; *Śrī Nityānanda*; *Gambhīrāya Śrī Gaurāṅga*; *Nīlācale Vraja-mādhurī*; *Līlā-mādhurī*; *Gīta-govinda*; *Sānuvāda Sarva-saṁvādinī*.
