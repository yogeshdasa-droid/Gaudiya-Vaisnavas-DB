## In short

**Maṅgarāja Mahāpātra**, an officer of **King Pratāparudra** of Orissa, was ordered by the king, with **Haricandana**, to bring a new boat for **Lord Caitanya** when He left Purī for Vṛndāvana after the Ratha-yātrā and stayed at **Cuttack**; he did so, and went with the Lord together with Haricandana, Rāmānanda and many Oṛiyā and Bengali devotees (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 16.113-116).

## At Cuttack

After the car festival the Lord said He wished to go to Vṛndāvana, and on the way stayed for a time at Cuttack. The king ordered all his subjects to put up new houses for Him, and told Haricandana and Maṅgarāja Mahāpātra to bring a new boat. Maṅgarāja did so and accompanied the Lord.

## Sources

The record's text. It notes that «Maṅgarāja» and «Mahāpātra» may have been the titles of two persons later merged into one (*Bhakti Movement in Orissa*, p. 86).
