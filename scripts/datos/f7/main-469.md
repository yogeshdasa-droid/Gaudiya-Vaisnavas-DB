## In short

**Haridāsa dāsa** (born **Harendra Kumāra Cakravartī**, 1898) of the **Haribol Kuṭīra** in Navadvīpa is the author of the **Śrī Śrī Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna**, the great dictionary of Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava lives and holy places, and the rescuer and editor of many lost Gauḍīya books. He took dīkṣā from **Harimohana Śiromaṇi Prabhu** and veśa from **Giridhārī "Haribol" Sādhu** of the Haribol Kuṭīra; by the order of **Manohara dāsa Bābājī** of Govinda-kuṇḍa he spent his life searching for vanished Gauḍīya literature.

## Scholar

He was born in **September 1898** at **Mahugrāma** in the Pheni subdivision of Noakhali (Bangladesh). His father **Gaganacandra Tarkaratna** and his grandfather **Golakacandra Nyāyaratna** were well-known scholars; his younger brother **Maṇīndrakumāra** left home as a child and, as **Mukunda dāsa Bābājī**, lived fifteen years with him at the Haribol Kuṭīra as his godbrother. A brilliant student, he passed the M.A. in Sanskrit (Vedānta) standing first, with a gold medal. Before that he had received dīkṣā from **Vaiṣṇavācārya Harimohana Śiromaṇi Prabhu**; he taught at the Īśvara Pāṭhaśālā of Comilla only until he had repaid a loan taken for his guru, strict and helpful to his students, punctual and affectionate, and later taught for a while at Comilla College.

## Haridāsa dāsa

Longing to renounce, he went to Navadvīpa and Vṛndāvana and took veśa from **Giridhārī Haribol Sādhu** — so called for his loud "Haribol" kīrtana — becoming **Haridāsa dāsa**; he lived on mādhukarī in Navadvīpa and stayed with his guru at the Haribol Kuṭīra (Giridhārī passed away in Purī in 1944). From then on he gave Giridhārī Haribol as his father's name and never spoke of his family or his degrees: "That man is dead." In Vṛndāvana he lived at **Govinda-kuṇḍa** in strict service, and there **Manohara dāsa Bābājī** mercifully ordered him to search for the lost Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava literature — the work of the rest of his life.

Sureścandra Datta tells (*Śrī Śrī Sudarśana*, Phālguna 1364 BS): having failed to find a manuscript of Sanātana Gosvāmī's *Kṛṣṇa-līlā-stava*, he sat weeping on the bank of the Yamunā crying "O Prabhu Sanātana!", and a bundle of papers floated to the shore — with the ancient manuscript of the *Kṛṣṇa-līlā-stava* in it.

## Works

Among the books he wrote or edited: *Āryā-śatakam*, *Āścarya-rāsa-prabandha*, *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi*, *Aiśvarya-kādambinī*, *Kāvya-kaustubha*, *Kṛṣṇa-janma-tithi-mahotsava-vidhi*, *Kṛṣṇa-virudāvalī*, *Kṛṣṇāhnika-kaumudī*, *Gāyatrī-bhāṣya*, *Gīta-candrodaya*, a commentary on the *Gopāla-tāpanī*, *Gopāla-virudāvalī*, *Gaura-caritra-cintāmaṇi*, *Gaurāṅga-virudāvalī*, *Camatkāra-candrikā*, *Caitanya-mātā-mañjuṣā*, *Chandaḥ-kaustubha*, *Daśaślokī-bhāṣya*, *Dāna-keli-cintāmaṇi*, *Durlabha-sāra*, *Nikuñja-keli-virudāvalī*, *Paddhati*, *Kṛṣṇa-bhakti-ratna-prakāśa*, *Bhakti-sāra-pradarśinī*, *Mathurā-māhātmya*, *Mādhava-mahotsava*, a commentary on the *Yoga-sāra-stava*, *Govinda-rati-mañjarī*, *Rādhā-kṛṣṇārcana-dīpikā*, *Kṛṣṇa-līlā-stava*, *Virudāvalī-lakṣaṇa*, *Vṛndāvana-mahimāmṛta*, *Śyāmānanda-śataka*, *Rasāmṛta-śeṣa*, *Sādhana-dīpikā*, *Siddhānta-darpaṇa*, *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, *Svātma-pramodinī*, the *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Jīvanī* and the *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*.
