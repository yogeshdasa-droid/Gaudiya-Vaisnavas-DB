## In short

**Mādhava dāsa Bābājī** (1857–1933), born at **Baradhariyā** near Rāmakeli, at the meeting of the Mahānandā and the Padmā, was the favourite disciple of **Premānanda Gosvāmī** of Śṛṅgāra-vaṭa in Vṛndāvana, who gave him veśa and, later, a second dīkṣā; he took **Rādhikānātha Gosvāmī** as his śikṣā-guru. Humble, a tireless nurse of sick Vaiṣṇavas and a lover of the scriptures, he lived some twenty years at Gopīnātha Bāgh, a place called **Siddha-pīṭha**.

## To Vraja

He lost his father as a child and, his mother's only son, had little schooling, but loved the company of saints, bhakti books and saṅkīrtana. At twenty-seven he left his loving, childless wife and went to Vṛndāvana, to **Premānanda Prabhupāda of Śṛṅgāra-vaṭa**, who gave him veśa as **Mādhava dāsa**; when he found that the mantra of his first dīkṣā in his village was not right, Premānanda, after testing him with the whole charge of Nityānanda's festival, had him pick a flower from the temple courtyard and gave him dīkṣā. In the guru's family he was treated like a widowed daughter, and the Gosvāminīs taught him to cook for the festivals. When his wife came to Vṛndāvana and he wanted to go off to other villages, his guru said: "She will do bhajana in her way and you in yours; why leave Vṛndāvana?"

## Through Vraja

With his guru's blessing he lived with **Jagadānanda**, disciple of Siddha Nityānanda dāsa, at Saṅketa, at Varṣāṇā by Bhānu-kuṇḍa and at Kāmyavana by Vimalā-kuṇḍa, each begging separately and eating together. Once, feeling he had failed in bhajana, he stopped going for mādhukarī and lay in his hut — and Rādhārāṇī came as a brāhmaṇa girl and fed him. He lived with **Dharma dāsa Bābājī** at the temple of Śrī Madanagopāla at Kāmyavana, once served by Siddha Jayakṛṣṇa dāsa, and later the Vaiṣṇavas made him mahānta of Siddha Bābā's monastery there, a post he kept for a time out of obedience. Deciding once at Jhāḍu-maṇḍala to take only what came by itself, trusting Sanātana Gosvāmī, he was called in at Gopāla Bhaṭṭa's monastery to offer the bhoga for its sick priest. At Varṣāṇā he kept company with Rāmakṛṣṇa dāsa Bābājī, Haricaraṇa dāsa Bābājī and Haṁsa dāsa Bābājī of the Nimbārka line. He never let a day pass without studying śāstra; he nursed Jagadānanda at Varṣāṇā to the end, and his guru at Agra in 1895.

## Rādhikānātha and his friends

When **Rādhikānātha Gosvāmī** came to Vṛndāvana, Mādhava was enchanted by his Hari-kathā and took him as śikṣā-guru, though Rādhikānātha treated him as an intimate friend. Through him he met **Rājarṣi Vanamālī Rāya** (1894), his inseparable friend for more than twenty years, from whom he never took money — he begged only from Rādhikānātha. Siddha Jagadīśa Bābā loved him; when **Rādhāramaṇa-caraṇa dāsa** came to Vṛndāvana he was pleased with him, and his disciple **Rāmadāsa Bābājī** came to regard Mādhava dāsa as his guru. He would never sit in the same row as the Vaiṣṇavas when taking prasāda. After Rādhikānātha's passing in 1911 he lived at Dillīra Bāgān near his samādhi; there the silent Vedāntic sannyāsī **Nārāyaṇa Muni** learned Bengali to read the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* with him, and the sannyāsī **Kṛṣṇānanda**, sent by him to the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, took dīkṣā from Prāṇagopāla Gosvāmī and regarded Mādhava as his guru. The young Rājā of Munger, **Raghunandana Prasād Siṁha**, became his intimate; once Mādhava told him: "When I die, don't give money for my funeral — give it to me now, and I'll spend it on kīrtana and Kṛṣṇa-kathā," and the king gave him five hundred rupees and held many festivals at his place in the ṭhaurā of Gopāla Chaḍīdār at Gopīnātha Bāgh, which came to be called Siddha-pīṭha.

In **1933**, with his head on the lap of **Prāṇagopāla**, listening to kīrtana, he entered the eternal Vṛndāvana.
