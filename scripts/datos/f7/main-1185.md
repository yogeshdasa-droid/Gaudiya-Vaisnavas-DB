## In short

**Rādhikānātha Gosvāmī** of **Śāntipura**, a descendant of **Śrī Advaita Prabhu** — son of **Rāmacandra Gosvāmī**, tenth in Advaita's line and a famous logician — was a great teacher of the Bhāgavatam and of rāgānugā bhajana in Vṛndāvana at the end of the nineteenth century. Gaura Kiśora Śiromaṇi, Mādhava dāsa Bābājī, Svāmī Saccidānanda Vrajabālā and Rājarṣi Vanamālī Rāya were close to him. He passed away in **1911**.

## Burma

He lost his parents at seventeen and studied the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam with **Madana-gopāla Gosvāmī**. A brāhmaṇa from Burma, Rājavallabha Cakravartī, took him there for a few months; the King of Burma made him court paṇḍita, gave him a gold leaf inscribed "Śrī Gosvāmī Paṇḍita Rāja-guru", a gold crown and a gold sacred thread (some twenty and forty tolās). In **1877**, when an epidemic broke out in Burma, he came back to Bengal and married; he went back once more, but his patron had died and the country was in turmoil, so he returned.

## Vṛndāvana

At thirty-three he went to Vṛndāvana, came back, and in **1891** went again with his family and **Rājarṣi Bahādur**; after moving several times between Vṛndāvana and Śāntipura he settled in Vṛndāvana, serving in a house near **Keśī-ghāṭa** given by Gaurahari dāsa and Gaurakiśora dāsa, spending his days in the company of Vaiṣṇavas, speaking of Kṛṣṇa and explaining the scriptures; once, when plague broke out, he went back to Śāntipura for two and a half years. When he left Vraja for Bengal in 1890, Gaura Kiśora Śiromaṇi wept at Mathurā station till he fainted, and died a few days later (record of Śiromaṇi). He taught **Saccidānanda Vrajabālā** to worship as a gopī — Kṛṣṇa Himself once led Vrajabālā to his door (record of Vrajabālā) — and **Mādhava dāsa Bābājī** took him as his śikṣā-guru, though Rādhikānātha treated him as a friend. At fifty-six, ill with heart trouble, he took **sannyāsa** on the bank of the Yamunā on the day of Dola-pūrṇimā. Before his passing he built a secluded bhajana-kuṭīra at **Keyārī-vana**, near Rājarṣi Bahādur's temple, where his samādhi now is.

## Works

He wrote the *Rahasyārtha-prakāśikā*, a commentary on Rūpa Gosvāmī's *Nikuñja-rahasya-stava*, translated into Bengali Viśvanātha Cakravartī's *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-bhāvanāmṛta*, *Camatkāra-candrikā* and *Saṅkalpa-kalpadruma*, and wrote a *Stava* and a *Gītāvalī*. It is said that once, as he came back from bathing in the Yamunā, **Viśvanātha Cakravartī** walked home with him and accepted his hospitality; Viśvanātha's seat is still worshipped at Keśī-ghāṭa.
