## In short

**Rāmadāsa Bābājī of Varṣāṇā** — before his renunciation **Śrī Rameśacandra De** — was initiated in the family of **Śrīla Abhirāma Gopāla** and worshipped in sakhya-rasa; in Vraja he was drawn to rāgānugā bhajana by the mercy and company of **Siddha Jagannātha dāsa Bābājī**. He did bhajana at Varṣāṇā, near **Bhānu-kuṇḍa** and at last in **Loṭana-kuñja**, a sādhaka of iron resolve.

## At Varṣāṇā

His fame spread quickly and many came to serve him. Seeing him doing bhajana in the open, the **Queen of Tāḍāś** built him a kuṭīra near Bhānu-kuṇḍa, and he lived there; but because so many people came, the local paṇḍās criticized him as one who had gathered a fortune, and these disturbances hampered his bhajana. So he left that hut for a new one in **Loṭana-kuñja**, built by **Dr. Bipin Bihārī dāsa**, and did bhajana there to the end.

## The chopper

He made vows, wrote them in his diary and kept them strictly. Once **Haridāsa dāsa**, the author of the *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Jīvana*, saw a chopper under his pillow and asked about it. Rāmadāsa Bābājī said that on the day he deviated from the way of bhajana shown by Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, he would behead himself with it.
