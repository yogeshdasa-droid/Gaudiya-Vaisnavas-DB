## In short

**Svarūpa dāsa Bābājī** («Svarūpadāsajī») lived at the **Sātāsana maṭha** in Jagannātha Purī, near the samādhi of **Haridāsa Ṭhākura**, where many Vaiṣṇavas have done bhajana in seclusion. Blind, yet bathing alone in the sea before dawn, he spent the day in his kuṭīra and the evening chanting and dancing before Tulasī, and was honoured as a saint of the highest attainment.

## His day

He stayed the whole day inside his kuṭīra. In the evening he came out into the open yard, offered obeisances to Tulasī, and sang, danced and chanted the holy name, while many Vaiṣṇavas waited there to meet him. Some visitor would always offer him a handful of Jagannātha's prasāda, and he took only what was needed to satisfy his hunger; others read to him from books like the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*. About ten at night he went back to his kuṭīra for bhajana, and very early, while it was still dark, he went to the sea for his morning ablutions — blind as he was, he managed to bathe in the sea alone, for he did not like to accept service from others.

He had no attachment to wealth, welcomed every visitor with pleasant, charming words and advised everyone always to remember the holy name of Kṛṣṇa.
