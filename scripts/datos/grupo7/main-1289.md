## In short

Śrī **Rāmānujācārya**, the great ācārya of the **Śrī-sampradāya** and teacher of **Viśiṣṭādvaita**, wrote the **Śrī-bhāṣya** on the *Brahma-sūtra*, the *Vedārtha-saṅgraha*, the *Vedānta-sāra*, the *Vedānta-dīpa*, the *Gītā-bhāṣya* and the *Gadya-traya*, and stood against Śaṅkara's Advaita. He is held to be an incarnation of **Lakṣmaṇa** (Śeṣa). He was born at **Śrīperumbudūr** (Tamil Nadu) in **938 Śaka** (1017) on **Caitra śukla pañcamī** (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*).

## Life

Son of **Āsuri Keśava** (Keśavācārya) and **Kāntimatī**, he was named **Lakṣmaṇa**. He studied Vedānta at Kāñcī with **Yādavaprakāśa**, an Advaitin, but disagreed with his teacher's explanations and was estranged from him (it is said Yādavaprakāśa even plotted his death). **Yāmunācārya** of Śrī Raṅgam wished to meet him but passed away; three fingers of his body stayed closed, and opened only when Rāmānuja vowed to fulfil his three wishes — to write a commentary on the *Brahma-sūtra*, to honour Parāśara and Vyāsa, and to spread the glory of Nammāḻvār. He received the pañca-saṁskāra from **Mahāpūrṇa** (Periya Nambi) and took sannyāsa at Kāñcī; with the śūdra-born saint **Kāñcīpūrṇa** he lived in great humility. He became the ācārya of **Śrī Raṅgam**; when **Goṣṭhīpūrṇa** gave him the secret mantra he shouted it from the temple tower to everyone, ready to go to hell if they were saved.

## Preaching

He travelled all over India, established the worship of Nārāyaṇa in many temples (Tirupati, Melukoṭe) and wrote his works. Persecuted by the Coḷa king, he lived for years at **Melukoṭe** (Yādavādri) in Karnataka, where the Hoysaḷa king Viṣṇuvardhana became his follower; his disciple **Kūreśa** lost his eyes for him. He is said to have lived a hundred and twenty years (to 1137). From his line came Rāmānanda Svāmī, twenty-one generations later (*Abhidhāna*, «Rāmānanda»).

## Lord Caitanya and the Śrī-sampradāya

On His tour of the South Lord Caitanya stayed four months at Śrī Raṅgam with **Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa**, of the Śrī-sampradāya, the father of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 9).

## Appearance and disappearance

**Appearance:** Caitra śukla pañcamī, 938 Śaka (1017), at Śrīperumbudūr (*Abhidhāna*; Gauḍīya calendar).
**Disappearance:** Māgha śukla daśamī (Gauḍīya calendar); 1137.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Lakṣmaṇa** / Ananta Śeṣa.
