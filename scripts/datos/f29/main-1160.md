## In short

**Rādhāmohana Vidyāvācaspati**, seventh in descent from **Advaita Prabhu**, was the foremost of the learned paṇḍitas of **Śāntipura** in the eighteenth century, famed in Bengal and beyond for his commentaries on Smṛti and Nyāya and his works on Navya-nyāya. He wrote many books on the subtle theology of Vaiṣṇava rituals and practice, among them a ṭippanī on the *Tattva-sandarbha*.

## Life

He was most likely born in the fourth decade of the eighteenth century: **King Kṛṣṇacandra** of Navadvīpa gave him a grant of land dated 1167 BS (1762).

## His works

**Bhāgavata-tattva-sāra** (manuscript, 17 pages: explanations of some disputed ślokas of the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam); **Tattva-saṅgraha** (ms., 54 pages); **Bhakti-rahasya** (in the book *Śāntipura-paricaya*); **Kṛṣṇa-bhakti-sudhārṇava** (ms., 186 pages; Baṅgīya Sāhitya Pariṣat ms. 896, 205); **Śrī Kṛṣṇārcana-candrikā** (BSP ms. 897); **Tattva-dīpikā** (BSP ms. 177, 326, 335); **Kṛṣṇa-bhajana-krama-saṅgraha**; **Tattva-sandarbha-ṭippanī** (published in Calcutta, Devakīnandana Press, Caitanyābda 433); **Kṛṣṇa-tattvāmṛta**; **Kṛṣṇa-bhakti-rasodaya**.
