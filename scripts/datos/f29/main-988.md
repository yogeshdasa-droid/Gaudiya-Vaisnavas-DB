## In short

**Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍitācārya** (1287–1350), of **Viṣṇumaṅgala**, was the third and youngest son of **Trivikrama Ācārya** (Trivikrama Tīrtha), Madhvācārya's disciple. A devotee, poet and philosopher like his father, he wrote some twenty books, the most famous being the **Madhva-vijaya**, the life of Madhvācārya, and its commentary, the **Madhva-vijaya-bhāva-prakāśikā**.

## The Madhva-vijaya

In his books he makes it clear that he was a small boy while the great Madhvācārya was preaching and showing his pastimes. Trivikrama Tīrtha told his son these stories, and Nārāyaṇa put into verse the life, travels and dynamic preaching of Madhvācārya as recorded in Trivikrama's diary. Since Madhva's generally accepted birth year is 1238, he was forty-nine when Nārāyaṇa was born, and Nārāyaṇa would have spent thirty years in the company of Madhvācārya and his disciples.

His father Trivikrama was the author of the *Hari-vāyu-stuti*, the *Uṣā-haraṇa* and the famous *Tattva-pradīpa*. After Trivikrama's death, Nārāyaṇa's younger brother took sannyāsa and became known as **Viṣṇu Tīrtha** (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Madhya 9.245, purport).
