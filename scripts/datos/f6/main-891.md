## In short

**Mahānidhi Svāmī** (now **Mahānidhi Madana-gopāla dāsa Bābājī**), born on **6 February 1951** in New Jersey (United States), a disciple of Śrīla **A. C. Bhaktivedānta Svāmī Prabhupāda** since 1975, has lived for some thirty years in India, at **Śrī Rādhākuṇḍa**, writing and teaching on Śrī Guru, Mahāprabhu, Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa, the holy places, the dīkṣā-mantras and rāgānugā-bhakti; he has written 25 books in English and 11 in Hindi.

## Life

He grew up by the sea in New Jersey, studied medicine at Rutgers University (1969–1973) and went on to Los Angeles. A vegetarian from eighteen, he read many spiritual books and met many teachers, and from the *Bhagavad-gītā*, the *Īśopaniṣad* and the teachings of Mahāprabhu understood that life is meant only for love of God and the eternal service of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa. In **1975** Śrīla Prabhupāda accepted him as a disciple, as **Mahānidhi dāsa**. After ten years as a celibate preacher he took ISKCON sannyāsa in **1985** as **Mahānidhi Svāmī**. In America he distributed the *Gītā* and *Bhāgavatam* (1975–1981), organised ISKCON's first Ratha-yātrā in **Washington D.C.** (1981) and was president of the Baltimore temple (1982–1986).

## Rādhākuṇḍa

In **1977** he visited Navadvīpa, Purī and Vṛndāvana; seeing Rādhākuṇḍa for the first time he felt a hidden memory of a past life there, and sitting at **Mānasa-pāvana-ghāṭa** prayed to Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī to let him one day live on the bank of her lake. He came every year until 1986, and in **1987**, consumed by "Vṛndāvana fever", moved there for good; in 1998 he became an Indian citizen. From 1989 to 1992 he did bhajana in Śrīla Prabhupāda's rooms at **Sevā-kuñja**, in the Rādhā-Dāmodara temple, and in **2002** moved to Rādhākuṇḍa. In **2014** the 99-year-old **Paṇḍita Kṛṣṇadāsa Bābājī Mahārāja**, who had done bhajana in Vraja for seventy-seven years, gave him bābājī-veśa in the **"Advaita Parivāra"** line of Śrī Advaita Prabhu, with the name **Mahānidhi Madana-gopāla dāsa Bābājī**.

Every Kārttika since 1994 he has spoken on Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī, her mañjarīs and the pastimes of Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa, and in each Puruṣottama month; his *Rāsa-līlā* kathā of 2012 was broadcast twice a day on Sādhana TV. He holds a "Sunday Satsaṅga" of questions and answers at his Rādhākuṇḍa āśrama. His books have been translated into Russian, Lithuanian, Macedonian, Chinese, Spanish, Italian, French, Marathi, Hindi, Bengali and other languages; among them *Ānanda Vṛndāvana Campū* (Kavi Karṇapūra's book), *Appreciating Navadvīpa Dhāma*, *Appreciating Śrī Vṛndāvana Dhāma*, *The Art of Chanting*, *Bhāvanā-sāra-saṅgraha*, *Samādhis*, *Gāyatrī Mahimā Mādhurī*, *Jagannātha Purī Handbook*, *Rādhā-kuṇḍa Mahimā Mādhurī* and *Śrī Caitanya-maṅgala*.
