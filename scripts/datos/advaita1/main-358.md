## In short

Gopāla Miśra was the third son and a disciple of Śrī Advaita Ācārya (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.19). In his past life he was **Gaṇeśa**.

## Life

According to the *Advaita-prakāśa* (11) he was born on the **śukla daśamī of Kārtika** (about 1500 CE?). He was born with his eyes closed; Advaita called out loudly "Gaura Hari!", and the child opened them. As a baby he would stop drinking his mother's milk to listen to the chanting of the holy name, and cry when it stopped.

At the cleaning of the Guṇḍicā temple in Purī, Gopāla fainted in ecstasy while dancing. Advaita chanted the Nṛsiṁha mantra and tried everything, but could not revive him and was grief-stricken. The Lord put His hand on the boy's chest and said: "Gopāla, get up!" — and hearing the Lord's voice he rose at once (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.21–25).
