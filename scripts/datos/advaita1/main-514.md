## In short

Īśāna Nāgara was a brāhmaṇa of Śrī Advaita Ācārya's branch, brought up in Advaita's house, and the author of the **Advaita-prakāśa** (1568 CE), the life of his master. He was born in **1414 Śaka (1492 CE)** at **Navagrāma**, Lāuḍ pargana, Śrīhaṭṭa (Sylhet, Bangladesh).

## Life

He lost his father when he was five, and his widowed mother took him to Advaita's house at Śāntipura; Advaita arranged his education.

He was very fiery. Once, when he was about to touch Lord Caitanya's feet, the Lord saw his sacred thread and forbade him, since he was a brāhmaṇa; Īśāna at once tore off his thread.

By the order of Sītā Devī he married at the age of **seventy**. His wife is said to have been from **Teotha**, on the bank of the Padmā; they had three sons, **Puruṣottama Nāgara**, Harivallabha Nāgara and Kṛṣṇavallabha Nāgara. His descendants live at Goālanda and Bāṅkpāl near Teotha; the royal family and the Bāgchis of Teotha are disciples of his line. He wrote the *Advaita-prakāśa* at Lāuḍ in **1568 CE**.
