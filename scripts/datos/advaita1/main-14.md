## In short

Acyutānanda was the eldest son of Śrī Advaita Ācārya and Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī, a branch of Śrī Caitanya and the dīkṣā disciple of **Gadādhara Paṇḍita** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 87–88). From childhood he knew Lord Caitanya as the Lord, and his view is the one the Vaiṣṇavas accept: "What Acyuta holds is the essence" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.20). "I praise Acyutānanda, full of the bliss of rasa, dearest to Gadādhara, son of Śrī Advaita" (*Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* 14). His Śrīpāṭa is **Śāntipura**. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Kārttikeya** and the gopī **Acyutā**.

## Life

He was born of Sītā Devī in **1425 or 1426 Śaka** (1503/1504 CE); B. V. Tīrtha gives 1428 Śaka.

**Who is the Lord's guru?** When he was about five, a sannyāsī at Śāntipura asked Advaita how Keśava Bhāratī was related to Lord Caitanya. Advaita, speaking in the ordinary way, said Bhāratī was His guru. Little Acyuta was angry: "You are the friend of all the living beings and the father of all — it is written in the Vedas," and he explained the truth about Śrī Caitanya; everyone was delighted to hear such a child (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 4.138–205).

When the Lord came to Śāntipura, Acyuta, covered in dust and laughing, ran to see His feet and rolled at them, and the Lord took him on His lap (Antya 1.213–220). When the Lord punished Advaita, Acyuta wept to see his father's devotion (Madhya 19). He spent many days with the Lord in Purī (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.150), danced before the Ratha-yātrā cart (Madhya 13.45) and at Guṇḍicā (Madhya 14.71), and in the seven-party kīrtana (Antya 10.60). He was at the **Kheturī** festival.

He never married. His brother Kṛṣṇa Miśra had two sons, Raghunātha and Dolagovinda; Raghunātha's line lives at Madana-gopāla-pāḍā in Śāntipura.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Kārttikeya** and the gopī **Acyutā** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 87–88).

## Other persons of the same name

Acyutānanda the Oriya poet, and Rājā Acyutānanda of Rayaṇī on the Subarṇarekhā, disciple of Śyāmānanda and father of Rasika Murāri — other persons.
