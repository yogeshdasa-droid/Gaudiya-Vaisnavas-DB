## In short

Kṛṣṇa Miśra (Kṛṣṇadāsa Miśra) was the second son of Śrī Advaita Ācārya and Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī, a branch of Advaita: "Kṛṣṇa Miśra, the Ācārya's son, in whose heart Śrī Caitanya always sits" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.18).

## Life

The *Advaita-prakāśa* (11) says he was born of Sītā on the **kṛṣṇa trayodaśī of Caitra**, about 1418 Śaka. The *Prema-vilāsa* tells that when Sītā's sister Śrī lost her newborn son and wept, Sītā, with Advaita's permission, put Kṛṣṇadāsa in Śrī's arms: "I give you this son; he will be yours" — so Śrī brought him up (the *Abhidhāna* notes that the two books do not agree).

After the Lord left this world Advaita gave **Kṛṣṇa Miśra** the service of his Deity (*Advaita-prakāśa*). When Vīrabhadra Gosvāmī came to Śāntipura, Kṛṣṇa Miśra received him with great care and led him in kīrtana to the Advaita-bhavana (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 13).

His sons were **Raghunātha** and **Dolagovinda**. Raghunātha's descendants still live at **Madana-gopāla-pāḍā** in Śāntipura; Dolagovinda's three sons went to Māldaha, and in that line, generations later, came the sādhaka Vīracandra Gosvāmī, who took sannyāsa and installed a Deity of Lord Caitanya at Katwa. A *Kṛṣṇa-miśra-caritra* about him is known.
