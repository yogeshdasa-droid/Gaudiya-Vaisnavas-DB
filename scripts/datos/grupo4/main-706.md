## In short

Śrī **Kavi Karṇapūra** (**Paramānanda Sena**, **Purīdāsa**), youngest son of **Śivānanda Sena**, was the great poet of the Lord's circle: author of the **Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka**, the **Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā**, the **Ānanda-vṛndāvana-campū**, the **Alaṅkāra-kaustubha** and many other works. His guru was **Śrīnātha Paṇḍita**, a disciple of Advaita. In Vraja he is the sakhī **Guṇacūḍā** (*Vaiṣṇavācāra-darpaṇa*).

## Birth and names

Before his birth the Lord told Śivānanda in Purī: "name your next son Purīdāsa". He was born in **1524** (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*; Śrīla Prabhupāda: 1448 Śaka, 1527). His home was **Kāñcanapāḍā**. As a baby in Purī he sucked the Lord's toe. At seven, asked by the Lord to say something, he spoke a verse of his own: *śravasoḥ kuvalayam akṣṇor añjanam urasaḥ mahendra-maṇi-dāma / vṛndāvana-ramaṇīnāṁ maṇḍanam akhilaṁ harir jayati* — "Glory to Hari, the blue lotus of the ears, the collyrium of the eyes, the sapphire necklace on the breast — the whole ornament of the women of Vṛndāvana". Svarūpa Dāmodara named him **Karṇapūra**, "the delight of the ears" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Antya 16.65–76). Once the Lord asked him to say "Kṛṣṇa" and he would not; Svarūpa understood that he kept the mantra the Lord had given him in his mind (Antya 16).

## Works

*Śrī Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* (1494 Śaka, 1572), and four years later the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*; *Ānanda-vṛndāvana-campū* (opening with praise of his guru Śrīnātha Paṇḍita); *Caitanya-carita-mahākāvya*; *Alaṅkāra-kaustubha*; *Kṛṣṇāhnika-kaumudī*; *Āryā-śataka*; a commentary on the tenth canto; a *Caitanya-sahasra-nāma-stotra*; *Rādhā-kṛṣṇa-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (*Abhidhāna*). Bhaktisiddhānta held that the Sanskrit *Caitanya-carita* is by his eldest brother Caitanya dāsa.

## His brothers

**Caitanya dāsa** and **Rāmadāsa** — the parrots Dakṣa and Vicakṣaṇa (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 145).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Guṇacūḍā sakhī** (*Vaiṣṇavācāra-darpaṇa*: "Guṇacūḍā sakhī is Kavi Karṇapūra, living at Kāñcaḍāpāḍā").

## Not to be confused

A **Kavi Karṇapūra** of Khaṇḍaghoṣa is the father of a translator of the *Gīta-govinda*. The record had a short entry headed «Paramānanda Purī» that is about him.
