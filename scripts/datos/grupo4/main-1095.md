## In short

Śrīpāda **Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī**, a tridaṇḍī sannyāsī of the Śrī-Raṅgam family of **Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa** — his brother — was the uncle and guru of **Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī**. He became a great devotee of Lord Caitanya and lived in **Vṛndāvana**, writing the **Śrī Caitanya-candrāmṛta**, the **Rādhā-rasa-sudhānidhi** and the **Vṛndāvana-mahimāmṛta**. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he is **Tuṅgavidyā** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 163).

## Works

*Śrī Vṛndāvana-mahimāmṛta*, *Śrī Rādhā-rasa-sudhānidhi*, *Śrī Caitanya-candrāmṛta*, *Saṅgīta-mādhava*, *Āścarya-rāsa-prabandha*, commentaries on the *Śruti-stuti*, the Kāma-bīja and Kāma-gāyatrī and the *Gīta-govinda*, *Śrī Gaura-sudhākara-citrāṣṭaka*, the *Navadvīpa-śataka* and others (manuscripts at the Pāṭbāḍī) (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). Growse (*Mathura*) gave the *Rādhā-rasa-sudhānidhi* to Śrī Hita Harivaṁśa; of the two manuscripts at the Govinda library of Jaipur one has two extra verses on Mahāprabhu and the other bears Harivaṁśa's name; the *Abhidhāna*, from the language and the mood of the sakhī, gives it to Prabodhānanda. The Hindi *Bhaktamāla* praises him: "Śrī Prabodhānanda, great rasika, a root of joy, dear to Śrī Caitanya-candra…".

## "Whoever reads the *Caitanya-candrāmṛta*…"

The *Gauḍīya* weekly (24.50) says: reading Kavi Karṇapūra one becomes greedy for the lotus feet of Caitanya-candra; reading Prabodhānanda's *Caitanya-candrāmṛta* a faithful soul gets one-pointed faith in Gaura's feet; reading the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, the most fortunate become eager to serve Gaurahari as Svarūpa, Rāmānanda, Sanātana, Haridāsa, Rūpa and Raghunātha served Him (*Abhidhāna*).

## Not to be confused

Not **Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī**, the Māyāvādī leader of Kāśī who took shelter of the Lord (Whisper, 06-11-2026: two persons; the Gauḍīya Maṭha holds they were one).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Tuṅgavidyā** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 163).
