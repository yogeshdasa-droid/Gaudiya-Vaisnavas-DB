## In short

Śrī **Narahari Sarakāra Ṭhākura** (Narahari dāsa) of **Śrīkhaṇḍa**, a Vaidya, was one of the Lord's most intimate companions: with Gadādhara Paṇḍita he was always with Him and fanned Him with the cāmara (Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura: *narahari ādi kori cāmara ḍhulāya*). In Vraja he is **Madhumatī**, Rādhā's dear sakhī, keeper of the honey. "The residents of Śrīkhaṇḍa — Mukunda dāsa, Śrī Raghunandana, Narahari dāsa, Cirañjīva and Sulocana — are great branches" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.78–79). His disciple **Locana dāsa** wrote the *Caitanya-maṅgala*.

## Birth and family

He was born in **1401 or 1402 Śaka** at Śrīkhaṇḍa, four miles west of Kāṭoyā (Burdwan). Father: **Nārāyaṇa dāsa** (Nārāyaṇa Deva); mother: **Śrī Goyī Devī** (as our copy of the *Abhidhāna* reads), daughter of Murāri Sena; brothers: **Mukunda** (father of **Raghunandana Ṭhākura**, from whom the Ṭhākura family of Śrīkhaṇḍa spreads) and **Mādhava** (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 11.730; *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). When their father died, Mukunda arranged his studies in Navadvīpa and went as physician to the Sultan of Gauḍa; Narahari soon became learned and a knower of bhakti-rasa. Before meeting the Lord he wrote songs on the pastimes of Rādhā-Govinda in Sanskrit and Bengali.

## With the Lord

He was in all the Lord's pastimes in Navadvīpa and went to Purī every year; he alone could sing the glories of Gaura in His presence. Once the Lord and Nityānanda came to Śrīkhaṇḍa and asked him for honey; he turned the water of a pond into honey — it is still called **Madhu-puṣkariṇī** (*Abhidhāna*). By the Lord's order in a dream he had three deities of Gaura made: they are served at **Śrīkhaṇḍa, Kāṭoyā and Gaṅgānagara**. With Dvija Haridāsa he spoke the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-caitanya-sahasra-nāma*, made known by Lokānandācārya.

## Writings

*Bhakti-candrikā-paṭala*, *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-bhajanāmṛta* (on the honour due to Vaiṣṇavas, the dīkṣā- and śikṣā-guru, and when a guru may be given up), *Śrī Caitanya-sahasra-nāma*, *Śacīnandanāṣṭaka*, *Rādhāṣṭaka*, and many songs, "like nectar", some in the *Padakalpataru*. His Gaura songs are hard to tell from those of **Narahari Cakravartī**, who quotes them in the *Bhakti-ratnākara*.

## Disappearance

About 1540, on **Agrahāyaṇa kṛṣṇa ekādaśī**: "the Agrahāyaṇa kṛṣṇa ekādaśī is above all, when Ṭhākura Narahari disappeared" (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 9.513). At his festival **Śrīnivāsa Ācārya** was in charge and **Vīrabhadra Gosvāmī** came; by his mercy a blind man saw. His descendants' disciples were many in Purnea (Buchanan Hamilton). One text says his samādhi is at Rādhā-kuṇḍa.

## Appearance and disappearance

**Appearance:** 1401 or 1402 Śaka (1479–1480).
**Disappearance:** Agrahāyaṇa kṛṣṇa ekādaśī, c. 1540 (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 9.513; *Abhidhāna*). The record had "śukla".

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Madhumatī**, Rādhā's prāṇa-sakhī who supplies honey (*Caitanya-maṅgala*; *Bhakti-ratnākara*).
