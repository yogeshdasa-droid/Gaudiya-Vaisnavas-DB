## In short

Śrī **Vāsudeva Ghoṣa**, the great singer and poet of Gaura, with his brothers **Govinda** and **Mādhava Ghoṣa**: "Govinda, Mādhava and Vāsudeva, three brothers; Caitanya and Nitāi danced in their kīrtana" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.115). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he is **Guṇatuṅgā**, who sang the songs of Viśākhā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 188).

## Family and home

North-Rāḍhī Kāyasthas; their maternal uncle's house is said to have been at Buraṅgā in Śrīhaṭṭa; their father lived at Kumārahaṭṭa, and after his death the brothers came to Navadvīpa. Later Vāsudeva lived at **Tamluk** (Tāmralipta), Govinda at **Agradvīpa**, Mādhava at **Dāiṁhāṭ**. The *Abhidhāna* says the brothers stayed unmarried and were always with Lord Caitanya and Nityānanda.

## With the Lord

Lord Caitanya sent Vāsudeva and Mādhava with Nityānanda to preach in Bengal; Govinda stayed with the Lord (Ādi 10.117–118). Every year they came to Purī for Ratha-yātrā; Govinda Ghoṣa led one of the kīrtana parties, with his two brothers singing the responses and Vakreśvara dancing (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 13.42–43).

## His songs

His Gaura songs are among the finest: *yadi gaura nā hata tabe ki haita* ("if Gaura had not come, how could we have lived?"), *śacīra āṅgināya nāce viśvambhara rāya*, *jaya jaya jagannātha śacīra nandana* and many more on the Lord's childhood, kīrtana and sannyāsa. A history of Medinipur ascribes to him the books *Gaurāṅga-carita* and *Nimāi-sannyāsa* (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). At Tamluk there is a deity of Mahāprabhu with a unique gesture: it is said that, hearing of the Lord's departure, Vāsudeva dug his own grave and sat in it, and the Lord appeared, blessing him with one hand and telling him with the other to come out.

## Appearance and disappearance

**Disappearance:** Kārtika śukla dvitīyā (Gauḍīya calendar).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Guṇatuṅgā** (Govinda was Kalāvatī, Mādhava Rasollāsā) (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 188).
