## In short

Śrī **Vaṁśīvadana Ṭhākura** (Vaṁśīvadanānanda, Vaṁśī dāsa) of **Kuliyā-Pāhāḍapura** in Navadvīpa was in Kṛṣṇa-līlā **Kṛṣṇa's beloved flute** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 179). After the Lord's sannyāsa he cared for **Śacī** and **Viṣṇupriyā** in Navadvīpa, and with Viṣṇupriyā's leave installed the deity of Gaurāṅga she served. His great-grandson wrote his life in the **Vaṁśī-vilāsa**.

## Birth

He was born on **Madhu-pūrṇimā** (Caitra pūrṇimā), **1416 Śaka** (some say 1427 or 1428), son of **Chakaḍi Caṭṭopādhyāya**, a kulīna brāhmaṇa; at his birth Mahāprabhu and Advaita were in the house (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*; *Vaṁśī-śikṣā*). "Kuliyā and Pāhāḍapura, two villages, where Vaṁśīvadana, Kavidatta and Sāraṅga Ṭhākura always lived" (*Prema-vilāsa*).

## Later life

In his last years he lived at **Bilvagrāma**; the Bhaṭṭācāryas of Bilvagrāma are his descendants. The Gaura deity he installed for Viṣṇupriyā is served by the descendants of Yādava Miśra. His grandson **Rāmacandra Gosvāmī** was brought up by Jāhnavā Mātā; his great-grandson **Vallabha dāsa** (Rājavallabha) wrote his life in the *Vaṁśī-vilāsa* (*Abhidhāna*).

## Appearance

**Appearance:** Caitra pūrṇimā (Gauḍīya calendar), 1416 Śaka.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Vaṁśī**, Kṛṣṇa's flute (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 179).
