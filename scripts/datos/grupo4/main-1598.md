## In short

Śrī **Vakreśvara Paṇḍita**, "the Lord's dear servant, who danced in one mood for twenty-four praharas" — seventy-two hours — in Śrīvāsa's house (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.17). He asked the Lord for ten thousand gandharvas to sing while he danced; the Lord said: "I have one wing like you; with another I could fly." He was **Aniruddha**, and the sakhī **Śaśirekhā** entered him (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 71–73); Dhyānacandra calls him **Tuṅgavidyā**. In Purī he was the dancer of the kīrtana parties; after the Lord he kept the **Gambhīrā** in Kāśī Miśra's house, where his disciple **Gopāla Guru** set up **Rādhākānta**.

## Life

He was born at **Guptipāḍā** near Triveṇī (GPC); the *Abhidhāna* gives his śrīpāṭa as "Seṭerī(?)". Sārvabhauma says in Karṇapūra's play that he knew Śrīvāsa, Vakreśvara, Ācāryaratna and Puṇḍarīka as children — so he was older than the Lord. He was with the Lord in Navadvīpa, at His sannyāsa, at Rāmakeli, and in Purī (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.17–18; Madhya 1.238; *Caitanya-bhāgavata* Madhya 1.139).

## Devānanda Paṇḍita

**Devānanda Paṇḍita** of Kuliyā, a famous reader of the *Bhāgavatam*, let his students throw out Śrīvāsa, who was weeping in ecstasy at his class; the Lord said: "one who reads the book Bhāgavata but disrespects the person Bhāgavata will never taste it". One evening at Kuliyā Devānanda watched Vakreśvara dance and, stick in hand, kept the crowd back for him till midnight; Vakreśvara blessed him: "may you have love of Kṛṣṇa". When the Lord came to Kuliyā, He forgave Devānanda: "because you served Vakreśvara you are Mine; Kṛṣṇa lives in his heart; wherever he goes becomes Vaikuṇṭha" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Antya 3; *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*).

## In Orissa

Gopāla Guru's *Vakreśvarāṣṭaka* (manuscripts at the Pāṭbāḍī of Varāhanagara, nos. 140 and 677) says he preached the Lord's message in the south and west. He made many disciples in Orissa; the Oriya poet **Govinda deva**, of his family, wrote the *Gaura-kṛṣṇodaya* (published by Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī). Yadunātha's *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta* (36) counts him in Gadādhara's branch: "glorious in Utkala and Tailaṅga…". Amṛtalāl Pāl wrote a *Vakreśvara-carita* (1307 BS).

## Appearance and disappearance

**Appearance:** Āṣāḍha kṛṣṇa pañcamī (Gauḍīya calendar; Dhyānacandra).
**Disappearance:** Āṣāḍha śukla saptamī (Gauḍīya calendar); Dhyānacandra's *Paddhati* and the record: Āṣāḍha śukla ṣaṣṭhī.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Aniruddha**, with Śaśirekhā entering him (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 71–73); **Tuṅgavidyā** (Dhyānacandra).
