## In short

Śrī **Rāghava Paṇḍita** of **Pāṇihāṭi** (24 Parganas, on the Gaṅgā): "Rāghava Paṇḍita, the Lord's foremost servant in food" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.24). Every year he and his sister **Damayantī**, the Lord's dear maidservant, filled the famous bags — **Rāghavera jhāli** — with foods for the Lord, which Makaradhvaja Kara carried to Purī, and the Lord ate from them all year (Ādi 10.25–27; Antya 10.13–). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he is **Dhaniṣṭhā**, who brought Kṛṣṇa food (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 166).

## With the Lord and Nityānanda

By the Lord's order he cooked with his own hands for Nityānanda, heard from the Lord about Nityānanda's glory, performed Nityānanda's abhiṣeka at Pāṇihāṭi and made Him a garland of kadamba flowers that bloomed on a jambīra tree (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Antya 5.83–108, 266–284; *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). The Lord visited his house on His way to Vṛndāvana (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 16). At Pāṇihāṭi **Raghunātha dāsa** held the festival of curd and flat rice (Antya 6). His jhāli held pickles, sweets, kāsandi, dried śāk and a hundred other things, made by Damayantī through the year.
