## In short

**Viśvāsa** was the secretary of the Muslim governor who ruled beyond the Orissa border when **Lord Caitanya**, on His way to Vṛndāvana, was waiting there. Sent by the governor to the Orissa officer, he saw the Lord and at once began to chant «Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa», overwhelmed with ecstatic love.

## At the Orissa border

When the Lord reached the border, an officer of the Orissa government came to meet Him and told Him that the land ahead was ruled by a Muslim governor, a drunkard, out of fear of whom no one could use the road freely; he advised the Lord to stay at the border some days until a peaceful agreement could be made, so that He could cross the river by boat in peace. Meanwhile a follower of the governor came in disguise to the Orissa camp, watched Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and went back to tell the governor of His wonderful qualities. The governor's mind changed, and he wished to meet the Lord; he sent his own secretary, Viśvāsa, to the Orissa officer. Viśvāsa came to see Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, and on seeing Him began to chant the holy name, «Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa», overwhelmed with ecstatic love.
