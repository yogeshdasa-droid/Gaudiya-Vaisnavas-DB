## In short

Sūryadāsa Paṇḍita, with the title **Sarakhela**, was the father of **Vasudhā** and **Jāhnavā**, the wives of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu, and a branch of Nityānanda: "Sūryadāsa Sarakhela and his younger brother Kṛṣṇadāsa Sarakhela had firm faith in Nityānanda; they were reservoirs of prema" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.25). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **King Kakudmī**, the father of Revatī, "effulgent as the sun" (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 65).

## Family

He was one of the six sons of **Kaṁsāri Miśra** and **Kamalā Devī** of **Śāligrāma** (Nadia): Dāmodara, Jagannātha, Sūryadāsa, **Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita**, Kṛṣṇadāsa and Nṛsiṁha-Caitanya. His wife was **Bhadrāvatī**. He got the title Sarakhela as the accountant (treasurer) of the king of Gauḍa. He lived at Śāligrāma, and later at Ambikā-Kālnā (*Abhidhāna*); his younger brother Gaurīdāsa moved to Ambikā with his permission.

## His daughters' marriage

He dreamt that he gave both his daughters to Nityānanda Prabhu, whom he saw as Balarāma with the girls at His sides as Revatī and Vāruṇī, and said to a brāhmaṇa friend: "Until He accepts them I shall never have peace." The friend went to Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita in Navadvīpa; Nityānanda agreed. When Nityānanda came to Śāligrāma with the devotees, Sūryadāsa ran out to meet Him, fell at His feet weeping and could not speak; the Lord embraced him, and he gave Him his two daughters (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 12; see the record of Jāhnavā Mātā).

## Work

He wrote the **Bhoga-nirṇaya-paddhati**, which sets the order in which the devotees sit at the bhoga offering of Śrī Gaura-Govinda; since it also mentions offerings for Vasudhā, Jāhnavā and Vīrabhadra, he seems to have lived long, into Vīrabhadra's time (*Abhidhāna*).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Kakudmī**, king of Raivata (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 65).
