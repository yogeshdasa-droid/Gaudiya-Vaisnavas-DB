## In short

Padmāvatī Devī was the mother of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu and the wife of **Hāḍāi Paṇḍita** of Ekacakrā. She was the daughter of **King Mukuṭa Rāya of Mauḍeśvara**. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā she was **Rohiṇī**, Balarāma's mother, and in Rāma-līlā **Sumitrā** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 40).

## Life

She had seven sons; Nityānanda was the eldest (*Prema-vilāsa* 24). When a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī begged for her twelve-year-old son, her treasure and life, she gave Him without a word at a single word from her husband: "Whatever is your wish, my lord, is my word" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 3.93). The *Abhidhāna* compares her with the Rajput stories of mothers who sacrificed a son to save their master's: those show only courage, while Padmāvatī's gift was for the service of a Vaiṣṇava, an ideal of motherhood and of devotion to her husband.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Rohiṇī** and **Sumitrā** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 40).

## Other persons of the same name

Padmāvatī, the second wife of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya, daughter of Raghu Cakravartī of Gopālapura, later called **Gaurāṅgapriyā** (her own record, «Gaurāṅga Priyā»).
