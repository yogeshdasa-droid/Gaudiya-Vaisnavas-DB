## In short

Śrī Vasudhā Devī was the elder wife of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu and the mother of **Vīracandra (Vīrabhadra Gosvāmī)** and **Gaṅgā Devī**. She was the elder daughter of **Sūryadāsa Sarakhela** of Śāligrāma; her younger sister **Jāhnavā** was married to Nityānanda at the same time. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā she was **Vāruṇī**, Balarāma's consort; some say she is **Anaṅga-mañjarī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 65–66).

## Life

Her marriage is told in the record of Jāhnavā Mātā: their father dreamt of giving both daughters to Nityānanda, the marriage was held at Śāligrāma, and Nityānanda brought His wives to Śacī Mātā, to Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī at Śāntipura, to Uddhāraṇa Datta at Saptagrāma and to Khaḍadaha (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 12).

Her first children, it is said, died one after another when Abhirāma Ṭhākura bowed to them; Gaṅgā and Vīrabhadra survived his obeisances. When Jāhnavā came back to Khaḍadaha from her pilgrimage, Vasudhā and she greeted each other with respect.

She is named in the *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā*s of Jīva (41–42), Devakīnandana (12) and Vṛndāvana dāsa (15).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Vāruṇī**; or **Anaṅga-mañjarī** — "both opinions are right" (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 65–66).
