## In short

Mādhava Ācārya (Mādhava Caṭṭopādhyāya) of **Jirāṭ-Balāgaḍ** was the husband of **Gaṅgā Devī**, the daughter of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu, and a branch of Nityānanda: "Pītāmbara, Mādhavācārya, Dāsa Manohara" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.52). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **King Śantanu** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 69).

## Life

His father was **Viśveśvara Caṭṭopādhyāya** (Viśveśvara Ācārya), a vārendra brāhmaṇa of the Kāśyapa gotra, and his mother **Mahālakṣmī Devī**, who died just after his birth. Viśveśvara gave the child to his close friend of the same village, **Bhagīratha Ācārya**, and his wife **Jayadurgā Devī** (Mahālakṣmī's friend), and took sannyāsa and went to Kāśī. Bhagīratha, a rāḍhī brāhmaṇa of the Caṭṭa line, lived at Nanda-grāma near Katwa; brought up as his son, Mādhava took his gotra, studied many śāstras, became a great scholar and was called "Ācārya" (*Prema-vilāsa* 21). "Nityānanda Prabhu's daughter was named Gaṅgā; the Lord gave her in marriage to Mādhava Ācārya" (*Prema-vilāsa* 19). Their son was Gopīvallabha, from whom the Gosvāmīs of the Gaṅgā line at Jirāṭ descend.

He is also called Nityānanda's disciple. Devakīnandana's *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā* names him at its end.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**King Śantanu**, husband of Gaṅgā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 69).

## Other persons of the same name

The *Abhidhāna* lists several Mādhava Ācāryas:
- Mādhava Ācārya of Śrī Caitanya's branch (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.119) — his own record.
- Mādhava Ācārya, a contemporary of Lord Gaurāṅga in Gadādhara Paṇḍita's branch — his own record; the *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā* praises a Mādhava Ācārya "cool in his poetry", author of the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-maṅgala*.
- An Oriya Mādhava, perhaps a disciple of Gadādhara, who wrote a *Caitanya-vilāsa* in Oriya.
