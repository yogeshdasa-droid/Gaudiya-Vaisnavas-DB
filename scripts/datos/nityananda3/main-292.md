## In short

Gaṅgā Devī was the daughter of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu and Vasudhā Devī, and the sister of Vīrabhadra Gosvāmī. She is known as the river **Gaṅgā** herself (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 69). She married **Mādhava Ācārya** (Mādhava Caṭṭopādhyāya) of **Jirāṭ**, who in Kṛṣṇa-līlā was King Śantanu: "Nityānanda Prabhu's daughter was named Gaṅgā; the Lord gave her in marriage to Mādhava Ācārya" (*Prema-vilāsa* 19).

## Life

Abhirāma Ṭhākura bowed to her twelve times and she remained unharmed and smiling; seeing her power he composed the **Gaṅgā-stotra**, of twenty verses (*Abhidhāna*). Her son was **Gopīvallabha**; his line is known at Jirāṭ as the Gosvāmīs of the **Gaṅgā-vaṁśa**. Her descendants still serve the Deity of **Śrī Gopīnātha** that Jāhnavā Mātā found in the Ganges, at Gopīnātha-talā, Jirāṭ (Hooghly).

Jīva's *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā* names her; Devakīnandana names only her husband, and Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja names Vīrabhadra but not Gaṅgā. B. B. Majumdar wondered whether the long dispute between the line of Gaṅgā and that of Nityānanda (through Vīrabhadra) had already begun when the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* was written.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

The river **Gaṅgā**; her husband was King Śantanu (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 69).

## Other persons of the same name

Gaṅgā Devī, mother of Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi and wife of Bāṇeśvara Brahmacārī, is another person (her own record).
