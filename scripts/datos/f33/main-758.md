## In short

**Kṛṣṇadāsa Guñjamālī**, born at **Lahore**, came at seventeen, drawn by Lord Gaurāṅga, to the Gopāla Deity of Govardhana, and when **Lord Caitanya** came to Vṛndāvana surrendered at His feet. The Lord gave him the **guñjā-mālā** from His own neck — hence his name — and at His command he installed Deities at **Multān**, in Gujarat and at **Olambā** (Punjab), founding the **Baḍa Gauḍīya Gaddī**. He preached the holy name widely, even in Sindh, and ended his life in Vṛndāvana.

## Life

Leaving his homeland, he went for darśana of Gopāla at Govardhana and stayed with the sevaka of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī. When Lord Caitanya visited Vṛndāvana, Kṛṣṇadāsa surrendered to Him. At the Lord's command he installed a Deity at Multān and made his nephew and disciple, **Banwāricandra**, its mahānta; he installed another Deity in Gujarat. Later he installed a Deity at the village of Olambā in the Punjab and made **Janārdana Vipra** (#591) mahānta of that gaddī. Through his company many took to the Vaiṣṇava path; in Sindh he brought many Muslims to Vaiṣṇavism. At last he went to Vṛndāvana and lived there to the end.
