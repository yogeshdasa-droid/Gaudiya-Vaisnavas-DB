## In short

**Rāmagopāla dāsa** (Rāmagopāla Rāyacaudhurī), disciple of **Ratikānta Ṭhākura** of Raghunandana's family of Śrīkhaṇḍa, a renowned Vaiṣṇava poet, completed in **1595 Śaka (1673)** the **Rasa-kalpavallī**, in twelve sections, and wrote the **Śrī Narahari-śākhā-nirṇaya** and **Śrī Raghunandana-śākhā-nirṇaya**. He was of the family of **Cakrapāṇi Caudhurī** of Śrīkhaṇḍa.

## His family

While Lord Caitanya was at Nīlācala, two brothers of Śrīkhaṇḍa, **Cakrapāṇi** and **Mahānanda**, surrendered at His feet, introducing themselves as attendants of Raghunandana; the Lord gave them advice and sent them to **Narahari Sarakāra**, inspired by whom they began the service of the Deity **Vṛndāvanacandra**. Cakrapāṇi Caudhurī's son was Nityānanda; Nityānanda's son was Gaṅgārāma, and Gaṅgārāma's son Śyāmarāya. Śyāmarāya's elder son **Madana** put the *Govinda-līlāmṛta* into Bengali verse, and his younger son, Rāmagopāla, compiled the *Rasa-kalpavallī*.
