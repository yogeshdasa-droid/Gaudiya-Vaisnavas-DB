## In short

**Rāmacandra Khān of Benāpola** — born **Śāntidhara**, of a Śrotriya Rāḍhīya brāhmaṇa family; «Khān» was his title — was a zamindār of **Kāgaja-pukhuriyā** near Benāpola (Jessore) and a childhood friend of **Hussain Shah**. He tried to degrade **Haridāsa Ṭhākura** by sending him a prostitute, and treated **Nityānanda Prabhu** with contempt; soon after, he was ruined (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 3). He is not Rāmacandra Khān of Chatrabhoga (#1246).

## The offences

He sent a prostitute to Śrīla Haridāsa Ṭhākura to bring him down. Once Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu came to his house to preach and sat in the pavilion of the goddess Caṇḍī; Rāmacandra would not meet or speak with Him, and after He left had his servants scrape off the outer layer of clay and wash the place with water and cow dung, thinking it polluted by a Vaiṣṇava's touch (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 3.101-102).

## His ruin

After this, great misfortune came on him. When he failed to pay the king's dues, the king's wazir attacked his house, looted his property, imprisoned him and even defiled Caṇḍī's pavilion (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Antya 3.156-160).
