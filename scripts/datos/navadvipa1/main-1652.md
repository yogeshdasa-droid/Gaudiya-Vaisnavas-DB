## In short

Śrī Vāsudeva Datta Ṭhākura was the **elder brother of Mukunda Datta** and, like him, one of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu's singers. Born in an Ambaṣṭha (vaidya) family of Cakraśālā in Chittagong, he came with his brother to Navadvīpa and later set up his śrīpāṭa at **Kāñcanapāḍā** in the 24 Parganas. The Lord asked **Śivānanda Sena** to look after his household affairs. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Madhuvrata**, a singer of Vraja (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 140).

## Family

"In the land of Chittagong is the village of Cakraśālā, where the respected Datta family of Ambaṣṭhas lives. In that family two devotees were born, Śrī Mukunda Datta and Vāsudeva Datta; Vāsudeva was the elder and Mukunda the younger. Both came to live in Navadvīpa" (*Prema-vilāsa* 22, quoted in the *Abhidhāna*, entry Mukunda Datta). One text of the record says his śrīpāṭa was at Chānhārā in Chittagong. In the book *Suvarṇavaṇik* by Dharmānanda Bhāratī he is said to have come from the suvarṇa-vaṇik (jeweller) community instead.

Kavi-karṇapūra writes that Kṛṣṇa's singers Madhukaṇṭha and Madhuvrata were born in the Datta family and became the singers of Śrī Gaurāṅga; Gaura and Nityānanda loved to dance in the kīrtana of Mukunda and Vāsudeva.

## Life

He was a branch of the Caitanya tree (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.41). Later he settled at **Kāñcanapāḍā** (Kāñcrāpāḍā) in the 24 Parganas, where he set up his śrīpāṭa. The Deity of **Madana-gopāla** that he worshipped now stands at **Māmagāchi**, near Pūrvasthalī.

Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja writes that Lord Caitanya told **Śivānanda Sena** to take care of Vāsudeva Datta's family affairs (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*). Every year he came to Purī for the Ratha-yātrā with the devotees of Bengal and sang in the kīrtana groups; among the foods served to the Lord, "this was brought by Vāsudeva Datta" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya-līlā). In his *Mahākāvya*, Kavi-karṇapūra calls him "*bhiṣag-ṛṣabha*", the best of physicians. B. B. Majumdar (*Caitanyacarita-r Upādāna*, p. 686) finds it hard to believe, from Kavi-karṇapūra's *Mahākāvya* and the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, that Vāsudeva lived at Nīlācala in later years.

He is praised in the Vaiṣṇava-vandanās of Jīva (93), Devakīnandana (26) and Vṛndāvana dāsa (30), and mentioned by Murāri Gupta (4.17.5), in the *Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* (8.33), the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Ādi 2.16) and by Jayānanda.

## Guru and disciples

- A branch of the Caitanya tree (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.41).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Madhuvrata**, a singer of Vraja (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 140).
