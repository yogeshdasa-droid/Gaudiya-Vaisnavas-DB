## In short

Śrī Śrīnidhi Paṇḍita — also called **Śrīkānta** — was the youngest brother of **Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita** and a branch of the Caitanya tree. With his brothers Śrīvāsa, Śrīrāma and Śrīpati he lived in Navadvīpa and served Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu with his whole family. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Padmanidhi** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 102–103).

## Family

He was the youngest of the five sons of **Jaladhara Paṇḍita**, a Vaidika brāhmaṇa of Śrīhaṭṭa who settled in Navadvīpa: Nalina, Śrīvāsa, Śrīrāma, Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi (*Prema-vilāsa* 23; *Abhidhāna*). The family had houses in Navadvīpa and at Kumārahaṭṭa.

## Life

"Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and Śrīrāma Paṇḍita, two brothers, two branches known to the world; **Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi their two brothers**; the four brothers with their servants and maidservants, in whose house the Lord always performed saṅkīrtana. The four brothers with their whole family serve Caitanya and know no other god" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.8–11). Śrīnidhi is named again among the Lord's branches: "Śrīnidhi, Śrī Gopīkānta, Miśra Bhagavān" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.110).

Every day the four brothers sang the names of Kṛṣṇa, worshipped Him and bathed in the Gaṅgā three times; they took part in the kīrtanas in Śrīvāsa's courtyard — to the annoyance of the skeptics of Navadvīpa (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 11) — and after the Lord's sannyāsa they went every year with Śrīvāsa to see Him in Purī. For the life of the family see the record of Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Padmanidhi** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 102–103; the record had verse 171).
