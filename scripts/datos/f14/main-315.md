## In short

**Gaurāṅga dāsa Bābājī** was a very old Vaiṣṇava of **Vṛndāvana** who lived near the samādhi of **Sanātana Gosvāmī** and, from his mādhukarī, offered bhoga every day to **Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī**. Indifferent to wealth from his very birth, he saw Lord Caitanya come for him at the moment of his death.

## The «bugs»

He was so old that it took him nearly half an hour to climb the steps by the samādhi. Once a rājā came for his darśana while he was out and, telling no one, left some money under his bed. That night the Bābājī could not sleep a wink, rolling from side to side. In the morning he called some Vaiṣṇavas: "I think there are bugs in my bed — I couldn't sleep at all." Searching for the bugs they found the money, and the Bābājī had it used for bhoga at the samādhi for the Vaiṣṇavas.

## His passing

Shortly before he died he called the caretaker of **Madana-mohana** and asked him to take everything from his room. "Don't worry, Bābājī," the man said, "there is nothing in this room to take to Madana-mohana's store." Then he called one **Sanātana dāsa Bābājī**: "Sanātana, are you blind? There! Lord Caitanya has come Himself — offer Him a seat." Saying this, he breathed his last.
