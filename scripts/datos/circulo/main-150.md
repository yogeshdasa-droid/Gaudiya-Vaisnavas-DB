## In short

Śrīla Bhūgarbha Gosvāmī, a brāhmaṇa and a disciple of **Gadādhara Paṇḍita**, was — with his nephew **Lokanātha Gosvāmī** — one of the first of Gaura's devotees to settle in Vṛndāvana. At Mahāprabhu's command the two of them brought to light the lost places of Kṛṣṇa's pastimes. He and Lokanātha were so close that they were "one soul in two bodies". He knew nothing but talk of Lord Caitanya. He passed away in Vṛndāvana on Kārtika Śukla Caturdaśī, and his samādhi is beside Rūpa Gosvāmī's at the Rādhā-Dāmodara temple. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he is **Prema Mañjarī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 187).

## Dates

- **Appearance:** not known.
- **Disappearance:** the fourteenth day of the bright fortnight of Kārtika (**Kārtika Śukla Caturdaśī**, October–November), in Vṛndāvana.

## Family

According to the *Abhidhāna* (quoting the *Sādhana-dīpikā* 8), he was the **paternal uncle of Śrī Lokanātha Gosvāmī**; one text of the record also says that Lokanātha was his brother's son. The other texts call them dearest friends.

## Life

He was a disciple of **Gadādhara Paṇḍita**: "The eleventh branch of Gadādhara Gosvāmī was Bhūgarbha Gosvāmī and the twelfth was Bhāgavata dāsa. Both of them went to Vṛndāvana and resided there for life" (*bhūgarbha gosāñi āra bhāgavata dāsa*, *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.82). And: "Bhūgarbha Gosāñi, a disciple of Paṇḍita Gosāñi, was always engaged in topics of Lord Caitanya and knew nothing else" (*paṇḍita-gosāñira śiṣya — bhūgarbha gosāñi / gaura-kathā vinā āra mukhe anya nāi*, *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 8.68). (Paṇḍita Gosāñi here is Gadādhara Paṇḍita; one text of the record wrongly says Paṇḍita Haridāsa.)

On the order of Mahāprabhu, he and **Lokanātha** went to live in Vraja — on foot from Navadvīpa, by way of Rājmahal, Tājpur, Pūrṇiyā, Ayodhyā and Lucknow; only Subuddhi Miśra had gone there before them (*Abhidhāna*, entry Lokanātha). At the Lord's command Bhūgarbha **unearthed the buried places of Kṛṣṇa's pastimes** in Vṛndāvana (*Prema-vilāsa* 7). The *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* tells that Rūpa Gosvāmī, with Bhūgarbha and other followers, went to see Śrī Kṛṣṇa (Gopāla) in the house of Viṭṭhaleśvara at Mathurā.

Narahari Cakravartī writes in the *Bhakti-ratnākara*: "Bhūgarbha Gosvāmī and Lokanātha Gosvāmī were world-preachers; they were so close and affectionate to one another that they were like one body" — "one, only their bodies were different". In Vṛndāvana the two were counted among the principal Gosvāmīs, being senior to many in age and experience.

**Narottama Dāsa Ṭhākura**, who had much loving association with him, names him in one of his songs of lament:

"O Hari! Why am I addicted to this abominable path of karma? I never worshipped Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa in Vraja even a little, nor understood the path of spontaneous devotion. Svarūpa Dāmodara, Sanātana Gosvāmī, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Raghunātha dāsa, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī, Bhūgarbha Gosvāmī, Jīva Gosvāmī and Lokanātha Gosvāmī — I never offered a drop of service to their lotus feet. How then shall I attain the goal of life? … My life and birth are useless. Alas, alas! Thus laments Narottama Dāsa."

The *Śākhā-nirṇaya* (15) praises him: "I always bow to Bhūgarbha Gosvāmī, who arose from the depths of the earth and is famous everywhere, the great-hearted master who gives love for Kṛṣṇa."

He passed away in Vṛndāvana on Kārtika Śukla Caturdaśī. His **samādhi** is near the samādhi of Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī, in the courtyard of the **Śrī Śrī Rādhā-Dāmodara** temple.

## Guru and disciples

- **Guru:** **Gadādhara Paṇḍita Gosvāmī** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 8.68 and 12.82; Jadunāth).
- **Disciples:** **Caitanya dāsa**, the pūjārī of Śrī Govinda-deva; **Kumudānanda Cakravartī** (the record has Mukundānanda; the *Abhidhāna* notes both readings); **Premī Kṛṣṇadāsa**; and others (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 8.69).

## Works

No written works of his are named in the sources.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Prema Mañjarī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 187).
