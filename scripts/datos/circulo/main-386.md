## In short

Śrī Gopīnātha Pūjārī Gosvāmī, a Gauḍīya brāhmaṇa from **Devavana** near Hardwar, was a disciple of **Śrī Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī**, who at the end of his life entrusted to him the service of **Śrī Śrī Rādhā-ramaṇa** in Vṛndāvana. A life-long brahmacārī, he passed the service on to his younger brother **Dāmodara**, whose descendants still serve Rādhā-ramaṇa today.

## Life

- **Meeting his guru:** when Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī went on pilgrimage to Uttarākhaṇḍa, he took the Gauḍīya brāhmaṇa Gopīnātha from **Devavana**, near Hardwar (in the Sahāranpur district), as his disciple and brought him to Vṛndāvana (*Abhidhāna*).
- **The service of Rādhā-ramaṇa:** for many years Gopāla Bhaṭṭa saw his pure devotion and deep love, and at the time of his departure he placed the service of the Deity he had installed, **Śrī Rādhā-ramaṇa**, in Gopīnātha's hands (*Prema-vilāsa* 18).
- **Dāmodara:** Gopīnātha never married. Before his own departure he gave the service to his younger brother **Dāmodara**, who took his place (*Abhidhāna*, entry Dāmodara Pūjārī). Since then Dāmodara's descendants have performed the sevā-pūjā with their own hands.

## The family of the sevāites

Many learned paṇḍitas and devotees of Śrī Gaurāṅga have appeared in this family, among them **Gallujī Mahārāja, Sakhālāla, Gopīlāla, Madhusūdana Sārvabhauma, Dāmodaralāla and Banamālīlāla**. Madhusūdana Sārvabhauma's book *Śrī Rādhā-ramaṇa Prākaṭya* describes many events in the life of Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī.

## Guru and disciples

- **Guru:** **Śrī Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī**.
- **Successor:** his younger brother **Dāmodara**.
