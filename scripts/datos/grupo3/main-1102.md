## In short

**Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī** was the leader of the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs of **Kāśī** (Vārāṇasī), with thousands of disciples (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Madhya 3.37–40). He criticised Lord Caitanya, was won over by Him at a meeting of the sannyāsīs, and later, at the temple of Bindumādhava, fell at His feet and took His shelter (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 7, Madhya 17.104–143, 25.5–160; *Bhaktamāla* 22.7).

## His criticism

He called the Lord "a sannyāsī in name only, a sentimentalist who dances with fanatics". The devotees of Kāśī — Tapana Miśra, Candraśekhara Vaidya, Paramānanda Kīrtanīyā — were deeply hurt. A Mahārāṣṭrian brāhmaṇa who had received love of Kṛṣṇa from the Lord told of Him in Prakāśānanda's assembly, and was told: "Hear Vedānta from me; do not go to Him" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 17).

## The meeting of the sannyāsīs

The Mahārāṣṭrian brāhmaṇa invited all the sannyāsīs of Kāśī, and the Lord too, who accepted to show them mercy. He bowed to them, washed His feet and sat in the unclean place where He washed them, showing an effulgence like millions of suns; they all rose. Prakāśānanda took Him by the hand and seated Him among them: "You are Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, a disciple of Keśava Bhāratī, of our Śaṅkara line: why do You dance and sing instead of studying Vedānta?" — "My guru saw I was a fool, unfit for Vedānta, and told Me always to chant the holy name, the essence of all mantras; the name makes Me dance." Then, asked about Vedānta, the Lord showed the faults of Śaṅkara's commentary and explained the sūtras directly; the sannyāsīs begged forgiveness and began to chant "Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 7).

## At Bindumādhava

On His way back from Vṛndāvana, the Lord danced in kīrtana at **Bindumādhava** with Tapana Miśra, Candraśekhara, Sanātana and others. Prakāśānanda came with his disciples, joined the kīrtana with tears and trembling, and fell at the Lord's feet asking forgiveness. The Lord told him: "Vyāsa, who wrote the *Vedānta-sūtra*, wrote the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* as its commentary: study the *Bhāgavatam*." From then on Prakāśānanda and his disciples took shelter of the Lord, and all Kāśī chanted the holy name (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 25).

## His guru

His own guru is not known. The record had «Keśava Bhāratī» as his guru: that came from his words to the Lord ("You are a disciple of Keśava Bhāratī"). He is **not Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī**, the author of the *Caitanya-candrāmṛta*, who has his own record (Whisper, 06-11-2026); the Gauḍīya Maṭha holds that they were one person, and the *Abhidhāna* refers the reader to the entry «Prabodhānanda».

## Not to be confused

Not **Prakāśānanda**, son of Ratnākara Miśra of Kāṣṭhakāṭā, disciple of Advaita Ācārya, who has his own record.
