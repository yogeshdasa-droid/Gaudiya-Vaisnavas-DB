## In short

Śrī **Keśava Bhāratī** gave **sannyāsa** to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu at **Kāṭoyā** (Katwa) and the name **Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya**. A Vārendra brāhmaṇa of **Kuliyā**, named **Kālīnātha Ācārya** before sannyāsa, he too was a disciple of **Mādhavendra Purī** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 52, 117; *Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Sāndīpani Muni**, who gave Kṛṣṇa the sacred thread; others say **Akrūra**.

## Before sannyāsa

"There was a Vārendra brāhmaṇa, Kālīnātha Ācārya, a vipra of Kuliyā, the best in every virtue; becoming a disciple of Mādhavendra he took sannyāsa, and was known in the world as Keśava Bhāratī" (*Prema-vilāsa* 23). His brother was **Balabhadra**. Some say he was born in the family of Mahāmahopādhyāya Śūlapāṇi, others that he descended from **Umāpati Dhara**.

## The sannyāsa of Śrī Caitanya

In Navadvīpa the Lord told Nityānanda the day of His sannyāsa and the name of the giver, Keśava Bhāratī (*Caitanya-bhāgavata* Madhya 25.10). At Kāṭoyā, seeing Him, Bhāratī took Him as the guru of the world (Madhya 28.105–126); the Lord, as if by chance, spoke a mantra into Bhāratī's ear and then took that same mantra from him; Bhāratī, wondering what name to give Him, gave Him the name **Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya** (Madhya 28.154–174). He then went with the Lord on His way (Antya 1.13–52) and is named again at Antya 4.139–188 and 9.130–150.

## His family

The brahmacārīs of Cũcuḍā (Chinsurah) call themselves of his family; the brahmacārīs of Denuḍa (Mantreśvar police station, Burdwan), where there is a «Bhāratīra puṣkariṇī», say the same; so do families of Kālābāḍī and Gopālpur (Nadia), Bāgpur (Murshidabad), the Bhaṭṭācāryas of Śrībarā (Midnapore) and Goptipāḍā, and the Sarakāras of Mañjayānī and Kṛṣṇanagar.

## Appearance

**Appearance:** Māgha śukla ekādaśī (Gauḍīya calendar).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Sāndīpani Muni**; some say **Akrūra** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 52, 117).
