## In short

**Kāśīśvara Brahmacārī** (Kāśīśvara Gosāñi), of Lord Caitanya's branch (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.138), was a disciple of **Īśvara Purī**. With **Govinda** he served his guru, and by his guru's last order both came to serve Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in Purī. He was very strong, and when the Lord went to see Jagannātha he walked in front and held back the crowds with his hands: "he takes the Lord to see Jagannātha, keeping the crowd away in front" (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Bhṛṅgāra**, Kṛṣṇa's servant who carried water from the Yamunā, and the sakhī **Śaśirekhā** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 137, 166).

## Coming to the Lord

Govinda came first to Nīlācala; the Lord hesitated to accept service from His guru's servant; when Kāśīśvara arrived, Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya settled it: "the order of the guru is not to be weighed" (*ājñā gurūṇāṁ hy avicāraṇīyā*), and the Lord accepted them (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 10.131–148). He also served prasāda to the devotees after kīrtana. See also *Bhaktamāla* 20.12.

## One Kāśīśvara or two?

The *Abhidhāna* and Kavi Karṇapūra keep him apart from **Kāśīśvara Paṇḍita** of Cātarā, who took Śrī Gaura-Govinda to Vṛndāvana (Kelimañjarī), who has his own record; Śrīla Prabhupāda and others take them as one person.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Bhṛṅgāra** (Govinda was Bhaṅgura); also **Śaśirekhā** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 137, 166).
