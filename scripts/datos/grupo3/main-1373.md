## In short

An elderly brāhmaṇa of the **Sanoḍiyā** community of **Mathurā**, a disciple of **Mādhavendra Purī**, who received Lord Caitanya at Mathurā and went with Him round Vraja (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 17.163–188).

## Meeting the Lord

When the Lord came to Mathurā He saw Keśavajī at Kṛṣṇa's birthplace and danced; a brāhmaṇa fell at His feet and danced with Him. Taking him aside, the Lord asked: "Where have you got this treasure of love?" — "Mādhavendra Purī came to Mathurā on his tour, came to my house and made me his disciple; he even ate at my house. After installing Gopāla he served Him; that Gopāla is still worshipped on Govardhana." The Lord at once bowed at his feet: "You are in the place of My guru; I am your disciple." The brāhmaṇa, afraid: "You are a sannyāsī! Seeing Your love I think You must be related to Mādhavendra Purī — without him there is not even a scent of such love." Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya told him of the relationship, and he danced in joy.

## The Lord eats at his house

He took the Lord home and asked Balabhadra to cook; the Lord said: "Mādhavendra Purī ate at your house: you cook and give Me." A sannyāsī does not eat from a Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa, but Mādhavendra had seen him behave as a Vaiṣṇava. The brāhmaṇa: "Foolish people will blame You; I will not bear it." The Lord: "The Vedas, Purāṇas and sages do not agree; a devotee's behaviour shows the real purpose of religion, and the behaviour of Mādhavendra Purī is its essence: one should follow the path of the mahājanas." The people of Mathurā came to see the Lord, and this brāhmaṇa went with Him on His tour of the twelve forests (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 17–18).

## Sources

The record's text, *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 17 and the *Śrī Śrī Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*, entry «Sanoḍiyā brāhmaṇa».
