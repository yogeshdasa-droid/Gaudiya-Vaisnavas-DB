## In short

Śrī **Kāśīśvara Paṇḍita**, a devotee of Lord Caitanya, went to **Vṛndāvana** by the Lord's order and installed there **Śrī Gaura-Govinda**, the deity the Lord gave him as His own self, at the side of Śrī Govindadeva. "Who can tell the glory of Kāśīśvara, who brought Śrī Gaura-Govinda to Vṛndāvana? Kāśīśvara, dear to the Lord, is known in the world; Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana are enchanted by his qualities" (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 6.444, 479). In Vraja he was **Kelimañjarī** (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*; *Sādhana-dīpikā* 2.41).

## Gaura-Govinda

When Rūpa Gosvāmī found Śrī Govinda at Gomā-ṭīlā and sent word to Purī, the Lord asked Kāśīśvara to go to Vṛndāvana to help in His service. "To leave You will split my heart in two." Knowing his heart, the Lord gave him, carefully made, a deity of His own form, and sat to eat with it; seeing the deity eat, Kāśīśvara was in great bliss. The Lord named it **Śrī Gaura-Govinda**. Kāśīśvara took Him to Vṛndāvana, seated Him at the right of Śrī Govinda and served Them with great love (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 2.440–444). Gaura-Govinda is still with Govindadeva at **Jaipur**.

## Cātarā

He belonged to **Cātarā**, near Śrīrāmpur; his nephew (sister's son) **Rudra Paṇḍita** was the first priest of Rādhā-Vallabha of **Vallabhapur**, a mile from Śrīrāmpur station, and his disciple and nephew, the son of Mahādeva Bhaṭṭācārya, received from him the service of Gaura-Nityānanda deities (*Abhidhāna*). An old temple at Cātarā is said to be his. His father was **Vāsudeva Bhaṭṭācārya**, of the Kāñjilāl (Kanu) family; the surname Caudhurī; in Bhaktisiddhānta's time the Vallabhapur temple was in the hands of Śivacandra Caudhurī, a descendant of his brother (Śrīla Prabhupāda, *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.138, purport).

## One Kāśīśvara or two?

Kavi Karṇapūra and the *Abhidhāna* have **two** Kāśīśvaras: this Kāśīśvara Paṇḍita of Cātarā and Vṛndāvana (Kelimañjarī), and **Kāśīśvara Brahmacārī (Gosvāmī)**, the disciple of Īśvara Purī, who with Govinda served the Lord in Purī and cleared the crowds before Him in the temple (Bhṛṅgāra; *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.138, Madhya 10.131–148), who has his own record. Śrīla Prabhupāda's purport and other books take them as **one person**, the disciple of Īśvara Purī who later went to Vṛndāvana; the texts of this record tell it that way. **Govinda Gosāñi**, the pūjārī of Śrī Govinda, was "the disciple of Kāśīśvara Gosāñi" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 8.66).

## Appearance and disappearance

**Disappearance:** Kārtika śukla caturdaśī (Gauḍīya calendar); one text: the full moon of Āśvina, the day of Govinda's mahā-rāsa.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Kelimañjarī** (*Abhidhāna*); the record also gave Bhṛṅgāra, which belongs to Kāśīśvara Brahmacārī.
