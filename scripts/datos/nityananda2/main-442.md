## In short

Halāyudha Ṭhākura was an associate of Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu. According to the *Ananta-saṁhitā* he was one of the **twelve gopālas** (some lists put him in place of Nāgara Puruṣottama); the *Vaiṣṇavācāra-darpaṇa* and other books count him among the twelve **upagopālas**. His Śrīpāṭa was at **Rāmacandrapura** in Navadvīpa-dhāma, on the north-west bank of the Ganges.

## His identity in Kṛṣṇa-līlā

The books differ: some say he was the "second Subala" gopāla (the *Vaiṣṇavācāra-darpaṇa* lists him as Subala among the upagopālas), others **Prabala** gopāla; the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (134) makes him a sakhā of Balarāma (*Abhidhāna*).

## Rāmacandrapura

The old village of Rāmacandrapura was lost when the Ganges changed its course; the present village of that name was built some seventy or seventy-five years before the source was written.

"I worship Halāyudha Ṭhākura with respect" (*Vaiṣṇava-vandanā*): this Halāyudha Ṭhākura, a devotee of Lord Gaurāṅga, may be the same person (the *Abhidhāna* gives the two entries one after the other).

## Other persons of the same name

Halāyudha, the poet of the *Sadukti-karṇāmṛta* collection (*Viśvakoṣa* 22, p. 536), and Halāyudha Bhaṭṭācārya are other persons.
