## In short

Maheśa Paṇḍita was one of the **twelve gopālas**, counted in both Śrī Caitanya's branch (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.11) and Śrī Nityānanda's: "Maheśa Paṇḍita, the seventh of the twelve gopālas, was very liberal; in great love of Kṛṣṇa he danced like a madman to the beat of a kettledrum" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 11.32). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Mahābāhu** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 129). His Śrīpāṭa is now at **Pālapāḍā**, near Cākdaha (Nadia).

## Family

He was the **younger brother of Jagadīśa Paṇḍita** of Yaśaḍā (*Abhidhāna*). Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura mentions this as what some people say, adding that his birthplace is not certain; one text says he was born in what is now Cākdaha.

## Life

He was a companion and very dear friend of Nityānanda Prabhu, and was at His famous festival of chipped rice and yogurt at **Pāṇihāṭi** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 6.62). Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura calls him "a great mahānta, very dear to Nityānanda" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5.744). He used to come to Khaḍadaha; when Narottama dāsa Ṭhākura came there, Maheśa Paṇḍita met him with great affection and could hardly bear to bid him farewell (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 8.220).

## His Śrīpāṭa

His Śrīpāṭa was first at **Saraḍāṅgā**, then at **Maśipura** (Yaśipura), east of Jirāṭ; when the Ganges washed both villages away, the Deities stayed for a time at **Beleḍāṅgā** and are now at **Pālapāḍā**, about a mile south of Cākdaha station, between Simurāli and Cākdaha on the Sealdah–Kṛṣṇanagara line, in the area called Pañcanagara Paragaṇā (with Beleḍāṅgā, Berigrāma, Sukhasāgara, Cāndūḍe, Mānasapoṭā…). His **samādhi** was moved there too. The temple has Śrī Śrī Nitāi-Gaurāṅga, Śrī Gopīnātha, Śrī Madana-mohana, Śrī Rādhā-Govinda and a śālagrāma (Bhaktivedānta Svāmī). The *Caitanya-saṁhitā* places a Maheśa Paṇḍita's Śrīpāṭa at Barāhanagara; whether he is the same person is not known (*Abhidhāna*).

## Disappearance

On the **kṛṣṇa trayodaśī of Pauṣa** (the Gauḍīya calendar, and Bhaktivedānta Svāmī; another text says the śukla trayodaśī).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Mahābāhu** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 129).
