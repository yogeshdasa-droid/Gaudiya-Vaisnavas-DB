## In short

Nāgara Puruṣottama is counted by the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* among the **twelve gopālas** of Śrī Nityānanda; in Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Dāma** gopāla: "Nāgara Puruṣottama, who was Dāma in Vraja before" (*Bhaktamāla* 3). Some say "Nāgara" was a title, others that his family came from the Nāgara country. In prema he once drank snake venom and was not harmed (*Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna*).

## Who he was

The sources do not agree:
- The *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* counts Nāgara Puruṣottama (Dāma) and Puruṣottama dāsa (Stoka-kṛṣṇa), son of Sadāśiva Kavirāja, as two different gopālas.
- Bhaktivedānta Svāmī calls Sadāśiva's son Nāgara Puruṣottama, making them one person.
- Some lists put Halāyudha Ṭhākura in his place among the twelve.

He is not Puruṣottama Nāgara of Teotha-Bāṅkpāl (Dhaka), the eldest son of Īśāna Nāgara (his own record).

## Sources

The record's text (*Bhaktamāla* 3) and the *Śrī Śrī Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna* (Haridāsa Dāsa), entry «Puruṣottama Nāgara» 1.
