## In short

The **Mahārāṣṭrian brāhmaṇa** of **Vārāṇasī**, whose name is not given, was the devotee who, pained to hear the Māyāvādī sannyāsīs blaspheme Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu everywhere in the city, invited all of them — and the Lord — to his house. At that meeting **Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī** and his followers were won over to the holy name (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Ādi 7).

## The invitation

Hearing the blasphemy, he thought unhappily: "Whoever closely sees the characteristics of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu at once realizes who He is and accepts Him as the Supreme Lord. If somehow I can bring all the sannyāsīs together, they will surely become His devotees when they see Him. I shall have to live in Vārāṇasī the rest of my life; if I do not try this, I shall go on suffering." So he invited all the sannyāsīs of Vārāṇasī, and then went to Mahāprabhu, fell at His lotus feet and joyfully begged Him to accept. The Lord smiled and accepted, to show His mercy to the Māyāvādīs.

## The meeting

When He saw the sannyāsīs, Mahāprabhu offered them obeisances, washed His feet and sat down on the ground where He had washed them, manifesting an effulgence as brilliant as millions of suns. Attracted, the sannyāsīs all rose. Their leader, **Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī**, took Him by the hand, seated Him with great respect in the midst of the assembly and asked why He — Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, disciple of Keśava Bhāratī, of their own Śaṅkara-sampradāya — avoided them and gave up meditation and Vedānta to chant and dance with "fanatics".

Mahāprabhu answered: "My spiritual master considered Me a fool. 'You are not qualified to study Vedānta,' he said, 'so You must always chant the holy name of Kṛṣṇa. This is the essence of all mantras; in this age of Kali there is no other religious principle.' Since I received this order I always chant the holy name, and that name sometimes makes Me chant and dance. I do not do it intentionally." Then, with their leave, He explained the purport of the Vedānta-sūtra and the real mission of Śrī Śaṅkarācārya. The sannyāsīs humbly said: "You are Vedic knowledge personified, directly Nārāyaṇa. Kindly excuse the offences we committed by criticizing You." From then on they too always chanted "Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!", and the Lord blessed them with Kṛṣṇa-nāma (Caitanya-caritāmṛta Ādi 7).

## Afterwards

The brāhmaṇa's happiness knew no bounds. With tears in his eyes he fell at Mahāprabhu's feet: "You are the fulfiller of all desires! By Your mercy all my desires have been fulfilled." He took Mahāprabhu, Prakāśānanda and the other devotees into the dining hall; while taking prasāda the Lord called out "Hari! Hari!", and that day Kāśī was overwhelmed with love for Kṛṣṇa. After a few more days in Kāśī, Mahāprabhu bade farewell to His devotees and left for Purī.
