## In short

Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya was a branch of Śrī Advaita Ācārya: "Bhāgavatācārya and Viṣṇudāsācārya" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.58). He was at the great festival of **Kheturī**.

## Two Viṣṇudāsa Ācāryas

The *Abhidhāna* (vol. 1, p. 135) says there were two Viṣṇudāsa Ācāryas. From one descend the Gosvāmīs of **Māṇikyaḍihi**, vārendra brāhmaṇas; this Viṣṇudāsa is connected with Mādhavendra Purī's line and wrote the **Sītā-guṇa-kadamba**, a life of Sītā Devī. From the other descend the Gosvāmīs of **Kāndikhāli**, rāḍhī brāhmaṇas. Both villages are still on the bank of the Bhāgīrathī.

## Other persons of the same name

- Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya of Nityānanda's branch, a brāhmaṇa of Navadvīpa, brother of Nandana Ācārya — his own record.
- Viṣṇudāsa Kavīndra, a kāyastha who was put out of the Gauḍīya community.
