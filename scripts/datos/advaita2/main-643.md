## In short

Kāmadeva Paṇḍita was a disciple of Śrī Advaita Ācārya, a rāḍhī brāhmaṇa of strict habits and a leading kulīna of the **Khaḍadaha** mela.

## Life

He married **Rādhārāṇī**, the daughter of **Kamalākara Pippalāi** of Māheśa (one of the twelve gopālas). It was through Kāmadeva's own efforts that **Nityānanda Prabhu came to live at Khaḍadaha**. A Kāmadeva went with Acyutānanda, Advaita's son, from Śāntipura to Katwa for the disappearance festival of Gadādhara dāsa (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 10.403). His great-grandson **Cāṅdaśarmā** installed Śrī Śrī **Rādhākānta** at Khaḍadaha.

B. B. Majumdar mentions a Kāmadeva Caitanya dāsa, disciple of Advaita (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 12), a brāhmaṇa of Khaḍadaha — probably the same. The *Padakalpataru* has one song by a poet named Kāmadeva.
