## In short

Kamalākānta Viśvāsa was a branch of Śrī Advaita Ācārya and his close servant: "Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, the servant of Advaita" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.28). He kept the accounts of Advaita's household.

## The letter to King Pratāparudra

While in Purī he wrote a letter to **Mahārāja Pratāparudra**, proving with many arguments that Advaita Prabhu was the Supreme Lord Himself, and at the end asking for money because Advaita had a debt of **three hundred rupees**. By chance the letter came into Lord Caitanya's hands. The Lord was pained that His devotee should show Advaita as a beggar before a king, and forbade Kamalākānta to come near Him. When Advaita heard of it he pleaded at the Lord's feet; the Lord then forgave Kamalākānta and told him: "Never accept a king's money; eating the food of worldly men spoils the mind" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.29–52; *Abhidhāna*).
