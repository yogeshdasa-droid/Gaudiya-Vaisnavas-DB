## In short

Kāmadeva Nāgara was once a disciple of Śrī Advaita Ācārya, but was rejected by him for holding to the path of jñāna.

## The disciples who would not give up jñāna

At one time, for a special reason, Advaita Prabhu taught jñāna-yoga, and some of his disciples took those words as final. When he later preached that bhakti-yoga is the highest, they would not accept it and held to the path of knowledge; though he forbade them again and again, they would not give up their former view, and so they were put out of the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava community. Among them are named **Kāmadeva Nāgara**, **Āgala Pāgala** and **Śaṅkara**:

"Advaita preached the doctrine of bhakti to all his disciples; all gave up jñāna and practised bhakti — but Kāmadeva Nāgara, Āgala Pāgala and Śaṅkara would not give up jñāna. Śaṅkara said: 'We are jñānavādīs; without jñāna no one attains perfection.' Advaita said: 'Give up jñāna.' Śaṅkara said: 'Defeat me in argument'…" and in the end Advaita gave them up (*Prema-vilāsa* 24; *Advaita-prakāśa* 20, p. 93; *Abhidhāna*).
