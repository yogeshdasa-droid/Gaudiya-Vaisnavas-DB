## In short

Yadunandana Ācārya was a branch of Śrī Advaita Ācārya: "Śrī Yadunandana Ācārya, of Advaita's branch, whose own branches and sub-branches cannot be written" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 12.56). He was the family priest and guru of **Hiraṇya** and **Govardhana Majumdār** of Saptagrāma, and so the **dīkṣā guru of Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 6.161–162).

## Life

When Raghunātha dāsa was still at home and longing to escape to the Lord, Yadunandana Ācārya came to his house one night and asked him to persuade a brāhmaṇa who had left his Deity's service to come back to it; going with his guru, Raghunātha took the chance and fled to Purī (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 6.161–180).

## Other persons of the same name

- Yadunandana Ācārya of Jhāmaṭapura, of the Pippalāi family, disciple of Vīrabhadra Gosvāmī, whose daughters Śrīmatī and Nārāyaṇī married Vīrabhadra — his own record.
- A Yadunandana of Śrī Caitanya's branch (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.119); the *Abhidhāna* says it is not clear which Yadunandana he is.
- The son-in-law of Kamalākara Pippalāi according to the *Vaiṣṇavācāra-darpaṇa*.
