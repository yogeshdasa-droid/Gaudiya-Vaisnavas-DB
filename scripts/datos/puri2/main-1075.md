## In short

Śrī Paramānanda Purī was a dear disciple of **Mādhavendra Purī** and one of the nine roots of the tree of devotion, the middle root (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 9.13; *Abhidhāna*). Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu respected him as His guru, and in Purī he lived near the Lord to the end. "The heads of all, the two who knew the Lord's heart: Paramānanda Purī and Svarūpa Dāmodara" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.125). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **Uddhava** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 118).

## Birth

He was born in a brāhmaṇa family in **Tirhut** (Trihuta: today the districts of Muzaffarpur, Darbhanga and Chapra), and was a dear disciple of Mādhavendra Purī (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 3.178).

## Meeting the Lord at Ṛṣabha-parvata

On His journey to the South the Lord saw Śrī Nārāyaṇa at Ṛṣabha-parvata and heard that Paramānanda Purī was staying nearby for the four months of the rains. "Paramānanda was there for the cāturmāsya; hearing this, the Lord went to Purī Gosāñi; the Lord bowed at his feet, and in love Purī Gosāñi embraced Him" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 9.167–169). For three days they stayed together, mad in talks of Kṛṣṇa. Purī said he would go to Puruṣottama and then to Bengal to bathe in the Gaṅgā; the Lord asked him to come back soon to Nīlācala, "and we shall spend our days together in talks of Kṛṣṇa" (Madhya 9.170–175).

## In Purī with the Lord

He went to Navadvīpa, to the house of the Lord, where Śacī Mātā received and fed him, and when he heard the Lord was back in Nīlācala he came at once with **Dvija Kamalākānta**. The Lord worshipped his feet and Purī embraced Him; he told the Lord that the devotees of Bengal were coming to see Him (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 10.92–98). The Lord gave him a quiet room and a servant in the house of **Kāśī Miśra**. Purī loved the Lord as a father loves his son, and the Lord honoured him as His guru: wherever He was invited, He took Purī Gosvāmī with Him. Later Purī moved to a maṭha on the western side of the temple.

**The well.** One day the Lord came with Gadādhara Paṇḍita to Purī's maṭha. Purī had dug a well, but the water was bad — "more mud than water". The Lord raised His arms and prayed: "Lord Jagannātha, give Me this boon: let the Gaṅgā enter this well." The next morning the well was full of clear water; the devotees walked round it, and the Lord said: "Whoever bathes in this water or drinks it gets the fruit of bathing in the Gaṅgā, and pure love for Kṛṣṇa." And: "I am in this world only by the love of Purī Gosvāmī; I belong to him, he may sell Me wherever he likes; whoever sees him becomes dear to Kṛṣṇa" (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 3.233–257). Purī Gosvāmī's well can still be seen in Purī, on the way to the Lokanātha temple.

He was with the Lord in His last pastimes and in the water sports in the Narendra-sarovara (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 3.167–181, 10.42–46). When the Lord gave up **Choṭa Haridāsa**, Paramānanda Purī went to plead for him; the Lord stood up — "I will go to Ālālanātha with Govinda; you stay here" — and Purī with great difficulty brought Him back (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 2; *Abhidhāna*).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Uddhava** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 118).

## Another Paramānanda Purī

The *Abhidhāna* names a second Paramānanda Purī, the author of the *Govinda-vijaya* (*Baṅga-sāhitya-sevaka*).
