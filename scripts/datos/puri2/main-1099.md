## In short

Pradyumna Miśra was a brāhmaṇa devotee of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in Purī and a branch of the Caitanya tree: "Kāśī Miśra, Pradyumna Miśra, Rāya Bhavānanda" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.131). The Lord sent him to hear Kṛṣṇa-kathā from **Rāmānanda Rāya** (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 5.4–67). In Kṛṣṇa-līlā, Lord Gaura Himself entered him (āveśa) (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 74).

## Life

He came from Śrīhaṭṭa (Sylhet) and later settled in Orissa. According to the *Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* (Nirṇaya-sāgara edition) it was Sārvabhauma who introduced him to the Lord.

One day he asked the Lord to tell him about Kṛṣṇa. The Lord answered that He Himself did not know Kṛṣṇa-kathā, and sent him to Rāmānanda Rāya. Pradyumna found Rāmānanda in a garden, training two young devadāsīs for his drama — bathing and dressing them with his own hands — and waited a long time, then went back. The Lord explained that Rāmānanda's mind was not touched at all, and sent him again; this time Rāmānanda spoke to him about Kṛṣṇa until late, and Pradyumna came back fully satisfied (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 5.4–67; *Abhidhāna*, entry «Rāmānanda Rāya»). See also *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Madhya 1.120, 10.43.

He is named in the Vaiṣṇava-vandanā of Devakīnandana (66), and in the *Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* of Kavi Karṇapūra (act 8). He is considered the author of the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-caitanyodayāvalī*; a manuscript of a *Śikṣāṣṭaka* in his name is in the Utkal University library (cy 336).

## Not the Lord's cousin

Another Pradyumna Miśra, of the family of Kīrti Miśra of Buruṅgā in Śrīhaṭṭa, was a cousin (or, some say, a nephew) of the Lord; according to Bimanbihari Majumdar he cannot be this Pradyumna of Purī. He has his own record.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

Lord Gaura entered him (āveśa) (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 74).
