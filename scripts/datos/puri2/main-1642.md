## In short

Vāṇīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka (Nāyaka Vāṇīnātha) was the youngest son of **Bhavānanda Rāya** and a brother of **Rāmānanda Rāya**, a branch of the Caitanya tree (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.133). His father gave him to the Lord as His servant, and from then on he stayed always with Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in Purī (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 10.61).

## Service

He took great care in bringing and serving prasāda to the Lord and the Vaiṣṇavas in Nīlācala (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 9.55). When his brother Gopīnātha was raised on the cāṅga, Vāṇīnātha too was arrested with the family; without fear he went on chanting the holy name, counting the names by marking lines on his body, and the Lord's mercy freed them all (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 9.55; *Abhidhāna*). He is named in the Vaiṣṇava-vandanās of Jīva (165), Devakīnandana (65) and Vṛndāvana dāsa (57).

He is not Vāṇīnātha Paṇḍita (Brahmacārī), the brother of Gadādhara Paṇḍita, nor Vāṇīnātha Vasu of Kulīna-grāma (*Abhidhāna*).
