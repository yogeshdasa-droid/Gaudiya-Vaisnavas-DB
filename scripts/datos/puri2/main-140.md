## In short

Bhavānanda Rāya (Paṭṭanāyaka) of Orissa was the father of **Rāmānanda Rāya** and a branch of the Caitanya tree: "Kāśī Miśra, Pradyumna Miśra, Rāya Bhavānanda" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.131). He gave his five sons to the service of the Lord. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā he was **King Pāṇḍu**, the father of the Pāṇḍavas, and his wife was Kuntī (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 122).

## Family

A devotee of Lord Jagannātha who lived in the village of **Bentapur**, in the district of Purī (near Ālālanātha). His five sons: **Rāmānanda Rāya** (the eldest), **Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka**, **Kalānidhi**, **Sudhānidhi** and **Vāṇīnātha** — "Rāmānanda Rāya, Paṭṭanāyaka Gopīnātha, Kalānidhi, Sudhānidhi, Nāyaka Vāṇīnātha" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.133). Śikhi Māhiti is said to have been the son of his brother.

## Meeting the Lord

One day he came with his sons to the house of Kāśī Miśra to see the Lord, and Sārvabhauma introduced each of them. The Lord embraced him, praised Rāmānanda, and said: "You are Pāṇḍu, and your five sons are the five Pāṇḍavas." Bhavānanda answered: "I am a low, worldly śūdra, and yet You have embraced me: You are the Lord Himself. From today all I have is Yours — my five sons, my servants, my house. Let my son Vāṇīnātha stay always with You and serve You" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 10.49–61).

Kavi Karṇapūra praises him (*Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* 8). In his *Guru-bhakti-gītā* Acyutānanda calls him a gopī. Verses 30 and 89 of the *Padyāvalī* may be his (*Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* 8.2; *Caitanya-carita-mahākāvya* 12.130; *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Madhya 10.46).

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**King Pāṇḍu** (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 122).
