## In short

Śrī Mādhavī Devī (Mādhavī Dāsī), a kāyastha lady of Orissa, was the youngest sister of **Śikhi Māhiti** and **Murāri Māhiti**, and a branch of the Caitanya tree. "Mādhavī Devī, the sister of Śikhi Māhiti, is counted among the maidservants of Śrī Rādhā" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Ādi 10.137). Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu counted her as the **"half"** of His three and a half intimate devotees. In Kṛṣṇa-līlā she was **Kalākeli**, a maidservant of Śrī Rādhā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 189).

## The half vessel

"An elderly lady, always in austerity, a great Vaiṣṇavī, whom the Lord counts among the associates of Rādhikā. In the world there are three and a half vessels: Svarūpa Gosāñi, Rāya Rāmānanda and Śikhi Māhiti — three — and his sister is the half" (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 2.104–106). Kavi Karṇapūra calls her a lady of great intelligence. With Murāri she accepted the Lord at once, and by their influence Śikhi too came to Him.

When **Choṭa Haridāsa** begged fine rice for the Lord's meal, it was from Mādhavī Devī that he got it — and for speaking with a woman the Lord gave him up (*Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, Antya 2).

She established the worship of the Deity of **Śrī Gopīnātha** in the village of **Bentapur**, near the house of Bhavānanda Rāya and near Ālālanātha; the Deity is still worshipped there.

## Writings

It is said that she wrote the Sanskrit drama *Puruṣottama-deva-nāṭaka* (*Abhidhāna*); some say King Pratāparudra appointed her to write the **Mādalā Pāñji** of the Jagannātha temple. Some Bengali songs signed «Mādhavī dāsa» in the *Padakalpataru* are thought by scholars to be hers (*Abhidhāna*). The record says she followed the «Sudhāmārgī» tradition, and that Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja's *Caitanya-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (manuscript in the Orissa State Museum, L 470 B) names her.

## In Kṛṣṇa-līlā

**Kalākeli**, a maidservant of Śrī Rādhā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 189).

## Another Mādhavī

Mādhavī, the wife of Raghu (Rāghava) Cakravartī and mother of Lakṣmīpriyā, the second wife of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya, is another person, with her own record (*Bhakti-ratnākara* 13.206; *Abhidhāna*).

## Sources

The record's texts, *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* Ādi 10.137, Antya 2.104–106, *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* 189, and the *Śrī Śrī Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava Abhidhāna* (Haridāsa Dāsa), entries «Mādhavī Devī», «Mādhavī dāsa» and «Śikhi Māhiti».
